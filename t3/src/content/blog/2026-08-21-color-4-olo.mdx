  ## The real world isn't single wavelength

  The sun, being a massive ball of plasma, among basically any other white light source, will be emitting photons across the entire visible spectrum, and not just one wavelength. Whatever reaches our eye at any given moment will rarely be a single wavelength of photons, meaning we really need to look at the *combined* effect of all the photons hitting our cones, and not just one wavelength at a time.
