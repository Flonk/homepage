<DeckCard vizProps={{ contents: ["water"] }}>
  ## What is milk?

  Mostly water: about 87.5 % of cow's milk.
</DeckCard>

<DeckCard vizProps={{ contents: ["water", "fat"] }}>
  ## Fat

  About 3.9 %, in tiny globules. Lighter than the water round them, they rise to the top, which is where cream comes from.
</DeckCard>

<DeckCard vizProps={{ contents: ["water", "fat", "lactose"] }}>
  ## Lactose

  About 4.8 %: milk sugar, dissolved in the water.
</DeckCard>

<DeckCard vizProps={{ contents: ["water", "fat", "lactose", "protein"] }}>
  ## Protein

  About 3.4 %, most of it casein, held in the water in tiny clusters.
</DeckCard>

<DeckCard vizProps={{ contents: ["water", "fat", "lactose", "protein", "minerals"] }}>
  ## Minerals

  About 0.8 %, calcium and phosphate above all. Together these are milk, mean values for cow's milk from the Tetra Pak Dairy Processing Handbook.
</DeckCard>
