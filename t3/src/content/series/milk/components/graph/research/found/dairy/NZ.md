## Fresh milk (pasteurised and raw)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh milk
- What it is: Whole and reduced-fat drinking milk sold in bottles and cartons, with fat-percentage varieties. Fonterra's Anchor is the main brand named.
- How it is made: Not described in the sources opened, beyond the Takanini (Auckland) plant that processes Anchor milk.
- Where / who: Dairy farms across NZ supply milk, paid per kilogram of milk solids; industrial processing. Raw milk output 21.1 billion litres in 2019–20. Before refrigeration (before the 1880s) milk was made for local consumption only; early farm herds were Shorthorn cows, usually one or two per family.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_farming_in_New_Zealand — production figure, 1840s–1880s farm practice and payment by milk solids; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchor_(brand) — Anchor fresh milk, Takanini plant, fat varieties; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fonterra — Anchor milk brand context

## Whole milk powder
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: dried milk powder
- What it is: Dried whole milk, NZ's largest dairy export by volume and value.
- How it is made: Made at processing factories; the sources opened do not describe the process. Drying methods are described generally in the Powdered milk article, but not for NZ production.
- Where / who: Industrial, New Zealand factories (for example Synlait's Dunsandel and Westland's Hokitika plants); exports 1,319,542 tonnes (NZ$5.8 billion) in the year to 30 June 2018.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_farming_in_New_Zealand — export figures and factory production; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synlait — whole milk powders, Dryer 2 and Dryer 3; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powdered_milk — NZ is one of the largest producers as of 2021

## Butter
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: butter
- What it is: Churned milk fat, sold as a table butter (Anchor, Westgold) and used as an ingredient.
- How it is made: Farm-made by hand churn before the 1880s, when refrigerated shipping arrived; from the 1880s small factories made it. The sources opened do not give temperatures or steps.
- Where / who: Farm, then factory (Ashburton Cheese and Butter Factory at Flemington was an early example). Dairy Industry Act 1892 set export standards. Westland's Westgold unsalted butter won a 2016 award. Butter, AMF and cream exported together at 454,660 tonnes (NZ$3.8 billion) in 2017–18.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_farming_in_New_Zealand — farm and factory butter history, 1892 Act, export figure; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westland_Milk_Products — Westgold consumer butter brand; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchor_(brand) — Anchor Butter sold in NZ
- Unsure: the Anchor article says the NZ Anchor butter brand is owned by a Goodman Fielder subsidiary and suggests Fonterra made the products and Goodman Fielder packages them; not confirmed.

## Whey and whey protein concentrate
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: milk by-product and protein concentrate
- What it is: Whey protein concentrate, sold as an ingredient, and whey used to make bioethanol.
- How it is made: Not described in the sources opened.
- Where / who: Industrial; Fonterra's bioethanol plants at Edgecumbe, Tirau and Reporoa have used whey since 2004. In 2013 China briefly suspended NZ milk powder imports after a false botulism scare on NZ whey protein concentrate.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fonterra — whey bioethanol, 2004; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powdered_milk — 2013 suspension over whey protein concentrate

## Infant formula (milk-based)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: infant and toddler formula
- What it is: Milk-based formula powders for infants and toddlers, including a2 Platinum, made under contract for The a2 Milk Company.
- How it is made: Spray-dried in dedicated infant-grade dryers (Synlait Dryer 2 and Dryer 3; Westland Dryer 7). Steps are not described in the sources opened.
- Where / who: Industrial; Synlait (since 2012 agreement) and Westland (infant manufacturing from 2012; Westpro Nutrition launched 2012); infant formula exports 91,914 tonnes (NZ$1.2 billion) in 2017–18.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synlait — Dryer 2 and 3, a2 Platinum; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westland_Milk_Products — infant nutrition manufacturing from 2012, Dryer 7 in 2013; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dairy_farming_in_New_Zealand — export figure

## Hokey pokey (honeycomb ice cream)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: frozen dessert
- What it is: Vanilla ice cream with small lumps of honeycomb toffee. In NZ "hokey pokey" is the local name for honeycomb toffee.
- How it is made: Vanilla ice cream with honeycomb toffee pieces added. No method or temperatures given in the source.
- Where / who: Industrial; the ice cream dates from the mid-20th century, and the NZ term from the late 19th century. Second most popular NZ ice cream flavour after vanilla; exported to Japan, Australia and the Pacific Islands.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hokey_pokey_(ice_cream) — description, history, popularity, exports; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_cuisine — lists hokey pokey ice cream as a food developed in NZ
- Unsure: the origin of the term is uncertain according to the source.
