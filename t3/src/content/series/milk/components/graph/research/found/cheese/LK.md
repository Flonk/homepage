## Processed cheese (Sri Lankan market brands)
- Animal(s): cow (stated for brands A and B; other brands not stated)
- Kind: processed cheese
- What it is: The most-sampled cheese type in a 2024 survey of cheeses on sale in Sri Lanka: 28 of 50 samples from seven brands. Local brands A, B, D, F and G, and imported brands C and E.
- How it is made: Not described in the source.
- Where / who: Sri Lankan retail market. Brands are anonymised in the paper. Brand D's milk comes from a single intensively managed farm in the hill country, per the paper.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11336408/fullTextXML — PMC11336408 (Heliyon 2024): 28 processed, 14 hard, 5 semi-hard and 3 soft samples from seven brands; local versus imported brands; cow milk for brands A and B; brand D single farm
- Unsure: The study tests aflatoxin M1 and is not a production survey; few samples per brand.
