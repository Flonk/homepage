## Crema catalana (crema andorrana served as a Catalan-style cream)
- Animal(s): cow milk
- Kind: milk dessert (cooked custard)
- What it is: A custard of milk, egg yolk and sugar, flavoured with lemon zest and cinnamon or vanilla, with a caramelised sugar top. Named in Andorra as a typical dessert by about 21.5% of respondents in a 2017 survey, far more than any other milk dish.
- How it is made: Milk, sugar, egg yolks, cinnamon and lemon zest, thickened with cornflour in modern versions; sugar caramelised on top. No temperatures or times given in the source.
- Where / who: Originates in Catalonia. In the 2017 survey it was named most often by respondents over 55 (25%), by other-nationality residents (36%) and by French respondents (31%); the survey does not say whether it is home or restaurant food.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crema_catalana — ingredients, names (crema cremada, crema de Sant Josep), Catalonia origin; https://sociologia.ad/files/361/Patrimoni-Gastronomic/1255/Patrimoni-Gastronomic-Segon-Semestre-Observatori-2017.pdf — share of respondents naming crema catalana as a typical Andorran dessert
- Unsure: The 2017 survey shows it is named as typical, but Wikipedia places its origin in Catalonia, so it is a shared Catalan dish rather than an Andorran invention.

## Crema a l'andorrana (Andorran-style cream)
- Animal(s): cow milk
- Kind: milk dessert (baked or bain-marie custard, caramel top)
- What it is: A milk custard with lemon and cinnamon, topped with sugar that is burnt with a hot iron. Presented on visitandorra.com as the Andorran version of crème brûlée.
- How it is made: 750 ml milk infused with lemon peel and cinnamon for 5 minutes; yolks beaten with 200 g sugar; starch and potato starch blended with cold milk; milk strained into the egg mix; cooked in a bain-marie for 10 minutes without boiling; poured into plates or pots, topped with sugar, and burnt with a hot iron. Serves 4.
- Where / who: Home and restaurant dish across Andorra.
- Sources: https://visitandorra.com/en/gastronomy/andorran-recipes/crema-a-landorrana-andorrana-style-cream/ — full recipe and method; https://visitandorra.com/en/gastronomy/andorran-recipes/ — listed as Andorran-style crème brûlée
- Unsure: Closely related to crema catalana; the recipe is the Andorran tourism board's version, not a documented traditional one.

## Arròs amb llet (rice pudding)
- Animal(s): cow milk
- Kind: milk dessert (rice pudding)
- What it is: Rice simmered slowly in sweetened milk with lemon peel, dusted with vanilla. Listed among Andorran desserts on the official recipe index; named by a small number of respondents in the 2017 survey.
- How it is made: 1 litre milk brought to the boil with a pinch of salt; add lemon peel, 150 g rice and 200 g sugar; simmer 20–25 minutes until thick; remove peel; dust with vanilla powder. Serves 4. No temperatures given.
- Where / who: Home dessert across Andorra.
- Sources: https://visitandorra.com/en/gastronomy/andorran-recipes/arros-amb-llet-rice-pudding/ — recipe; https://sociologia.ad/files/361/Patrimoni-Gastronomic/1255/Patrimoni-Gastronomic-Segon-Semestre-Observatori-2017.pdf — named as a typical dessert (0.5%)
- Unsure: Survey share is small; the recipe is the tourism board's version.

## Flam (flan, with or without cream)
- Animal(s): cow milk
- Kind: milk dessert (baked custard with caramel)
- What it is: A baked custard of milk, eggs and sugar, cooked in moulds in a bain-marie over caramel. Named in the 2017 survey as a typical dessert "with or without cream" (1.8% of respondents).
- How it is made: Caramel dissolved in a pan with a few drops of water, poured into moulds; 500 ml milk heated with lemon peel and vanilla or cinnamon; 2 yolks beaten with sugar, then mixed with 6 eggs and the milk; poured into the moulds, placed in a bain-marie at 85 °C for 45 minutes; cooled. Serves 4.
- Where / who: Home and restaurant dessert.
- Sources: https://visitandorra.com/en/gastronomy/andorran-recipes/flam-flan/ — recipe with 85 °C bain-marie for 45 minutes; https://sociologia.ad/files/361/Patrimoni-Gastronomic/1255/Patrimoni-Gastronomic-Segon-Semestre-Observatori-2017.pdf — flam with or without nata named by 1.8% of respondents
- Unsure: Standard flan, not a distinctly Andorran dish; the survey wording suggests cream is sometimes added.

## Torrades de Santa Teresa (sweet milk-soaked French toast)
- Animal(s): cow milk
- Kind: dish where milk is the main soaking liquid (fried bread)
- What it is: Stale bread soaked in milk flavoured with lemon peel, dipped in beaten egg, fried in butter, lard or oil, and sprinkled with sugar and optional cinnamon. Named as a typical Andorran sweet on the official recipe index.
- How it is made: Infuse lemon peel in 500 ml milk; soak 4 slices of stale bread until soft; drain; coat in 2 beaten eggs; fry in butter (or lard or oil) until golden; drain; sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon. No times or temperatures given.
- Where / who: Home dish, especially for feast days (Santa Teresa).
- Sources: https://visitandorra.com/en/gastronomy/andorran-recipes/torrades-de-santa-teresa-toast-soaked-in-sugar-milk-egg-and-cinnamon/ — recipe; https://visitandorra.com/en/gastronomy/andorran-recipes/ — recipe index entry
- Unsure: Milk is a soaking liquid rather than an ingredient of a dairy product as such; included because the recipe is built on milk. Feast-day link is from the recipe title only.

## Iogurt (yoghurt; mostly imported, with some made at Casa Raubert)
- Animal(s): not stated for yoghurt; Casa Raubert's cheeses are made from sheep and cow milk
- Kind: fermented milk
- What it is: Yoghurt sold in Andorra. Most is imported: the Tridge trade table gives 797,860 (volume) and USD 2,876,331 for 2023, and 702,077 and USD 2,114,915 for 2022, under HS code 040320. Casa Raubert also makes yoghurt "from only the creamiest milk".
- How it is made: Not stated on the pages opened (no culture, heat treatment or process given).
- Where / who: Imported; made locally at Casa Raubert, Escàs (La Massana).
- Sources: https://www.tridge.com/market-overview/yogurt/AD — Andorra yoghurt import volume and value for 2022 and 2023 (HS 040320); https://visitandorra.com/en/points-of-interest/casa-raubert/ — yoghurt made from the creamiest milk
- Unsure: Tridge is a trade-data aggregator and states "volume" without units; its page says the latest reference year is 2024, but the last figures shown are 2023. No Andorran yoghurt maker other than Casa Raubert is named.
