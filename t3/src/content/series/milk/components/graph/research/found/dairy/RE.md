## Péi milk (lait péi; local fresh cow milk)
- Animal(s): cow, probably; the source does not name the animal
- Kind: fresh milk (drinking milk)
- What it is: fresh milk produced on Réunion's Hauts farms and sold locally under the name "péi" (local).
- How it is made: not described.
- Where / who: Réunion; supplied by the Sicalait cooperative and Hauts farmers; industrial bottling. At the 2023 Miel Vert fair Sicalait sold it at two euros per litre.
- Sources: https://www.leaderreunion.fr/en/miel-vert-le-lait-pei-fait-recette/ — péi milk sold at Miel Vert, its producers and the products made from it; https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicalait — the cooperative collects local milk (24,000 litres in 1962; twenty million litres in 2011)
- Unsure: the animal is not named in the source; "cow" is an inference. The source also says local fresh milk covers only about 25% of island consumption (see Danone entry).

## Danone Réunion organic yoghurt ("Le Bio de Danone Réunion")
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: yoghurt (ultra-fresh, organic)
- What it is: organic plain and Bourbon-vanilla yoghurts, free of colourings and preservatives, with a soft texture; launched May 2021.
- How it is made: reconstituted organic milk, sourced only from French farms, not from local fresh milk, because local organic milk was not yet available. Certified by Ecocert; produced at the Port factory. Cultures not described.
- Where / who: Réunion (Port); Danone; industrial.
- Sources: https://www.leaderreunion.fr/en/danone-reunion-releve-le-defi-du-bio/ — milk source, organic status and the local-milk shortage
- Unsure: the product is made in Réunion but from imported milk, so it is included as an industrial product, not a local dairy product.
