## Lait caillé (curdled milk; French "lait caillé", local name not found)
- Animal(s): Cow's milk (Peul herds in northern Togo).
- Kind: Soured or curdled milk, eaten fresh.
- What it is: Curdled milk made in traditional Peul dairies and sold in the local market; the Tridge overview lists curdled milk as a product of the raw-milk trade.
- How it is made: Not described in any opened source.
- Where / who: Peul women, northern Togo (memoire part 9: "lait cru et lait caillé ... appréciés chez les peuls").
- Sources: https://www.memoireonline.com/09/22/13123/m_Etat-des-lieux-de-l-levage-des-bovins-laitiers-dans-la-rgion-maritime-au-Togo9.html — "lait cru et lait caillé: ils sont appréciés chez les peuls" (section 5); https://www.tridge.com/market-overview/cow-milk/TG — raw milk "processed into curdled milk and local cheeses (e.g., Peulh/Wagashi) and butter".
- Unsure: Method and local name are unconfirmed; the existence of the product in Togo is confirmed.

## Beurre traditionnel (traditional butter; French "beurre", Peul dairies)
- Animal(s): Cow's milk.
- Kind: Butter, home-made.
- What it is: Butter made by Peul women from the cream of the milk.
- How it is made: The milk is left to rest; the cream layer that rises is collected and treated to obtain butter (memoire part 9, section 5).
- Where / who: Peul dairies in Togo; Tridge lists butter among the products of raw-milk processing.
- Sources: https://www.memoireonline.com/09/22/13123/m_Etat-des-lieux-de-l-levage-des-bovins-laitiers-dans-la-rgion-maritime-au-Togo9.html — "Le lait de la traite est laissé au repos ... Cette couche, recueillie et traitée permet d'obtenir le beurre" (section 5); https://www.tridge.com/market-overview/cow-milk/TG — "processing into curdled milk and local cheeses (e.g., Peulh/Wagashi) and butter".
- Unsure: Details of churning are not given.

## Yaourt / degue (yoghurt eaten with millet couscous; French "yaourt", Togo name "degue" for the dish)
- Animal(s): Not stated.
- Kind: Yoghurt, eaten as an accompaniment.
- What it is: Yoghurt served with millet couscous, the northern dish called degue; the yoghurt is the accompanying dairy product.
- How it is made: Not described in any opened source.
- Where / who: Northern Togo (fr.wikipedia "Cuisine spécialisée du Nord-Togo", Amuse-Gueules).
- Sources: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuisine_togolaise — "Degue : « Couscous de mil consommé généralement avec du yaourt »".
- Unsure: Whether this yoghurt is home-made or industrial is not stated; the source does not name the animal.

## Lait reconstitué en poudre (reconstituted milk powder, Vitalait)
- Animal(s): Cow's milk, imported as powder.
- Kind: Reconstituted milk, sold for the sub-regional market.
- What it is: Milk powder reconstituted in Togo and sold by Vitalait.
- How it is made: Imported powder is mixed with water (memoire part 7, section 2.2: processors "reconstitute imported powdered milk").
- Where / who: Vitalait, Togo's industrial sector.
- Sources: https://www.memoireonline.com/09/22/13123/m_Etat-des-lieux-de-l-levage-des-bovins-laitiers-dans-la-rgion-maritime-au-Togo7.html — "Reconditioned milk powder, sold by Vitalait for the sub-regional market" (section 2.2).
- Unsure: None on the product; the source is a thesis.

## Lait cru (raw milk, sold informally)
- Animal(s): Cow's milk.
- Kind: Raw milk, sold untreated in some areas.
- What it is: Raw milk is commonly traded and consumed without treatment in some areas; it is often sold in plastic bottles in the south.
- How it is made: Not applicable; milked and sold raw.
- Where / who: Informal trade in Togo; northern Peul herds and southern markets.
- Sources: https://www.tridge.com/market-overview/cow-milk/TG — "commonly traded and consumed in some areas without treatment"; "packaged in plastic bottles for sale" in the south.
- Unsure: Raw cow's milk in southern Togo showed unsatisfactory hygienic quality (same page).
