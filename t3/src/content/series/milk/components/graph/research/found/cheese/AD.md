## Orri (Casa Raubert mature cheese)
- Animal(s): sheep and cow milk from the local area
- Kind: mature cheese
- What it is: One of the three mature cheeses from Casa Raubert, Escàs, named with Conlloc and Pletiu.
- How it is made: Not stated; the page gives no ripening time or method.
- Where / who: Casa Raubert, Escàs (La Massana), which the page calls Andorra's only artisanal cheese dairy.
- Sources: https://visitandorra.com/en/points-of-interest/casa-raubert/ — names Orri, Conlloc and Pletiu as mature cheeses; cheeses made "exclusively with milk from sheep and cows from the local area"
- Unsure: The local-products page calls its "shepherd's cheeses" sheep-only (https://visitandorra.com/en/gastronomy/local-products/); the dairy's own page says sheep and cow milk.
