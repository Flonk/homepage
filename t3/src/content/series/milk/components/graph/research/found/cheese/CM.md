## Unconfirmed leads
- Pendidam (Peulh/Fulani; Far North Cameroon) — a skimmed, fermented cow-milk product; its curdled stage is called nyalloundé and its churned stage wourwandé — opened paper PMC13332423, https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13332423/fullTextXML. Fermented milk, not a cheese, so not written up as an entry.
- Kindirmou (Adamawa, Cameroon) — a traditional fermented milk — named in PMC13332423 (citing a Journal of Food Quality study, not opened). Fermented milk, not a cheese.
- Wara / warankashi / wagashi (West African soft white cheese made by Fulani women) — present across West Africa in PMC12497476; no Cameroon source opened, so presence in Cameroon is unverified. Second pass: a Europe PMC search for wagashi with Cameroon returned only a Ghana study (PMC10837644); presence in Cameroon still unconfirmed.
- Artisanal yoghurt and traditionally fermented milks of Northern Cameroon — named in PMC13332423 as a reference title only (Scientific African), not opened.
- Saplait (Cameroon dairy, founded 1986 by Henri Tame Soumedjong) — makes yogurt, butter and cheese per https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Tame_Soumedjong; no cheese named, and no Saplait page found.
- Sotramilk Bamenda (Cameroon, 1994–2008) — processed fresh cow milk into yogurt and cheese per https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Tame_Soumedjong; no cheese named.
