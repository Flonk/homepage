## Keju parut (Malay: "keju parut", grated cheese; generic, type not named)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: grated cheese (type not stated)
- What it is: A generic Malay term for grated cheese. It is an ingredient of the sambal served with nasi katok, a Bruneian dish of steamed rice with fried chicken and sambal.
- How it is made: Not described in the sources read.
- Texture / ripening: Grated; not described further.
- Where / who: Brunei, nasi katok sambal (Bruneian dish). Farm or industrial, and origin, not stated.
- Sources: https://ms.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masakan_Brunei — opened; the sambal for nasi katok is made from chilli and other ingredients, including grated cheese (keju parut).
- Unsure: This is a generic term, not a named cheese, and it is a sambal ingredient rather than a cheese eaten on its own. The cheese may well be imported or processed. Kept as an entry because a page opened names cheese as a Bruneian food ingredient.
