# Solothurn (CH-SO), Switzerland — cheese

## Emmentaler (Emmentaler AOP; Emmental)
- Animal(s): Cow
- Kind: Hard pressed, raw-milk cheese with eyes (holes)
- What it is: Swiss hard cheese from the Emme valley; its AOP production area is wider than the valley and includes canton Solothurn.
- How it is made: Raw cow's milk from small rural dairies, with only water, salt, natural starter cultures and rennet added; not traditionally pasteurised. Starter bacteria are Streptococcus thermophilus, Lactobacillus helveticus and Propionibacterium freudenreichii. Formed into rounds with a natural rind and aged in traditional cellars for at least four months (2–18 months depending on variety). Eyes form when bacteria act on hay dust picked up during milking. The page gives no heating, cutting or pressing steps or temperatures.
- Texture / ripening: Ripened 4 months minimum; the page gives no texture beyond the eyes.
- Where / who: Cross-regional cheese. The legal production area is Aargau, Bern (except Amtsbezirk Moutier), Glarus, Luzern, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Zug, Zürich and the See- and Sensebezirk of Freiburg. Solothurn is inside that area, so it is included here; the source does not say which Solothurn dairies make it. Protected status: AOP.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmental_cheese — production area (AOP rules), milk, starter cultures, ripening; https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_von_K%C3%A4sesorten_aus_der_Schweiz — lists Emmentaler with Kantone Aargau, Bern, Glarus, Luzern, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Zug, Zürich und Freiburg; https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schweizer_Lebensmittel_mit_gesch%C3%BCtztem_Ursprung — same AOP area for Emmentaler.
- Unsure: The Solothurn inclusion is the legal production area, not a source naming a Solothurn dairy. A Solothurn-specific maker was not found.
