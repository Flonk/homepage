## Isle of Man Mature Cheddar (Manx cheddar; with flavoured variants)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: cheddar (mature); also sold as smoked and flavoured variants
- What it is: The Isle of Man Creamery's core cheese. The mature cheddar is the base for its blended and smoked varieties. Sold in UK supermarkets, and per Wikipedia also in some Canadian supermarkets.
- How it is made: Not stated on the sources read (only "matured", "cold smoked over oak chips" for the smoked version). The creamery site describes its history as "humble cheesemaking beginnings" leading to a factory processing over 26 million litres of milk a year.
- Texture / ripening: Mature; the vintage version is matured 15–18 months for a fine, close texture.
- Where / who: Isle of Man Creamery (Isle of Man); milk from local family farms in the co-operative, cows described as grass-fed. Industrial, not farm-made.
- Sources: https://www.isleofmancreamery.com/products/cheese — product list, cow's milk, descriptions; https://www.isleofmancreamery.com — company on the Isle of Man, over 100 years of history, factory milk volume, grass-fed cows; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Man — Manx cheese in UK supermarkets, smoked and herb-flavoured varieties, Canada export, 2005 bronze, 578 tonnes
- Unsure: The Wikipedia sources do not say which cheddar is meant, so they are read as referring to this creamery's range.
