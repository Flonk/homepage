## Kaas 'Van Weenink' (kaas van Weenink; Dutch, cow's milk)
- Animal(s): Cow (the farm's own cows; milk used daily for the cheese)
- Kind: not stated on the page
- What it is: The main cheese of Kaasboerderij Weenink, an artisanal farm cheese made on the Eimersweg in Lievelde, in the Achterhoek (Gelderland).
- How it is made: Not stated (no milk treatment, rennet, pressing, salting or ripening details on the page).
- Texture / ripening: Not stated.
- Where / who: Kaasboerderij Weenink, Eimersweg 3, Lievelde (Achterhoek). Farm-made, sold from the farm shop. Protected status: none stated.
- Sources: https://www.kaasboerderijweenink.nl/ — farm's own page: main cheese "Kaas 'Van Weenink'", cow's milk from the farm's cows, farm shop opening hours and address; https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lievelde — farm described as making cheese by traditional methods ("ambachtelijke wijze"), located opposite Erve Kots.
- Unsure: The farm page does not describe the cheese's kind or process; the farm's listed phone and email were not used to ask for details.

## Subenhara (Subenhara-kaas; Dutch, dessert cheese)
- Animal(s): Not stated
- Kind: Dessert cheese ("dessertkaas") named after the town
- What it is: A dessert cheese named after Zevenaar (Gelderland). Discontinued.
- How it is made: Not stated.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated.
- Where / who: Made at the local dairy factory Zuivelfabriek De Liemers in Zevenaar, from 1963 to 1992 (per the article). Historic, not made now.
- Sources: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zevenaar_(stad) — states the cheese was named after the town, made from 1963 to 1992 at Zuivelfabriek De Liemers.
- Unsure: Whether it counts as a farm/industrial cheese is not stated; its milk and method are not described on any page opened. The town also had an older name "Subenhara" (11th century), which is unrelated to the cheese. Kept as a defunct entry because it has a named Gelderland cheese.
