## Butter (Botter; Bauerebotter, Molkereisbotter, pasteurised, demi-sel, concentrated)
- Animal(s): cow (the name "Botter" by law applies only to cow's or sheep's milk products)
- Kind: butter
- What it is: Butter made from milk fat and water (water content max 16%). Types: farm butter (Bauerebotter), dairy butter (Molkereisbotter), pasteurised butter, lightly salted demi-sel (2–5% salt), and concentrated butter (up to 99% fat). Buttermilk is the by-product of churning.
- How it is made: Churning of milk fat is implied by the source; the source gives a yield of about 22 litres of milk per kilogram of butter. Storage ideally at about 5 °C; butter begins to melt at 31 °C.
- Where / who: Farm butter made by hand on farms; dairy butter made in dairies. Luxembourg has a "Marque Nationale" label, and government-set prices apply to local butter in two categories (Rose pasteurised Standard A and B).
- Sources: https://lb.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botter — butter types, buttermilk, Marque Nationale, yield and storage temperatures; https://lb.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mëllech — cow's milk named "Koumëllech" and butter as "Botter"
- Unsure: The Marque Nationale price details are only in outline; the source does not give the Standard A/B criteria.
