## Swiss cheese (queso suizo; imported, used in the sandwich cubano)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: pressed, semi-hard (Swiss-type); the sandwich article does not say which Swiss variety
- What it is: Imported Swiss-style cheese used as the melted layer in the Cuban sandwich (sandwich cubano), pressed with roast pork, ham, pickles and mustard.
- How it is made: not given in the source.
- Texture / ripening: the sandwich is pressed until the cheese has begun to melt.
- Where / who: eaten in Cuba as a sandwich filling; the source does not say whether it is made in Cuba or imported. Not a Cuban cheese type.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_sandwich — names Swiss cheese as the cheese in the sandwich.
- Unsure: this is a foreign cheese eaten in Cuba, not a named Cuban cheese; included only because it is eaten there. Whether it is made locally is unknown from the sources.

## Gouda (queso Gouda; imported, used in Cuban-style pizza and soup)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: semi-hard Dutch-type cheese
- What it is: Imported Dutch-style cheese that a Cuban-cuisine cooking page says was widely available in Cuba and was the cheese used on Cuban-style pizza; it is also the cheese generally named in a Cuban-style cream-of-cheese soup.
- How it is made: not given in the sources.
- Texture / ripening: grated for pizza (one cup, blended with mozzarella in the red-pizza recipe); grated or cut small for the soup.
- Where / who: Cuba (home cooking and pizza places, per the cooking page); Miami Cuban pizza shops blend it half and half with mozzarella.
- Sources: https://icuban.com/food/pizza.html — Gouda widely available in Cuba and used for Cuban-style pizza; https://www.univision.com/delicioso/sopa-crema-de-queso-al-estilo-cubano — "4 onzas de queso (generalmente se emplea la variedad Gouda)".
- Unsure: Not a Cuban-made cheese; the source does not say when Gouda was available in Cuba or whether any is made locally. Both sources are recipe or food-writing pages.
