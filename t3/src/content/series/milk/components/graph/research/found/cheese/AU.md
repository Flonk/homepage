- Kind: Pressed cooked/hard (cheddared)
- What it is: Cheddar is the main cheese of Australia, over 55% of the market per the Wikipedia cheddar article. Australia and New Zealand call it "tasty cheese".
- How it is made: The cheddar article describes the general process: curd separated with rennet, then cheddared (kneaded with salt, cut into cubes to drain whey, stacked and turned). No Australian-specific steps were found.

- Where / who: Mostly industrial. Joseph Harding's sons introduced cheddar production to Australia, according to the cheddar article.

## Milawa Goat Camembert (Milawa Cheese Co, Victoria)
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: Soft white (bloomy rind), goat camembert
- What it is: A goat camembert from Milawa Cheese Co, a family business founded in the 1980s by David and Anne Brown and made at the historic Milawa Butter Factory.
- How it is made: Goat's milk from the Gough family farm, about an hour's drive from Milawa. The curd is handled gently for a soft, creamy texture. Each small round is turned daily so the white bloomy rind forms evenly. The paste is firm when young and becomes soft and runny as it matures. Hand-ladling and moulding are not described.
- Texture / ripening: Firm when young, soft and runny when mature; rind turned daily.
- Where / who: Milawa Cheese Co, Milawa, Victoria. Handcrafted by the producer's own description.
- Sources: https://www.calendarcheese.com.au/product/cheese/white-mould/milawa-goat-camembert/ — goat milk source, gentle curd handling, daily turning, rind and texture, producer history and butter factory location

## Holy Goat fresh curd, La Luna and Nectar (Sutton Grange, Victoria)
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: Fresh curd; surface-ripened (geotrichum rind); washed rind
- What it is: Goat cheeses from Carla Meurs and Anne-Marie Monda's organic farm and purpose-built dairy at Sutton Grange, central Victoria. Three are named: a fresh curd, La Luna (a large ring-shaped cheese with a geotrichum "geo" rind), and Nectar (a newer washed-rind cheese matured under geo mould).
- How it is made: The milk comes from a small herd (about 80 goats milked at any one time; Saanen and British Alpine stock). It is batch-pasteurised, then cheese is made every day by traditional slow lactic-acid fermentation. The curds are ladled by hand into hoops and left to drain. La Luna matures for at least three weeks; Nectar matures six to eight weeks.
- Texture / ripening: La Luna and Nectar are surface-ripened under geo mould; Nectar is semi-soft and sticky with a hint of smoke.
- Where / who: Holy Goat, Sutton Grange, Victoria. Organic, small herd, farmstead.
- Sources: https://www.gourmettraveller.com.au/dining-out/food-news/holy-goat-goats-milk-cheese-2561/ — producers, location, herd, batch pasteurisation, hand ladling into hoops, the three cheeses and their ripening
- Unsure: The article does not mention ash-coated cheeses. The fresh curd is not given a separate name.

## Emme (Hindmarsh Valley Dairy, South Australia)
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: Swiss-style hard cheese, raw milk
- What it is: A raw-milk goat's cheese made in the style of Emmentaler by Denise Riches at Hindmarsh Valley Dairy, near Victor Harbour on the Fleurieu Peninsula.
- How it is made: Goat's milk from Riches' own 80-hectare farm. It is handmade, turned and washed often during ageing, aged for six months, with a natural rind, and has begun to develop the holes that give Emmentaler its character. Non-animal rennet is used.
- Where / who: Hindmarsh Valley Dairy, South Australia. Riches is one of two Australian makers licensed for raw-milk cheese (the other is Bruny Island Cheese Company, Tasmania), the first such licences since the early 1960s.
- Sources: https://www.gourmettraveller.com.au/dining-out/food-news/the-producers-emme-raw-milk-goats-cheese-2544/ — maker, farm milk, Emmentaler style, handmade and washed ageing, six months, raw-milk licence
- Unsure: The article does not give the pressing method; "hard" is inferred from the Emmentaler style.
