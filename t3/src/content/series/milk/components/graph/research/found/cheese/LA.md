## Buffalo mozzarella (Laos Buffalo Dairy)
- Animal(s): water buffalo
- Kind: fresh pasta filata cheese
- What it is: mozzarella made from water buffalo milk in Luang Prabang, one of the cheeses the dairy makes for locals and visitors.
- How it is made: milk is pasteurised and homogenised on arrival from village herds (discoverlaos.today); the stretching and brining are not described in either source.
- Where / who: Luang Prabang; Laos Buffalo Dairy, founded by Susie Martin, Steven McWhirter, Chef Rachel O'Shea and Matthew O'Shea.
- Sources: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/luang-prabang-water-buffalo-cheese-food — "Chef O'Shea is able to make delicious blue cheese, feta, brie, Camembert, and mozzarella for locals and visitors in Laos."; https://discoverlaos.today/luangprabang/thing-to-do/laos-buffalo-dairy-one-and-only — names mozzarella among the products of the dairy.
