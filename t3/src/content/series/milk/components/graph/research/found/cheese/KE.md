- Animal(s): cow; goat in one producer's photo caption (Brown's)
- Kind: hard, pressed (industrial)
- What it is: a hard pressed cheese, sold in Kenya as an industrial or artisanal product.
- How it is made: not described in the sources.
- Texture / ripening: not described.
- Where / who: imported style, made by Brown's (Tigoni), Raka (Nyeri), Happy Cow (Nakuru), Eldoville (Karen), New Kenya Co-operative Creameries (Eldoret, Kitale, Mombasa, Nyahururu), Sirimon (Nanyuki), Doinyo Lessos (Eldoret). Not a Kenyan cheese by origin.
