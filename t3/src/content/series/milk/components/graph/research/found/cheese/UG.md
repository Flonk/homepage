## Ripened cheeses of Uganda (varieties not named)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: ripened cheese
- What it is: Ripened Ugandan cheese varieties, studied as a reservoir of lactic acid bacteria and yeasts for possible starter cultures.
- How it is made: Not described in the abstract.
- Texture / ripening: ripened; the microbiota show lactic acid bacteria (Pediococcus pentosaceus, Lactiplantibacillus plantarum, Pediococcus acidilactici) and yeast (Yarrowia lipolytica) plus staphylococci.
- Where / who: Uganda; cheese varieties not named in the record.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:PPR1133930&format=json&resultType=core — Europe PMC record of the preprint (PPR1133930, DOI 10.20944/preprints202512.1194.v1), abstract read: isolates from selected ripened Ugandan cheese varieties; P. pentosaceus inhibited S. aureus; multidrug resistance in P. acidilactici.
- Unsure: Only the abstract was readable (full text on preprints.org returned 403). The varieties are not named, so this is recorded as a category.
