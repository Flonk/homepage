## Triangle cheese (processed cheese; local name not given)
- Animal(s): not stated (the source does not say which milk it is made from)
- Kind: processed cheese
- What it is: a processed cheese, called "triangle cheese" in the source, described as the only cheese found in the Saharawi refugee camps. It was 30.5% of recorded dairy occasions among the surveyed women.
- How it is made: not given.
- Texture / ripening: not given.
- Where / who: eaten by Saharawi refugees in the camps near Rabouni and Tindouf, Algeria, not in the territory of Western Sahara. The source does not say it is made in the camps or the territory; it is probably industrial and imported, but this is not stated.
- Sources: https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12020568 (Europe PMC full text: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7071476/fullTextXML) — Nutrients 2020, "Dietary Habits of Saharawi Type II Diabetic Women Living in Algerian Refugee Camps"; states that processed triangle cheese is the only cheese found in the camps and gives its share of dairy consumption.
- Unsure: the camps are in Algeria, not Western Sahara. Whether this cheese belongs to Western Sahara at all is doubtful. Included only because the Sahrawi people are the main population of the territory. The cheese is not identified as made by anyone.
