## Φέτα (Feta; PDO; mainland Greece)
- Animal(s): sheep, or sheep with up to about 30% goat milk from the same area
- Kind: brined
- What it is: Greece's best-known brined cheese, protected by a PDO that covers mainland Greece and Lesbos.
- How it is made: pasteurise the milk (most producers do) and add a starter; at about 35 °C add rennet and let the curd form; chop and drain in moulds or cloth; cut and dry-salt, rest several days; age in about 7% brine for several weeks at room temperature, then at least two more months refrigerated at high humidity.
- Texture / ripening: crumbly, in brine; at least two months of cold ageing after the brine step.
- Where / who: industrial and farm production; PDO. The Greek Wikipedia list gives the regions as Macedonia, Thrace, Epirus, Thessaly, Central Greece, the Peloponnese and Lesbos; Attikí is not named there.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feta — PDO area ("mainland Greece and Lesbos Prefecture"), milk and production steps; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Κατάλογος_ελληνικών_τυριών — Φέτα region list without Attica.
- Unsure: whether Attikí is inside the PDO area. "Mainland Greece" would include it, but no source opened names it. Confirm against the EU PDO specification before listing as made in Attikí.
