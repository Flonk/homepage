## Whipped cream (served on pearl cassava or tapioca)
- Animal(s): cow (presumed; not stated)
- Kind: cream (topping)
- What it is: Whipped cream served as a topping on a dessert of pearl cassava (tapioca), sometimes with caramel.
- How it is made: Not described in the source.
- Where / who: Served with a pearl-cassava dessert in Solomon Islands cuisine; whether made locally or imported (e.g. tinned or UHT cream) is not stated.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_Islands_cuisine — notes pearl cassava "sometimes wrapped in pearl cassava and served with whipped cream or caramel"
- Unsure: The source does not say the cream is local, dairy-based or made in the country; it may be imported. Only one dish-level mention, no preparation detail.

## Anchor liquid milk (English; brand name)
- Animal(s): cow (presumed; not stated in the source)
- Kind: liquid milk, packaged, shelf-stable (imported brand)
- What it is: Packaged liquid milk sold in shops and markets, tracked as a food price during COVID-19.
- How it is made: Not described in the source.
- Where / who: Honiara, Auki and Gizo; mean prices in SBD for 1 l, 2020 then 2021: Honiara 10.3 then 19.5; Auki 13.8 then 20; Gizo 15 then 17.2.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10293874/fullTextXML — survey Table 1 (milk, anchor liquid, 1 l prices) and text noting milk prices rose at surveyed vendors since the pandemic began
- Unsure: Origin not stated. The table says 1 l but the appendix lists 1.5 l. The 2020 Gizo price rests on a single vendor.
