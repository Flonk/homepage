## Åland pancake (Ålandspannkaka; Swedish: Ålandspannkaka; English: Åland pancake)
- Animal(s): cow (whole milk, implied by recipe)
- Kind: milk-based dessert / baked semolina pancake
- What it is: An oven-baked semolina pancake flavoured with cardamom, eaten warm or cold with prune jam and whipped cream. Popular across the islands, especially with cycling tourists.
- How it is made: Semolina (or pudding rice) is heated in about 1 litre of whole milk for a few minutes until porridge-like, then cooled. It is mixed with flour, eggs, sugar, salt and cardamom, poured into a dish greased with butter, dotted with butter, and baked at 200°C for 50–60 minutes until golden.
- Where / who: Åland-wide; recipe by Fran Weaver (2012), with advice from Pettas Organic Home Bakery.
- Sources: https://finland.fi/?p=2586 — recipe (milk quantity, temperature, time, butter); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%85land_pancake — history, semolina and cardamom, served with prune jam and whipped cream.
- Unsure: The English Wikipedia article says the standard batter has no milk or butter, which contradicts the finland.fi recipe. The recipe is a single modern source, so the milk-based method should be read as one tradition, not a documented historic one.

## Whipped cream (grädde) on the Åland pancake
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: cream (served as topping)
- What it is: Real dairy cream whipped and served on the Åland pancake, hot or cold, usually with prune jam (raspberry for children). Locally the cream-and-jam topping is called snömos ("snow mash").
- How it is made: Whipped; no method or temperature given in the sources.
- Where / who: Åland-wide, served with the pancake.
- Sources: https://aland.travel/food — "real dairy cream" on the pancake, with plum or raspberry jam; https://visitaland.com/?p=16057 — whipped cream on the pancake; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%85land_pancake — "typically accompanied by prune jam and whipped cream"; "snömos" name.
- Unsure: Only described as a topping, not as a standalone product.

## ÅCA milk (Ålandsmejeriet ÅCA, including organic milk)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: heat-treated / pasteurised milk (drinking milk), organic line
- What it is: The milk sold by ÅCA (Ålandsmejeriet), the islands' cooperative dairy, in all supermarkets. Most restaurants use ÅCA as their main dairy supplier. The range includes organic milk.
- How it is made: No details on processing given in the sources read.
- Where / who: Industrial; Åland-wide; ÅCA is the dairy factory.
- Sources: https://aland.travel/food — ÅCA milk, butter, yoghurt, sold in all supermarkets and used by most restaurants; https://www.aland.com/en/artikel/wide-variety-of-small-food-producers — organic milk "in its range"; https://aland.com/en/artikel/be-an-axgan-enjoy-alands-locally-produced-food — organic milk from ÅCA.
- Unsure: The sources do not give the processing method, and do not say whether the organic line is a separate product.

## ÅCA butter (Åland butter)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: butter
- What it is: Butter made by the ÅCA dairy, sold in supermarkets, used by restaurants, and exported to mainland Finland, where it is described as creamy. It is also spread on black bread, which the aland.travel page recommends with cheese.
- How it is made: No method given by the sources read. A planned hand-churned or artisan butter with chef Michael Björklund and ÅCA at the Smakby centre beside Kastelholm Castle is described as a plan, not a finished product.
- Where / who: Industrial (ÅCA); the Smakby butter is a planned artisan line.
- Sources: https://aland.travel/food — ÅCA butter; butter on black bread; https://visitaland.com/?p=16057 — butter from ÅCA; https://finland.fi/?p=2586 — creamy butter exported to mainland Finland; Smakby plan with ÅCA.
- Unsure: Whether the Smakby butter has started production is not stated. Whether the export butter is the same as the supermarket butter is not stated.

## Yoghurt (Mattas gårdsmejeri)
- Animal(s): cow (five cows on the farm)
- Kind: fermented milk (yoghurt)
- What it is: Organic yoghurt made by the Mattas family farm dairy in Sund, northern Åland, without additives or preservatives. It won gold at the Swedish craft food championships.
- How it is made: From organic milk from the farm's own five cows. The milk is not homogenised and is never heated, so it keeps its natural traits; some products are unpasteurised. No culture or temperature given.
- Where / who: Small farm dairy, Sund, northern Åland.
- Sources: https://aland.travel/food — Mattas yoghurt, organic, milk not homogenised and never heated; https://visitaland.com/?p=16057 — Mattas yoghurt, gold at Swedish championships; https://visitaland.com/en/experience/culinary-aland — no yoghurt (not used).
- Unsure: "Never heated" and "unpasteurised" are the farm's claim from its own page as reported in the summary; treat as the producer's description. Second pass: mattas.ax says the dairy's milk comes from two nearby farms, which conflicts with the "own five cows" note above; the page does not name yoghurt at all, so the source for this entry is unchanged.

## Sour cream (Mattas gårdsmejeri; sour cream with potatoes)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: soured cream (cream)
- What it is: Sour cream from the Mattas farm dairy, which took silver at the Finnish craft food championships. Also served as a topping on waffle-pan fried Åland potatoes (with whitefish roe) at a food truck, Våffelrakan.
- How it is made: No method given by the sources read.
- Where / who: Small farm dairy, Sund, northern Åland; the food truck uses an unnamed sour cream.
- Sources: https://visitaland.com/?p=16057 — Mattas sour cream, silver at Finnish championships; https://www.aland.com/en/artikel/delicious-and-inspiring-artisanal-food — Våffelrakan serves sour cream with Åland potatoes and whitefish roe.
- Unsure: The food truck's sour cream may not be Mattas's.

- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: ice cream / frozen milk dessert
- What it is: Ice cream made on the Mattas farm and sold at its summer café in Sund.
- How it is made: From the farm's own milk; no method given.
- Where / who: Small farm dairy, Sund, northern Åland.
- Sources: https://aland.travel/food — ice cream made on the farm and sold at the summer café.
