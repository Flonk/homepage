## Fresh milk (breakfast and morning drinks; Mennonite milk)
- Animal(s): cow (presumed; the sources do not name the animal)
- Kind: fresh/raw or pasteurised milk, drunk plain or with cereal, and taken in morning drinks
- What it is: plain milk served at breakfast, with cereal, and as a base for morning drinks such as Milo, Ovaltine and cocoa
- How it is made: not described in the sources
- Where / who: eaten across Belize at breakfast. The Mennonite communities of Belize are described as producing milk, cheese, beans, corn and other farm goods
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Belize — breakfast milk and the morning drinks list; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mennonites_in_Belize — Mennonites produce milk and cheese; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronom%C3%ADa_de_Belice — breakfast includes cereal with leche (milk), coffee or tea
- Unsure: whether the milk is industrial or farm-fresh and whether it is pasteurised is not stated. The Milo, Ovaltine and cocoa drinks are often made with milk, but the source does not say so.
