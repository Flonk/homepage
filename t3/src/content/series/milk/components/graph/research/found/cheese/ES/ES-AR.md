## Tronchón (queso de Tronchón; also "queso Tronchón"; Valencian heading in the Wikipedia list)
- Animal(s): sheep, or goat, or both ("leche cruda o pasteurizada de oveja, cabra o ambas en función de la disponibilidad del ganado")
- Kind: cured, intensely pressed hard cheese
- What it is: a cured cheese from the village of Tronchón (Teruel) in the Maestrazgo, cited by Cervantes in Don Quixote and already famous across Spain by 1615. The guide calls it the most emblematic Aragonese cheese.
- How it is made: enzymatic coagulation with plant coagulant (cardoon, Cynara cardunculus) or animal rennet; intense pressing; ripening/air-drying; cutting and salting not given. Ripening time not given.
- Texture / ripening: flavour depends on how air-dried the cheese is.
- Where / who: Tronchón and the Maestrazgo (Teruel), spread to Castellón by transhumance. Makers in the guide: Queseros Artesanos de Tronchón (curado goat and sheep, 2-month sheep, requesón), Quesos Sardón (Las Abejambras sheep), La Val (cured and semicured). Protected by a quality mark in the Valencian Community since 23 Dec 2008 (per es.wikipedia).
- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queso_de_Tronchón — milk, coagulant, pressing, history, quality mark; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Spanish_cheeses — listed under Aragon; https://academiaaragonesadegastronomia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LIBRO-GUIA-DE-LOS-QUESOS-DE-ARAGON_DIGITALpdf.pdf — p.21 (emblematic, Cervantes), p.30 (Sardón), p.54 (Queseros Artesanos de Tronchón), p.55 (La Val)
- Unsure: the English list also puts "Queso Tronchón" under the Valencian Community, which conflicts with the Aragonese entry. Ripening time not found.

## Queso de Teruel
- Animal(s): sheep (raw milk, aged); goat (cured)
- Kind: cured / aged pressed cheese, several ages
- What it is: a cheese name for sheep and goat cheeses made by several dairies in the province of Teruel.
- How it is made: raw sheep's milk at Granja Perales, aged 3 months (semicured), 6 months (cured), 9 months (old), 12 months (añejo). Other makers use traditional curdling with animal rennet and pressing (Sardón, cow, goat and sheep milk).
- Texture / ripening: ripening stages above.
- Where / who: province of Teruel; Granja Perales (Perales del Alfambra), Quesos Sardón (Samper de Calanda), Queseros Artesanos de Tronchón.

## Queso del Benasqués (El Benasqués)
- Animal(s): cow, raw milk
- Kind: cured, pressed, uncooked hard cheese with a natural mouldy rind
- What it is: a cured cow's-milk cheese from Sahún in the Benasque valley, made by Quesos El Benasqués.
- How it is made: raw cow's milk, cured for 3 months; pressed, uncooked paste; natural mould rind. Coagulant, cutting and salt not given.
- Texture / ripening: 3 months curing; hard paste; natural mould rind.
- Where / who: Quesos El Benasqués, Benasque Baja, Sahún (Huesca), a family dairy. Prize-winning: bronze medal at the 1986 Barcelona cheese tasting and first prize at the Biescas autumn fair in 2007.
- Sources: https://academiaaragonesadegastronomia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LIBRO-GUIA-DE-LOS-QUESOS-DE-ARAGON_DIGITALpdf.pdf — p.25 (raw cow milk, 3 months, pasta dura prensada no cocida, corteza natural enmohecida)

## Queso de Radiquero (Quesos de Radiquero cheeses)
- Animal(s): goat, raw or pasteurised
- Kind: pressed and soft-paste goat cheeses; cream-style cheeses
- What it is: a family of artisan goat cheeses from Radiquero (Adahuesca, Huesca), founded in the late 1970s, known for creamy cheeses and award results.
- How it is made: several are raw goat's milk with animal-origin rennet ("cuajos de origen animal"), pressed: San Pelegrín, San Pelegrín al vino, San Pelegrín al tomillo. Sierra de Sevil is pasteurised goat's milk, pressed. Río Vero and Los Meleses are pasteurised goat's milk, soft-paste (Los Meleses uses animal rennet).
- Texture / ripening: described as creamy; no ripening times given.
- Where / who: Quesos de Radiquero (Adahuesca, Huesca), maestra quesera Conchi Mata; awarded at World Cheese Awards and by the Ministry.
- Sources: https://academiaaragonesadegastronomia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LIBRO-GUIA-DE-LOS-QUESOS-DE-ARAGON_DIGITALpdf.pdf — p.21 (creamy Radiquero), p.44 (product list), p.45 (history, awards); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Spanish_cheeses — "Radiquero" listed under Aragon

## Azul de Búnker (blue sheep cheese, Villanúa)
- Animal(s): sheep, raw milk
- Kind: blue (Penicillium roqueforti), unpressed paste
- What it is: a large blue sheep cheese from Villanúa (Huesca), named after a Civil War bunker at El Carrascal where it was once ripened; 8 to 10 kg pieces, sold portioned.
- How it is made: raw sheep's milk, Penicillium roqueforti; unpressed paste with mechanical holes; the paste is perforated during ripening so the holes connect and the mould spreads; sold at 2 to 4 months, before it becomes sharp.
- Texture / ripening: creamy and melting; spicy to mild with a slight sweetness; 2 to 4 months.
- Where / who: O Xortical (Villanúa, Huesca). Won a World Cheese Awards 2024 distinction, described as the most important award Aragón received at that competition; Cofradía del Queso de Cantabria blue cheese contest 2023 (guide).
- Sources: https://elespanol.com/aragon/vivir/20250324/pueblo-aragon-elabora-mejores-quesos-azules-intenso-sabor-textura-cremosa-trt/933656665_0.html — milk, mould, perforation, ripening, award; https://academiaaragonesadegastronomia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LIBRO-GUIA-DE-LOS-QUESOS-DE-ARAGON_DIGITALpdf.pdf — p.22 (queso azul de oveja, 2023 contest)

## Sierra de Albarracín (pure sheep cheeses)
- Animal(s): sheep (own flock), pasteurised or raw milk
- Kind: fresh, tender, semicured, cured, aged, rosemary, wine-rubbed, pressed and soft-paste; cream cheese
- What it is: a range of pure sheep cheeses from Albarracín (Teruel); named one of the most awarded dairies in Aragón.
- How it is made: pasteurised sheep's milk for the fresh, tender and some semicured types; raw milk for Oro (semicured) and Negra (cured); ripened in La Cava de Mía, a limestone cave-style room with juniper shelves.
- Texture / ripening: various ages; cave-ripened.
- Where / who: Quesos Sierra de Albarracín (Queso Artesano de Teruel SL), Albarracín; more than 50 World Cheese Awards medals.
- Sources: https://academiaaragonesadegastronomia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/LIBRO-GUIA-DE-LOS-QUESOS-DE-ARAGON_DIGITALpdf.pdf — p.52, p.53
