## Halloumi (Halloumi; Cypriot cheese, sold under the Baladna brand in Qatar)
- Animal(s): Goat and/or sheep milk traditionally; commercial versions often blend in cow's milk
- Kind: brined, semi-hard, grilling cheese
- What it is: A brined cheese originating in Cyprus, sold in Qatar under the Baladna brand as "Halloumi Cheese" and "Halloumi Cheese Low Fat".
- How it is made: Warm milk (30–34 °C) curdled with rennet; curds cut, reheated, pressed; simmered in hot whey (scalding); salted, garnished with mint, rested in salted whey for 1–3 days; mature halloumi kept in brine at least 40 days.
- Texture / ripening: Brined; mature version ages at least 40 days in whey.
- Where / who: Originates in Cyprus (EU and US rules reserve the name to Cyprus). In Qatar, produced or packed by Baladna; whether it is made locally or imported is not stated.
- Sources: https://www.baladna.com/en/brand/products/category/cheese — Baladna-brand "Halloumi Cheese" and "Halloumi Cheese Low Fat" in Qatar; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloumi — method and Cyprus origin.
- Unsure: Whether Baladna's halloumi is made in Qatar or imported is not stated.
