## Queso Colonia (Queso Colonia; Colonia)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: semi-hard, cooked, pressed cheese with a hard rind and large eyes
- What it is: The best-known Uruguayan cheese, named after the Colonia department. It was introduced in 1868 by Juan Teófilo Karlen, a Swiss settler, at Nueva Helvecia, and descends from Emmental but is softer and milder.
- How it is made: The sources opened do not describe the steps (heating, coagulant, cutting, pressing, brining). They say the eyes form through lactic fermentation over about 12 months of ripening.
- Texture / ripening: Pale yellow, elastic and smooth, with many evenly spaced eyes of 2–30 mm (the figures vary by source). Ripened 4 to 12 months. Mild and slightly sweet, with a flat cylindrical shape. Moisture is high and fat is medium.
- Where / who: Nueva Helvecia and the southwestern dairy basin; Swiss immigrant origin. No protected status found in the sources.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonia_cheese — origin, Karlen, 1868, and the softer-than-Emmental description; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queso_Colonia — milk, moisture and fat, eyes, ripening and texture; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Uruguay — listed as dairy output and in the picada; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cheeses — Uruguay entry
- Unsure: The sources disagree on texture: semi-hard in the Spanish article and the English article's own text; "soft cheese with a hard rind" in the English list. The fat figures also vary (32–60% in one, 40–59.9% in another). The English article calls it "semi-soft" in one place.

## Queso Yamandú (Yamandú; Zapicán's parent cheese)
- Animal(s): cow (the English list says unpasteurized milk, animal not stated)
- Kind: firm, semi-hard, Gouda-type cheese, washed-rind family, semi-cooked curd
- What it is: A Uruguayan cheese from the 1950s, developed by the dairy school of the Universidad del Trabajo in Nueva Helvecia. It is credited to Luis Bertotto and Rubén Schneider. Some experts call it the only cheese of wholly Uruguayan origin. It was served with quince paste in the Martín Fierro dessert.
- How it is made: The steps are not detailed in the sources. It is ripened 30 to 45 days.
- Texture / ripening: Gentle, slightly tangy, buttery, pale yellow, springy. It sometimes develops a small shiny eye. Today a flattened cylinder about 21 cm across, 8 cm tall, 4–5 kg, with a smooth yellowish rind. The original was flying-saucer shaped.
- Where / who: Nueva Helvecia, Colonia. No protected status found.
- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queso_Yamandú — origin, shape, ripening, revival in 2022; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Uruguay — firm semi-hard cow's-milk cheese, Martín Fierro; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cheeses — Uruguay entry, unpasteurized milk
- Unsure: The English list says production ceased in 1980, but the Spanish article says production declined and the school and some artisan dairies resumed it in 2022. I took the 2022 revival from the Spanish article. The English list also gives unpasteurized milk, which the Spanish article does not state.

## Mozzarella (Uruguay)
- Animal(s): cow (standard)
- Kind: stretched-curd (pasta filata), fresh
- What it is: Mozzarella is widely used in Uruguay, in the chivito, figazza, muzzarella pizza, panchos porteños, and milanesa a la napolitana. The Spanish article lists it among the dairy industry's output.
- How it is made: No Uruguayan method found. The generic method (en.wikipedia Mozzarella): curd cut at 1–1.5 cm, heated and drained, pH about 5.2–5.5, stretched by hand, brined.
- Texture / ripening: Fresh.
- Where / who: Nationwide, industrial and dairy-made.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uruguayan_cuisine — uses in dishes; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Uruguay — dairy output; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mozzarella — generic method; https://www.conaprole.uy/categoria-producto/quesos/ — "Queso Mozzarella" (soft, medium-to-high moisture, semi-fat to fatty) and "Queso Mozzarella Light"
- Unsure: The Uruguayan sources name the cheese without its production details.
