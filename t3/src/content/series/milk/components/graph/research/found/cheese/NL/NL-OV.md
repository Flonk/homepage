## Boer'n Trots (Boer'n Trots; cheese trademark, Q27978495)
- Animal(s): cow; goat (Honing Geitenkaas and some other varieties)
- Kind: washed-rind cheese, cylindrical; rind washed with caramel or honey, with flavoured varieties (cheese-style not classified in source)
- What it is: A Dutch cheese brand made at Landgoed Kaamps, an estate in Deurningen, Twente, with cow's milk from its own herd and goat's milk for some varieties. Made since 1985 according to the Wikipedia article; distributed by Vandersterre Groep.
- How it is made: Rinds washed with caramel or honey; flavour varieties include herbs, spices and seeds. Raw or pasteurised milk, coagulant, and ripening time not stated in the sources.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated. Honey-washed and caramel-washed styles, the source describes the flavour as fairly sweet.
- Where / who: Landgoed Kaamps, Deurningen (municipality Dinkelland), Twente, Overijssel. Estate's own dairy herd (about 280 cows per its site). Sold across the Netherlands and in parts of Belgium. Goat-milk Honing won gold at World Cheese Awards 2015 and International Cheese Awards 2016; cow-milk Honing won bronze at World Cheese Awards 2015.
- Sources: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boer'n_Trots — cheese line, milk types, washing, varieties, awards, availability; https://www.kaamps.nl/ — estate's own dairy herd and cheese making in Deurningen, Twente.
- Unsure: The Wikipedia article is the source for the brand's history and awards; the estate site confirms the location and herd but names no cheese varieties. The article does not say whether the cheese is made on the estate or in a dairy elsewhere; the Wikipedia text says "made at Landgoed Kaamps", which is followed here.
