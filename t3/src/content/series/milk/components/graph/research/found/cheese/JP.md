## Sakura (Japanese name not in sources; Sakura cheese)
- Animal(s): cow (Holstein or Brown Swiss; the Wikipedia article's category tags list it under cow's-milk cheeses)
- Kind: soft, surface-ripened, camembert-style
- What it is: A soft cheese made at Kyodo Gakusha Shintoku Farm in Tokachi, Hokkaido, aimed as a Japanese-style cheese rather than an imitation of a European one.
- How it is made: Rind washed in local sake (Yachi Yanagi dusting, Sasayuki treatment) and wrapped in bamboo leaf from the farm, according to the Wikipedia article. Culture-magazine pieces instead describe it as shiso-wrapped with a pickled cherry blossom. Milk heating, coagulant and ripening times are not given.
- Texture / ripening: Soft and creamy, camembert-style.
- Where / who: Kyodo Gakusha Shintoku Farm, Hokkaido (founded 1974 per Wikipedia; 1978 per Fortune). Gold at the Mountain Cheese Olympics in Appenzell, Switzerland, for a soft cheese; 2004 and 2007 cited in the 2014 culture piece.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakura_cheese — camembert-style, sake wash, bamboo leaf, farm and gold medal; https://culturecheesemag.com/blog/world-cheese-culture-japan/ — cherry-leaf scent and 2004/2007 golds; https://culturecheesemag.com/travel/5-japanese-cheesemakers-you-need-to-know/ — shiso/cherry-blossom description and Kyodo Gakusha lineup; https://dc.fortune.com/longform/japanese-cheese-artisan-tokachi-raclette-collective (redirects to https://fortune.com/longform/japanese-cheese-artisan-tokachi-raclette-collective/) — soft cheese made with cherry blossoms, Tokachi.
- Unsure: Leaf and wash details disagree between sources (bamboo leaf vs cherry leaf vs shiso; sake wash attributed to Sakura in Wikipedia and to a separate Sakagura in the culture piece). Founding year differs (1974 vs 1978).

## Sakeru Cheese (さけるチーズ, Sakeru)
- Animal(s): cow (100% Hokkaido raw milk, per the Megmilk Snow Brand version)
- Kind: stretched, string/pull-apart cheese (natural, unaged)
- What it is: A pull-apart string cheese by Megmilk Snow Brand, first sold in 1980 as "string cheese" and renamed in 1995.
- How it is made: Not aged; during processing the cheese is warmed, stretched and cooled so that the protein strands align and it tears lengthwise. Made at the Taiki plant in Hokkaido; the plant was expanded in 2023.
- Texture / ripening: Unaged, fibrous, tears cleanly.
- Where / who: Megmilk Snow Brand, Hokkaido plant, industrial. Developed at the Cheese Research Laboratory, Kobuchizawa, Yamanashi.
- Sources: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/さけるチーズ — maker, launch 1980, rename 1995, protein-alignment method, Taiki plant expansion; https://macaro-ni.jp/109582 — 100% Hokkaido raw milk for the Snow Brand product; https://www.meg-snow.com/english/business/dairy/ — string cheese listed.
