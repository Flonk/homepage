## Munster (Munster; Géromé in Lorraine; Munster fermier; Munster Géromé)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: washed-rind, soft
- What it is: Soft, washed-rind cylindrical cheese, 13–19 cm across and 2.4–8 cm thick, 450 g to 1.5 kg. A small "petit munster" (7–12 cm, at least 120 g) is also made.
- How it is made: Fresh, rennet-set whole cow's milk, raw or pasteurised. The unwashed, unkneaded curd is ladled into moulds, drained, salted, and the rind smoothed. The rind is hand-rubbed with Brevibacterium linens, giving the orange colour.
- Texture / ripening: Affinage at least 21 days (14 days for petit munster). Each cheese is hand-rubbed and turned every two days in humid cellars at 11 °C.
- Where / who: Made on both sides of the Vosges massif in Alsace (Bas-Rhin, Haut-Rhin), Lorraine (Vosges, Meurthe-et-Moselle, Moselle) and parts of Franche-Comté (Haute-Saône, Territoire de Belfort). AOC in France since 1969, AOP in the EU since 21 June 1996.
- Sources: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munster_(fromage) — milk, format, method, affinage, AOP and regions; https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Géromé — Géromé as the Lorraine name for Munster, with the Vosges origin.
- Unsure: The "Liste de fromages" page dates the Munster AOC to 1978 and lists "Munster fermier" and "Munster Géromé" as separate names. The Munster article gives 1969 for AOC. The Géromé anise variant is marked on its page as unsourced. The grouping of Munster fermier and Munster Géromé under Munster is my own; the list page keeps them apart.

## Chaource
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: soft, bloomy-rind, predominantly lactic
- What it is: Soft bloomy-rind cheese from the Aube; its production zone also reaches part of the Yonne.
- How it is made: Whole cow's milk, not standardised. Lactic starter ripens the milk at 23–35 °C before renneting. Renneting at 23–33 °C, once the milk is at least 20 °Dornic (or pH 6.6 or below); coagulation at least 12 hours. The curd is ladled into hollow moulds at pH 4.75 or below (or whey acidity of at least 53 °Dornic) and drains naturally at 20–30 °C with a mandatory turn. Dry salt on the surface only. Dries at least 24 hours at 10–18 °C. Affinage at least 14 days from renneting, including at least 8 days (excluding drying) at 8–14 °C. Large (450–700 g) and small (250–380 g) formats.
- Texture / ripening: Minimum 14 days' affinage (see above).
- Where / who: Aube (Grand Est, Champagne-Ardenne), with part of the zone in Yonne (Bourgogne-Franche-Comté). AOC since 1970, AOP since 1996.
- Sources: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaource_(fromage) — milk, method, temperatures, formats, AOC/AOP dates and region.
- Unsure: The "Liste de fromages" page tags Chaource to Bourgogne and Champagne-Ardenne and gives AOC 1977, against 1970 on the article. Only part of the zone is in Grand Est.
