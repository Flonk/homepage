## Fresh cow milk, raw (local name not confirmed)
- Animal(s): cow (smallholder dairy and zebu-type cattle)
- Kind: fresh milk / drink
- What it is: Milk from smallholder dairy and cattle herds. Some households drink it raw. A Malawi KAP survey of dairy and cattle farmers names drinking or eating raw milk and raw meat as a risk practice, and not drinking raw animal products as a good practice.
- How it is made: No processing described beyond milking at the homestead and handover to milk bulking groups.
- Where / who: Central region (rural Lilongwe dairy farmers in milk bulking groups from the Central Region Milk Producers Association) and southern region (BLADD area: Mulanje, Thyolo, Chiradzulo, Blantyre). That area had about 70% of the country's dairy herds and the highest milk production, per the paper. Home and smallholder, with cooperative collection.
- Sources: https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC12890104 — raw milk and dairy farmer practices in Lilongwe and southern Malawi; https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC8424705 — milk bulking groups, dairy herd distribution, BLADD milk-producing area, SHIMPA.
- Unsure: No local name is confirmed by any source opened. The sources describe raw-milk drinking as a risk behaviour, not as a named tradition, so how widespread it is is not established.

## Malted peanut milk drink (with non-fat milk powder)
- Animal(s): cow (non-fat dry milk powder as an ingredient)
- Kind: drink (powder-based, flavoured)
- What it is: A peanut-based beverage developed through a Malawian consumer-driven approach. It is made with peanut paste, malted barley or sorghum, and non-fat dry milk powder, plus sugar, salt, xanthan gum and water.
- How it is made: 5 L benchtop batches: water, peanut paste, sugar, salt, malted milk powder and xanthan gum are mixed. Malted milk powder plus peanut paste together make up 16% of the beverage by weight; in the optimal mix, peanut paste is 0.6 and malted milk powder 0.4 of that pair. The malt blend is malted barley or sorghum with non-fat dry milk powder at 1:20 or 1:30.
- Where / who: Lab and product development in Malawi. Industrial-style formulation; no market or sales data given.
- Sources: https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC8834101 — formulation, mixing ratios and sensory optimisation of the beverage.
- Unsure: This is a research formulation. The paper does not show that it is sold commercially, and the milk component is powdered, not fresh milk.
