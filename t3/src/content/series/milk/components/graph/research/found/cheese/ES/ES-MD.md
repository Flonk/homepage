## Queso de Campo Real (queso de Campo Real; "Gran Campo Real" as a trade name)
- Animal(s): Sheep (raw milk). Breeds named: Manchega, Negra Colmenareña, Rubia del Molar, and others.
- Kind: Pressed, enzymatic-coagulation sheep cheese; cured, pasta prensada.
- What it is: Cylindrical farm cheese, about 3 kg, 22 cm across, 12 cm high, with a hard natural rind marked with a pleita (woven esparto) pattern on the sides and a flor on the flat faces. The paste is firm and compact, with small and some larger eyes, ivory to yellowish-white when well aged.
- How it is made: Raw sheep's milk; enzymatic coagulation (rennet); pressed intensively, pasta prensada; brined by immersion in salmuera (brine); matured at least 9 months. Ingredients listed: sheep milk, rennet, sodium chloride. Minimum fat 45%, minimum dry extract 64%, max moisture 36%, NaCl 2.3%, pH 5.3. Cutting, press times, and temperatures are not stated in the sources.
- Texture / ripening: Matured at least 9 months. Firm, slightly dry, crumbly when old. Sensory profile: lactic acidified, sheep and cured notes, roasted sugar and hazelnut, slightly acidic, a little peppery and astringent.
- Where / who: Made in all municipalities of the Campiña del Henares comarca, in the southwest of the Community of Madrid. Origin: in the 1950s a group of shepherds in Campo Real formed a cooperative to make sheep cheese in the traditional way. The article names the cooperative Castellana de Ganaderos (founded early 1950s, 14 ranchers at founding; about 600 members and about 750 tonnes a year in 2017; flock of about 200,000 head). The Telemadrid piece names the producer as Quesos de Campo Real. About 60% of sales are regional. Protected status: none found; the Wikipedia article does not mention a PDO or similar. Sources differ slightly on the producer name, so treat it as a cooperative with a shared brand.
- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queso_de_Campo_Real — main article: origin, breeds, cooperative, flock and output, pairings; https://web.archive.org/web/20171111041741/http://www.alimentacion.es/imagenes/es/Queso%20de%20Campo%20Real_tcm8-11684.pdf — Spanish ministry electronic catalogue of Spanish cheeses (archived copy of the PDF): milk, rennet, pressing, brine salting, 9-month minimum, physical and chemical specs, sensory profile, origin; http://www.telemadrid.es/programas/ruta-179/campo-real-el-queso-un-manjar-100-madrileno — Telemadrid TV feature on Campo Real cheese: producer, output, regional market share, no production method; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campo_Real — the municipality is known for its sheep cheese (lead paragraph).
