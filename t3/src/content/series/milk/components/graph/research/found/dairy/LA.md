## Oliang / Lao iced coffee with condensed milk (kafe lao, kafae yen; oliang)
- Animal(s): cow (sweetened condensed milk)
- Kind: milk drink (coffee)
- What it is: strong Lao coffee served over ice, either black (oliang) or with sweetened condensed milk at the bottom of the glass, followed by a green tea chaser.
- How it is made: coffee poured over ice; condensed milk placed at the bottom of the glass when served with milk. Temperatures and times not given in source.
- Where / who: nationwide, everyday drink; home and café.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_cuisine — describes condensed milk served in the bottom of Lao coffee glasses and the oliang iced coffee black or with condensed milk; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10773088/fullTextXML — 24-hour recall table of rural Lao children lists "Condense milk (with water)" among the top items of the snacks and soft drinks group (a diluted drink, not the coffee itself).
- Unsure: the source is a general cuisine article; preparation details not confirmed beyond that.

## Cow milk, UHT (shelf-stable)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh-style milk, shelf-stable
- What it is: cow's milk sold as UHT, one of the top milk items in the diets of young children in rural Lao PDR.
- How it is made: UHT (ultra-high-temperature treatment), as the source's item label says; no process details given.
- Where / who: rural Lao PDR, children aged 6–59 months, 24-hour dietary recall.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10773088/fullTextXML — table of top food items per group lists "Cow Milk (UHT)" in the milk and milk products group.
- Unsure: the table does not say where the milk was produced (imported or local); it shows consumption, not manufacture.

## Buffalo yoghurt (Laos Buffalo Dairy)
- Animal(s): water buffalo
- Kind: fermented milk
- What it is: yoghurt made from water buffalo milk, noted as richer than cow-milk yoghurt.
- How it is made: buffalo milk collected daily from village herds is pasteurised and homogenised before the products are made (discoverlaos.today); the source does not describe the culture or the fermentation. The Nat Geo source says buffalo milk has about 50% more fat than cow's milk, which makes the yoghurt rich.
- Where / who: Luang Prabang; made by Laos Buffalo Dairy, an industrial/small commercial producer led by Chef Rachel O'Shea.
- Sources: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/luang-prabang-water-buffalo-cheese-food — lists buffalo-milk yoghurt among products made by Laos Buffalo Dairy; https://discoverlaos.today/luangprabang/thing-to-do/laos-buffalo-dairy-one-and-only — the dairy makes "yogurt, cheese and ice cream" and pasteurises, homogenises and stores the milk collected from the buffalo daily.

## Buffalo ice cream (lemongrass and tamarind flavours)
- Animal(s): water buffalo
- Kind: frozen milk dessert
- What it is: ice cream made from water buffalo milk in lemongrass and tamarind flavours.
- How it is made: the milk is pasteurised and homogenised before use (discoverlaos.today, which names ice cream among the dairy's products); the freezing and recipe are not described.
- Where / who: Luang Prabang; Laos Buffalo Dairy.
- Sources: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/luang-prabang-water-buffalo-cheese-food — lists buffalo-milk ice cream in lemongrass and tamarind flavours; https://discoverlaos.today/luangprabang/thing-to-do/laos-buffalo-dairy-one-and-only — names ice cream among products made from the pasteurised buffalo milk.
