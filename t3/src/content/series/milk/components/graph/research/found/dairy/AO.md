## Omavele (Nhaneca-Humbe; sour milk; also "leite azedo" in Huíla)
- Animal(s): goat (Serrana breed, per the thesis record); cattle milk in the Huíla sour-milk dish is not stated
- Kind: fermented milk (acidified, yoghurt-like)
- What it is: a traditional acidified milk of the Nhaneca-Humbe (Nyaneka-Humbe) agro-pastoral people of southwest Angola. The thesis describes it as low in pH, nutritionally valuable and reasonably safe. Wikipedia lists a Huíla "sour milk with maize porridge" (leite azedo com pirão de milho), which is probably the same product, but this is not confirmed.
- How it is made: the sources opened do not give steps. The thesis record says the researchers varied production temperature, production time, starter amount and method, and tested vanilla and lemon flavours in a laboratory replication. Traditional steps are not stated in any source read.
- Where / who: southwest Angola, Huíla and Cunene area; Nhaneca-Humbe people; traditional home production
- Sources: https://agris.fao.org/search/en/providers/125158/records/67497b007625988a372237c9 — 2014 master's thesis record (Bragança): omavele as traditional acidified goat milk from the Nhaneca-Humbe, lab replication with vanilla and lemon, piri-piri cheese; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angolan_cuisine — Huíla "sour milk with maize porridge" (leite azedo com pirão de milho)
- Unsure: the full thesis (method, temperatures, starter) was not readable (bot-check). It is not confirmed that Wikipedia's Huíla sour milk is the same product as omavele.

## Omashikwa (fermented buttermilk; Herero and Ovambo; Namibia with southern Angola)
- Animal(s): cattle (indigenous Sanga cattle, per the Namibian study)
- Kind: fermented buttermilk (butter churned out, buttermilk left to ferment)
- What it is: a traditional fermented buttermilk of the Herero and Ovambo communities. A review of southwest Angolan transhumance names it as made by the Herero and Ovambo of northern Namibia and southern Angola.
- How it is made (per the Namibian study, not Angolan): raw milk (about 20 L) in a calabash/gourd or plastic barrel, with about 12–15 pieces of omunkunzi (Boscia albitrunca) root and about 2% starter from a previous batch (backslopping). Covered and left 2–3 days at ambient 27–36 °C. Roots removed, then the mixture hand-churned 2–3 hours to separate butter; the remaining liquid is omashikwa.
- Where / who: the Namibian paper places production in rural northern Namibia. The Angolan review says Herero and Ovambo in southern Angola make it too. Home/smallholder.
- Sources: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2621.2006.01381.x (opened at https://academic.oup.com/ijfst/article/42/5/620/7864152) — the Omashikwa study: method above, Owambo and Herero, northern Namibia, comparison with a lab product; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10933900/ (Europe PMC PMC10933900 full text) — "fermented buttermilk produced by Herero and Ovambo of northern Namibia and southern Angola plays an important cultural and economic role"
- Unsure: the Namibian paper does not mention Angola at all. The Angolan claim rests only on one review sentence. Whether it is made in Angola is unconfirmed.

## Omaere (sour milk; Herero, Himba)
- Animal(s): cattle (Herero, women milking cows); cattle and goats (Himba)
- Kind: fermented/soured milk, staple with maize porridge
- What it is: a sour milk that Herero women prepared in pre-colonial times (named omaere in the Herero article). The Himba article describes a sour-milk staple with maize porridge made from milk of cows and goats, without naming it.
- How it is made: not described in the pages read. The Himba article says calabash vines are used in producing the soured milk.
- Where / who: mainly Namibia (Kunene Region, Herero and Himba); some Himba live across the Kunene River in Angola. The Angolan link is that people live on both sides; no source read says the product is made in Angola.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herero_people — omaere as pre-colonial sour milk prepared by women; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himba_people — sour milk and maize porridge staple, calabash vines, Himba living in Angola
- Unsure: the name omaere is given for Herero only; the Himba article gives no name. Whether this product is made in Angola is not confirmed; it is included only because Himba and Herero people live in southern Angola.

## Mumuíla cattle milk economy (fresh milk for diet and sale)
- Animal(s): cattle (main); goats and sheep also kept
- Kind: fresh/raw milk (diet and cash sale)
- What it is: cattle milk is the basis of the diet of the transhumant agro-pastoralists of southwest Angola (Huíla and Namibe), including the Mumuíla. The study says the economic use of cattle is mostly milk production, which also brings cash income. It names no local name or processing.
- How it is made: milked by women (main source of milk); no processing described.
- Where / who: Mumuíla and related agro-pastoralists, Huíla and Namibe provinces, transhumance routes; home use and local sale
- Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10933900/ — "economic use of cattle is mostly milk production, which is the basis of the agropastoralists' diet"; milk sold at AOA 200–400 per litre (price in the same paper); women do the milking
- Unsure: the paper's sample of agro-pastoralists was a transhumance and veterinary study, not a dairy study; the milk economy is stated only in passing.

## Leite em pó (milk powder, imported)
- Animal(s): not stated (cow's milk powder is not specified in the report)
- Kind: dried milk (powder)
- What it is: the main dairy import of Angola and the main raw input for local reconstituted milk and other dairy goods. Between 2019 and 2025 Angola imported about 295,000 t of dairy products, worth US$856 million (CIF), with milk powder the leading product by value and by quantity.
- How it is made: not made in Angola in quantity; the report does not describe any process. It is imported as powder and reconstituted or used as an ingredient by local industry.
- Where / who: imported. Portugal supplied 30.7% of dairy import value over 2019–2025; Malaysia, the Netherlands, Ireland, Belgium and France are also named as suppliers. Used by local makers such as Lactiangol and Gulkys.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "Entre 2019 e 2025, Angola importou 295 mil toneladas de lácteos, no valor de US$ 856 milhões, com predominância do leite em pó"; "Portugal ... 30,7% do valor total das importações"; "leite em pó, principal insumo para a fabricação de leite reconstituído"
- Unsure: the report does not say whether any milk powder is produced in Angola.

## Leite reconstituído UHT (reconstituted UHT milk)
- Animal(s): not stated; made from imported milk powder
- Kind: UHT milk, reconstituted
- What it is: the main local milk product, made in Angola from imported powder and sold as fluid UHT milk (leite fluido UHT), with demand rising in cities.
- How it is made: reconstituted from milk powder and other imported inputs; the report gives no further steps.
- Where / who: Lactiangol and Gulkys; the report says the medium-sized firms are in Luanda and the smaller ones are in other regions with better logistics.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "A produção local concentra-se principalmente na fabricação de leite reconstituído (UHT)"; "A Lactiangol atua na produção de leite reconstituído"; "A Gulkys produz leite reconstituído, iogurtes e leite condensado"
- Unsure: the report calls the sector "relativamente pequena" and gives no output figures for the reconstituted milk.

## Manteiga (butter, industrial)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: butter
- What it is: butter made by local dairy firms; named producers are Lactiangol and Aldeia Nova.
- How it is made: not stated. The report says Aldeia Nova works from its own milk and from bought-in milk ("leite próprio e de terceiros"), and that local factories depend on imported milk powder and other ingredients.
- Where / who: Lactiangol and Aldeia Nova; firms are mostly in Luanda.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "A Lactiangol atua na produção de leite reconstituído, bebidas lácteas e manteiga"; "a Aldeia Nova ... produzindo manteiga, iogurtes e queijos a partir de leite próprio e de terceiros"
- Unsure: whether the butter is churned from fresh milk or made from imported cream or butter is not stated. The 2017 Lactiangol capacity figure (1,200 kg/day of butter) is an unconfirmed snippet and is not used here.

## Iogurte (yoghurt, industrial)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: yoghurt
- What it is: packaged yoghurt made by local dairies; the report says urban consumers in Luanda are increasingly buying it.
- How it is made: not stated. The report lists yoghurt cultures and ferments among the inputs local industry imports.
- Where / who: Gulkys and Aldeia Nova; sold through supermarkets and retail chains in the main cities.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "A Gulkys produz leite reconstituído, iogurtes e leite condensado"; "a Aldeia Nova ... produzindo manteiga, iogurtes e queijos"; "crescente procura por: queijos, manteiga, leite condensado, iogurtes"; "culturas lácteas, fermentos" listed as imported inputs
- Unsure: the 2016 Novagrolider yoghurt snippet is not confirmed by the report, which lists Novagrolider under cheese only.

## Leite condensado (sweetened condensed milk)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: condensed milk
- What it is: sweetened condensed milk, made locally by Gulkys and also imported. The report says it is among the cheaper, long-keeping dairy products, favoured in rural areas.
- How it is made: not stated in the report.
- Where / who: Gulkys (local production); rural and low-income buyers.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "A Gulkys produz leite reconstituído, iogurtes e leite condensado"; "para grande parte do território angolano, incluindo a zona rural, o consumo de lácteos se concentra em ... leite condensado e derivados de conservação prolongada"
- Unsure: the report does not say how much of the condensed milk is local rather than imported.

## Bebidas lácteas (milk-based drinks)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: dairy drink
- What it is: milk-based drinks sold in Angolan retail. The report lists them among the most consumed dairy products.
- How it is made: not stated.
- Where / who: Lactiangol makes them; sold mainly through urban retail.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "A Lactiangol atua na produção de leite reconstituído, bebidas lácteas e manteiga"; "Os produtos mais consumidos em Angola são o leite em pó, leite fluido, bebidas lácteas e os queijos fatiados"
- Unsure: flavours and the milk used are not stated.
