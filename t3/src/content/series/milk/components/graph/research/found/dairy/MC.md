## Butter (beurre; eaten as a table spread)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: butter (spread)
- What it is: Butter is listed among Monégasque cuisine's common staples, served alongside honey and jam at the table.
- How it is made: Not stated in the Monaco source. Second source (English Wikipedia "Butter"): butter forms when cream is agitated, which damages the fat-globule membranes so the fat clumps and separates from the buttermilk; the grains are rinsed with water and then pressed and kneaded together to expel leftover buttermilk.
- Where / who: Home and restaurant table use; the source does not say whether it is made locally or imported (Monaco has no commercial agriculture, so it is most likely imported from France).
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monegasque_cuisine — lists butter with honey and jam as common staples; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butter — churning cream, rinsing and working the grains (method).
- Unsure: Local production is not supported by any source found; the product is recorded because it is eaten there.

## Cream in brandaminciun (crème, used in cooking)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: cream (cooking ingredient)
- What it is: Cream is one of the ingredients of brandaminciun, a traditional salted-cod dish.
- How it is made: The source says the dish "can be prepared with garlic, oil and cream" and gives no process.
- Where / who: Monegasque home cooking, as one of several ways to prepare the dish.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monegasque_cuisine — brandaminciun prepared with garlic, oil and cream.
- Unsure: Cream is one option among others; the dish is primarily a salt-cod dish.

## Béchamel sauce (on cardu at Christmas)
- Animal(s): cow (milk-based sauce)
- Kind: sauce (milk-based)
- What it is: A béchamel, a white sauce made from milk, is the sauce in which cardu (cardoon) is served at the Christmas meal.
- How it is made: Not stated in the Monaco source. Second source (English Wikipedia "Béchamel sauce"): cook butter with flour to make a roux, whisk in warm milk or cream, and stir over heat until it thickens; seasoned with salt, pepper and nutmeg (the article gives no ratios or simmering time).
- Where / who: Monegasque Christmas meal, home cooking.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monegasque_cuisine — cardu served in a béchamel sauce at Christmas; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9chamel_sauce — roux of butter and flour, warm milk or cream whisked in, stirred until thick.
- Unsure: The source does not describe the sauce's recipe; the milk-based nature is inferred from its name.

## Tarte au citron (butter-based lemon tart)
- Animal(s): cow (butter in the pastry)
- Kind: butter-based dessert
- What it is: A buttery lemon tart, listed as a Monégasque dessert.
- How it is made: Not stated in the Monaco source. Second source (French Wikipedia "Tarte au citron"): a pâte brisée or pâte sablée base, topped with a lemon curd of beaten eggs, sugar, lemon juice and zest, with butter or crème fraîche optionally added to the curd; no baking times given.
- Where / who: Served as a dessert in Monaco; the source does not say whether it is a home or restaurant dish.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monegasque_cuisine — described as "a buttery lemon tart often served as dessert"; https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarte_au_citron — pastry base and lemon-curd filling (components only; no baking method).
- Unsure: Butter is named only as a descriptor; the recipe is not given.
