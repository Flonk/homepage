## Susu masam (susu kerbau masam; sour buffalo milk)
- Animal(s): water buffalo
- Kind: fermented or sour milk curd, bamboo-fermented (listed as a cheese on Wikipedia)
- What it is: A rare traditional product from Kuala Berang, Terengganu, made from fermented buffalo milk. The milk is fermented until it largely solidifies.
- How it is made: Milk is fermented in bamboo for one night up to three days until it largely solidifies. Coagulant, heating, cutting, draining and salting were not found in the sources opened.
- Texture / ripening: Largely solidified after fermentation (per the List of cheeses article).
- Where / who: Kuala Berang area, Terengganu, Malaysia. Traditional food; no protected status found. The Harian Metro description says it is a traditional dish of the Terengganu community that is now declining because few people make it.
- Eaten with: rice and budu sauce, on its own, or with sugar.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cheeses — states the buffalo milk origin, Kuala Berang and Terengganu, bamboo fermentation of one night to three days, and the eating methods; https://www.hmetro.com.my/mutakhir/2019/12/530042/susu-kerbau-masam — article summary (Harian Metro, 29 Dec 2019): traditional Terengganu dish, now sidelined because few people make it; the body text was not readable, so the method is not taken from it.
- Unsure: The method comes only from one Wikipedia summary; the Malay sources did not give the method in the fetched text. The "solidifies" wording is not confirmed in the Malay sources. Whether it is a true cheese or a sour milk curd is a classification question.
