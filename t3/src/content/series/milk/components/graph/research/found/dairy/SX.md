## Guavaberry coquito (coquito de guayaba; other names: coquito, Christmas coquito)
- Animal(s): cow milk (in some versions evaporated/condensed milk); coconut milk and coconut cream are not animal milk
- Kind: milk drink (alcoholic, Christmas)
- What it is: a spiced, rum-based Christmas drink made with milk and coconut, flavoured with guavaberry. The guavaberry page names it as a guavaberry variant of coquito.
- How it is made: the guavaberry article lists the ingredients as spices, guavaberry, milk, sugar, coconut milk, eggs and rum; no steps or temperatures were given there. The coquito article gives the general method (not Sint Maarten specific): cook gently on the stove (usually with eggs) to thicken and stabilise, or blend all ingredients (no eggs); cool, add rum and vanilla, bottle with cinnamon, chill a few hours, and rest two weeks or more for fullest flavour.
- Where / who: Sint Maarten, as a Christmas drink, in the guavaberry tradition the guavaberry article ties to Sint Maarten and the Virgin Islands. Home-made.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guavaberry — names guavaberry liqueur as a common Christmas drink in Sint Maarten and lists milk in guavaberry coquito; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coquito — general coquito ingredients and method, origin Puerto Rico, no Sint Maarten mention
- Unsure: the source places guavaberry coquito in the Sint Maarten/Virgin Islands tradition only loosely; the Sint Maarten link is stated for guavaberry liqueur, not explicitly for the milk drink. Only one product was confirmed from an opened page.
