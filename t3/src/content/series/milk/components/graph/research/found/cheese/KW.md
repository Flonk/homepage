## Jibneh Arabieh (جبنة عربية; also jibni)
- Animal(s): Traditionally goat or sheep milk (Bedouin herders); current practice uses cow's milk.
- Kind: Soft white cheese; the Wikipedia category list includes "brined white cheeses", but the text does not say it is brined.
- What it is: A mild, white Middle Eastern table cheese, similar to feta but less salty; used in cooking and as a filling in pastries such as knafeh.
- How it is made: Not described on the page (no coagulant, cutting, draining, salting or ripening details found).
- Texture / ripening: Soft; no ripening described.
- Where / who: Found throughout the Middle East and especially popular in the Persian Gulf region; the page links it to Bedouin herders. Kuwait is not named.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jibneh_Arabieh — name, milk, type, Persian Gulf popularity; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cheeses — listed under Middle East with origin "Arabian Peninsula; popular in Persian Gulf Arab states".
- Unsure: The sources place it in the Persian Gulf region generally, not in Kuwait specifically; whether it is made or eaten in Kuwait is not confirmed by any opened page.
