## Leche (cow's milk, fresh and pasteurised)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh milk, drunk plain
- What it is: The everyday milk of Paraguay. It is the main breakfast drink and is served in school snack programmes.
- How it is made: No method given. Milk is sold as a commodity; a plant (Lácteos Trébol) processes about 470,000 litres a day, and its owner aims for 200,000 litres of milk a day across dairy plants.
- Where / who: Nationwide; industrial and farm supply.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_cuisine — milk as breakfast drink and school snack; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Paraguay — milk as breakfast drink and school snack, about 180 litres per person a year; https://infonegocios.com.py/plus/venta-de-queso-paraguay-se-triplico-en-las-ultimas-semanas-debido-a-la-elaboracion-masiva-de-comidas-tipicas — Trébol processes about 470,000 litres a day; https://www.abc.com.py/negocios/abc-campo/2026/06/13/la-lecheria-paraguaya-apunta-al-billon-de-dolares/ — about 900 million litres a year; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_cuisine (second pass, full page) — Mennonite plants in Curuguaty produce 80,000 litres of milk a day; Lactolanda and Trébol named as the leaders of the dairy industry; the Aprole association brings together about 80 producers, many of them cooperatives
- Unsure: Production figures disagree. Wikipedia gives about 1.85 million litres a day; ABC gives about 900 million litres a year (about 2.5 million a day).

## Leche en polvo (milk powder)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: dried milk
- What it is: Dried milk, a main value-added dairy export.
- How it is made: No method given. A powder plant would need about 200,000 litres of milk a day, which the article calls distant.
- Where / who: Industrial. About half of Paraguay's value-added dairy exports, around 90% of them to Brazil.
- Sources: https://www.abc.com.py/negocios/abc-campo/2026/06/13/la-lecheria-paraguaya-apunta-al-billon-de-dolares/ — milk powder as half of value-added dairy exports, 90% to Brazil, plant size; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_cuisine — milk powder listed among dairy products sold

## Suero (whey)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: whey, a by-product of cheese and milk processing
- What it is: The liquid left when milk is curdled. It is a common ingredient in sopa paraguaya and is released when queso Paraguay is cut.
- How it is made: Produced as a by-product of curdling. No method given beyond that.
- Where / who: Industrial processing. A dryer is installed to turn whey into concentrated protein for export.
- Sources: https://www.abc.com.py/negocios/abc-campo/2026/06/13/la-lecheria-paraguaya-apunta-al-billon-de-dolares/ — whey dried into exportable concentrated protein; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_cuisine — whey released when queso Paraguay is cut, and whey or milk used in sopa paraguaya

## Kivevé (sweet cornmeal and milk paste)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: semi-sweet dish with milk
- What it is: A semi-sweet paste of andaí squash and cornmeal, with milk and cheese.
- How it is made: Andaí squash, water, milk, sugar, cornmeal and cheese. The Kivevé Negro variant uses black honey cake soaked in cocido.

- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Paraguay — ingredients and the Kivevé Negro variant; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_cuisine — milk, pumpkin or andaí, water, salt, oil, onion, sugar, corn flour, fresh cheese
- Unsure: The English page gives a Guaraní name in one place and none in another. The English list includes salt, oil and onion, which the Spanish page does not.

## Mate dulce (sweet milk mate)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: hot milky drink
- What it is: A hot mate made with milk, sugar and dried orange peel, served in a gourd.
- How it is made: Milk is heated. Sugar is caramelised slowly in a kettle, the dried orange peel is burned, and everything is combined with the milk in a thermos. It may be flavoured with cinnamon, honey or sugar. Served with grated coconut or ground mbocayá. A skimmed-milk and sweetener version exists.
- Where / who: Home, widely drunk.
- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Paraguay — method and variants; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_cuisine — "sweet mates" described (text garbled)
- Unsure: The English description is garbled and says the drink replaces yerba mate with coconut. The Spanish page says yerba mate is used.

## Cocido con leche (mate cocido with milk)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: hot milk tea (mate)
- What it is: Mate cocido, a hot infusion of yerba mate and sugar, drunk with milk.
- How it is made: Yerba mate is dissolved in hot water by any method, then milk is added. Served hot and sweetened with sugar or sweetener if wanted.
- Where / who: Home and everyday.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paraguayan_cuisine — cocido alone or with milk; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Paraguay — cocido con leche preparation
