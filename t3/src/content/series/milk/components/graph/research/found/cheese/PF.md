## Saint-Tahitien (Saint-Tahitien; made in Taravao, Tahiti)
- Animal(s): cow (milk of dairy cattle on the Taravao plateau, sold as Vai Ora milk)
- Kind: not stated in the source I could open (name suggests a semi-hard or pressed cheese; unconfirmed)
- What it is: one of the three local cheeses of French Polynesia, named after the archipelago, made in Tahiti.
- How it is made: not stated in any page I could open; the Taravao article says only that cheese is made artisanally from local milk.
- Texture / ripening: not stated.
- Where / who: Taravao plateau, Tahiti; made by La Fromagère de Tahiti (founded 2016), described as the territory's only cheese producer.
- Sources: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taravao_(Tahiti) — names the cheese, producer and milk source; the article's references for the cheese (not opened): https://la1ere.francetvinfo.fr/polynesie/tahiti/polynesie-francaise/saint-tahitien-fromage-made-in-tahiti-433667.html and http://www.ladepeche.pf/saint-tahitien-fromage-100-tahitien/
- Unsure: the Taravao article is the only page opened; no making details confirmed.
