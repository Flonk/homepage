## Sweetened condensed milk
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: milk preserve (sweetened, condensed)
- What it is: Sweetened condensed milk is a common product in Cambodia, used in coffee and eaten on bread.
- How it is made: not given in the sources read
- Where / who: industrial; Angkormilk (Phnom Penh plant, brands Best Cows and Captain) produces it. Eaten with baguette at breakfast in towns.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuisine_of_Cambodia — condensed milk on a breakfast baguette and listed as a French-era adoption; https://cambopedia.com/cambodia-milk-dairy-products/ — Angkormilk sweetened condensed milk brands Best Cows and Captain

## Sweetened condensed milk coffee
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: drink (coffee with milk)
- What it is: Coffee served with sweetened condensed milk instead of black, hot or iced. The usual form in urban areas.
- How it is made: coffee poured over or mixed with sweetened condensed milk; no method given beyond that
- Where / who: urban cafés and households; a French-colonial-era adoption according to Wikipedia
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuisine_of_Cambodia — condensed milk coffee in urban areas

## Fresh cow's milk
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh/raw milk (farm-made)
- What it is: Fresh milk sold from small farms near Phnom Penh, in Takeo and in Preah Vihear province.
- How it is made: not given
- Where / who: industrial-scale farms: Kirisu Farm (Takeo, 100% pure milk, 0% fat, barista and cream milk), Moo Moo Farms (1,300–1,500 L a day), Techo Sen Russey Treb (Preah Vihear, 600–700 L a day)
- Sources: https://cambopedia.com/cambodia-milk-dairy-products/ — farm names, capacities and products; https://www.euromonitor.com/dairy-in-cambodia/report — fresh milk is the largest drinking-milk segment and local farms supply it
- Unsure: the cambopedia page calls Kirisu the only cow fresh milk producer in Cambodia, which is a company claim

## UHT (shelf-stable) milk
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: heat-treated milk (UHT)
- What it is: Shelf-stable milk, sold in small pouches.
- How it is made: UHT treatment (temperature and time not given in the source)
- Where / who: industrial; Angkormilk, a Vinamilk and BPC Trading joint venture with a Phnom Penh plant opened in 2015
- Sources: https://cambopedia.com/cambodia-milk-dairy-products/ — UHT liquid milk in 220 ml pouches, Angkormilk plant opened 2015; https://www.euromonitor.com/dairy-in-cambodia/report — shelf-stable milk category

## Butter (fresh)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: butter
- What it is: Butter made on farms and sold alongside fresh milk and cream. Butter and margarine are the preferred cooking fats in modern Cambodian cooking.
- How it is made: not given
- Where / who: Kirisu Farm makes fresh butter; butter is used in baguette sandwiches with sardines or pâté
- Sources: https://cambopedia.com/cambodia-milk-dairy-products/ — Kirisu fresh yoghurt and butter; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuisine_of_Cambodia — butter and margarine as preferred fats, butter in baguette sandwiches

## Clarified butter and curd in Angkorian Cambodia
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: clarified butter; curd
- What it is: Cows and their dairy, including curd and clarified butter, appear often in Angkorian inscriptions. They were used as sacred offerings, in royal and funerary rites, and in medicine.
- How it is made: not given in the abstract
- Where / who: Angkorian-era court, ritual and medical practice
- Sources: https://www.anthropos.eu/anthropos/journal/abstracts/1211/121-1-07.php — abstract of Swati Chemburkar, "Cows, Curd and Clarified Butter. Dairy in Ancient Cambodia"
- Unsure: the abstract does not say ghee by name; "clarified butter" is the closest match. The abstract also states that modern Cambodians eat very little dairy.
