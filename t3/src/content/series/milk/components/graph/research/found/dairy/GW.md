## Thiakry (millet couscous dessert with milk or yoghurt)
- Animal(s): not specified
- Kind: milk sweet / dessert
- What it is: Sweet millet couscous with milk, condensed milk, or yoghurt, with dried fruit and spices. Listed in the Guinea-Bissau cuisine article.
- How it is made: Millet or wheat granules combined with dairy, sweetened, and flavoured with raisins, desiccated coconut, nutmeg.
- Where / who: Widespread across West Africa; names cakri, bodé, dèguè. Origin attributed to Fulani herders in northern Senegal.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiakry — dairy options, ingredients, origin and names; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuisine_of_Guinea-Bissau — thiakry listed in the country's cuisine.
- Unsure: The thiakry article does not name Guinea-Bissau; the link comes only from the cuisine article.
