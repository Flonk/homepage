# Somalia (SO) — cheese
Searched:
- English Wikipedia "Somali cuisine" (opened in full): no cheese, curd or whey mentioned; dairy is ghee, butter, fresh milk, camel milk and a frozen milk dessert (caano jallaato).
- English Wikipedia "Camel milk" (opened): fermented camel milk products listed by name; suusac is named for Kenya and Somalia; no Somali cheese named.
- English Wikipedia "Breakfast by country", "Lahoh" (opened, Somalia sections): flatbreads, ghee, honey, beef jerky; no cheese entry for Somalia.
- Wikipedia title and full-text search for Somali cheese, Somali dairy and camel cheese (API): no Somali cheese article found.
- Europe PMC full-text search for "Somalia cheese", "Somali traditional cheese", "Somali cheese making", "Somalia goat cheese", "Somali soft/fresh cheese", "Horn of Africa cheese traditional", Somali dairy products: yielded camel-milk and food-system reviews; opened PMC13172142 (Horn of Africa camel milk review, 2026), PMC12591359 (indigenous dairy emulsions), PMC11877266 (Eastern African fermented foods), PMC13482330 (Somalia nutrition commentary), PMC13114710 (Horn of Africa fermented flatbreads). None describes a Somali cheese.
- Opened PMC13172142: says cheese from camel milk is largely experimental in the Horn of Africa and Somalia is described as having weak documentation of industrial processing; Somalia's packaged dairy is emerging in urban markets.
- Opened PMC12121524 (dhanaan, Ethiopian Somali region) and the Ethiopian pages referenced: Ayib (cottage cheese) and ergo are Ethiopian products, not Somali; left out.
- Not run: WebSearch (search budget exhausted in this session, so no web-search approach was possible), the Somali-language Wikipedia, and the national dairy and GI registers (no reachable source opened).
- Second pass: Europe PMC searches "Somali camel cheese" and "Somalia sheep goat milk products"; re-read PMC13172142 Table 3 (Somalia row lists raw camel milk, garris, dhanaan and fermented milk; no cheese), PMC12591359 and PMC11877266 (garris, suusac as fermented milks, not cheeses), PMC13482330 (cheese appears only as a generic processed-food class, not a Somali product); re-read Wikipedia "Breakfast by country" raw text (no cheese in its Somalia section, so the "processed mild cheese" snippet is not confirmed). WebSearch was unavailable (budget exhausted), so no Somali cheese was found.

Retry: Somali-language Wikipedia search for "farmaajo" (the Somali word for cheese): 20 of 49 hits opened as titles and snippets only; all are about people, places or politics, except Iswisarland (Switzerland), which is not Somali. No Somali cheese article.
Retry: Wikidata search for "cheese" (50 results): generic cheese types and surnames only; nothing Somali.
Retry: Europe PMC full-text searches for "Somalia cheese OR Somali farmaajo OR Somali camel curd" and "(Somali OR Somalia) AND (curd OR dried milk OR pastoralist dairy)": 50 results read as titles. Only PMC12574850 (camel milk powder, Somali Region of Ethiopia, not Somalia) is a dried dairy product; it is not a cheese, so it is not written up. PMC13482330 and PMC13640307 are Somalia commentary and food-taboo papers with no cheese.
Retry: WebSearch and the other PMC papers already opened were not repeated. Nothing new was found.

Result: no cheese could be confirmed from a page opened for Somalia. The fermented camel milks below are not cheeses under the brief and are listed as leads only.
