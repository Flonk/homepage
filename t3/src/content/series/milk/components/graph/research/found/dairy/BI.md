## Urubu (Kirundi; soured milk)
- Animal(s): not stated in the sources read (cow milk is the default for Burundian herds, but this is not confirmed)
- Kind: fermented milk (soured milk)
- What it is: a household milk product of Burundi, named in a review of Burundian traditional fermented foods and in the Wikipedia fermented-milk table.
- How it is made: not described in any source opened. The review abstract says milk products are made at household level but gives no steps.
- Where / who: household-made in Burundi (review abstract). Who makes it is not stated.
- Sources: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodres.2009.02.021 (Europe PMC abstract, https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=DOI:10.1016/j.foodres.2009.02.021&format=json&resultType=core) — lists urubu among Burundian milk products; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fermented_milk_products — urubu listed under Burundi (soured milk)
- Unsure: method and milk animal unknown; full review text could not be read.
