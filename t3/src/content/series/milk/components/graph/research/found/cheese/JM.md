## Tastee Cheese (canned processed cheese; Tastee)
- Animal(s): Cow (per the newswire article: "a variety of cow milk cheeses")
- Kind: Processed cheese, sold in a can and as a spread in a variety of flavours
- What it is: Canned processed cheese made by Dairy Industries Jamaica Limited (DIJL) in Kingston. It is a Jamaican Easter tradition ("bun and cheese"), per the newswire article.
- How it is made: The cheese is produced in a can. The sources do not describe the process steps.
- Where / who: DIJL, 111 Washington Blvd, Kingston, Jamaica. DIJL was founded in 1964 as a joint venture with the New Zealand Dairy Board and started canned cheese in 1968. In 2025 the Gleaner reported that DIJL is the only manufacturer in Jamaica and the wider Caribbean making processed cheese in a can.
- Sources: https://www.newswire.com/news/tastee-cheese-completes-49-years-7694441 — cow milk cheeses; Easter tradition; DIJL address; "a variety of flavours"; https://jamaica-gleaner.com/article/business/20251226/gracekennedy-acquire-makers-tastee-cheese — "the only manufacturer in Jamaica and the wider Caribbean producing processed cheese in a can"; https://businessviewcaribbean.com/?p=14365 — founded 1964; canned cheese since 1968; factory on Washington Boulevard, Kingston
- Unsure: The sources do not say whether the milk or cheddar base is local or imported. The company's history of supply was disrupted during the pandemic, per Business View. Also, the Gleaner article says a GraceKennedy acquisition of the maker was announced in December 2025.
