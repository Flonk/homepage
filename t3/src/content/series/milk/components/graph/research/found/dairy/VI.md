## Virgin Islands bread with butter and milk (dumb bread)
- Animal(s): cow (milk and butter)
- Kind: bread (yeast-free, baked) with dairy ingredients
- What it is: A yeast-free bread from the Virgin Islands made from flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, butter and milk, sometimes with shredded coconut or cornmeal. Cheddar cheese is used as a filling in some versions.
- How it is made: Mixed, kneaded into a round loaf, rested, then cooked in a skillet or Dutch oven over coals with more coals on the lid; done when golden brown and hollow-sounding when tapped, and rested after baking.
- Where / who: Virgin Islands; docents at Annaberg (historic site, Saint John, U.S. Virgin Islands) sometimes bake it.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumb_bread — ingredients (butter, milk, cheddar filling), method, origin in the Virgin Islands.
- Unsure: Dairy is an ingredient, not the main one, so this is a borderline entry. The article does not say whether the milk is fresh or evaporated. Its categories also list the British Virgin Islands, so the origin is somewhat ambiguous.

## Bushwacker (cocktail with milk or half-and-half)
- Animal(s): cow (milk or half-and-half, optional whipped cream topping)
- Kind: alcoholic milk drink
- What it is: A creamy, chocolate-style blended cocktail. Dark rum, Kahlúa, dark crème de cacao and cream of coconut, with milk or half-and-half, plus ice.
- How it is made: Everything, ice included, goes into a blender; strained into a hurricane glass and garnished with a maraschino cherry; whipped cream optional. The original reportedly also had vodka, a splash of triple sec and nutmeg.
- Where / who: Invented 1975 at the Ship's Store, Sapphire Pub, Sapphire Village, St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands. Commercial bar drink, not a traditional product.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bushwacker_(cocktail) — ingredients, origin (1975, St. Thomas) and preparation.
- Unsure: Milk is one of several liquid options, so the dairy content varies by recipe.
