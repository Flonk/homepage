## Touaregh (Tamasheq/Tuareg name; French "touaregh")
- Animal(s): sheep, goat or cow milk (fresh milk, with rennet)
- Kind: ripened pressed/cooked-type cheese (source calls it one of two ripened West African cheeses)
- What it is: A ripened cheese from Mali, named by a review as one of the two ripened cheeses produced in West Africa by traditional methods (the other being tchoukou from Niger).
- How it is made: Prepared from fresh sheep, goat or cow milk with rennet coagulation. The milk is stirred with a stick soaked in rennet; the coagulum is dried on a mat, and the cheese is then dried in thin pieces on tree branches (PMC9656659 full text, second pass). No heating, cutting, pressing or ripening times given.
- Texture / ripening: Described only as ripened; no detail given.
- Where / who: Mali. Traditional, non-industrial; the review does not name the people or region. Not protected status found.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9656659/fullTextXML (Europe PMC full text of PMC9656659, "Safety and Quality of Milk and Milk Products in Senegal — A Review") — names touaregh from Mali as a ripened cheese, lists its milks and rennet, and contrasts it with tchoukou from Niger; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9864875/fullTextXML (PMC9864875, "Immune System and Epidemics: The Role of African Indigenous Bioactive Substances") — lists touaregh (Mali) among exceptions to spontaneously fermented milk products in Africa.
- Unsure: Only two open sources, both reviews; the method comes from one of them (PMC9656659). A second method source was not found in the second pass. Confirm with a Malian or Tuareg-language source.

## Ta komart (Tamasheq; "ta komart")
- Animal(s): goat and camel milk
- Kind: unclear from source (described as a cheese)
- What it is: A cheese made from goat and camel milk, named as a Tuareg food in the Tuareg people article.
- How it is made: Not described in the source read.
- Texture / ripening: Not given.
- Where / who: Tuareg people. Country of production not stated; Tuareg communities live in Mali and neighbouring countries, but the source does not say Mali.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuareg_people — names ta komart as a cheese made from goat and camel milk among Tuareg foods.
- Unsure: Whether it is made in Mali specifically is not confirmed. Dedicated article at en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ta_komart returned 404.
