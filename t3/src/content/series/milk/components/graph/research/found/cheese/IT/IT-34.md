## Asiago (Asiago DOP; Asiago pressato, Asiago d'allevo; other names: Pegorin, historic sheep form)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: semi-cooked, pressed hard cheese (fresh "pressato" and aged "d'allevo" versions)
- What it is: A DOP cow's-milk cheese from the Asiago plateau and the foothills of Vicenza, Trento and parts of Padua and Treviso. It is one of the best-known Veneto cheeses; its pressed fresh form is eaten young and its aged form is grated.
- How it is made: Pressed Asiago: fresh whole milk is heated to about 35 °C, rennet and lipase are added, the curd is cut to nut-sized pieces and cooked again to about 45 °C, then put in perforated moulds, dry-salted and pressed (hydraulic press, about four hours). The rounds are then banded and aged. Aged Asiago (d'allevo) is matured for months, up to 24. The sources do not give every step for the aged version.
- Texture / ripening: Soft to hard depending on age; the d'allevo versions are crumbly. Ripening 1 to 24 months (PMC11675318 table).
- Where / who: Vicenza province (all), Trento province (all), parts of Padua and Treviso provinces; made in dairies on the plateau and in the lowlands; the d'allevo is also made in summer in the malghe (Altopiano dei Sette Comuni). DOP since 1978; the consortium was founded in 1979.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asiago_cheese — steps of pressed Asiago, origin on the plateau, and DOP area; https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asiago_(formaggio) — DOP zone by province, history, aged/pressed versions; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11675318/ — ripening range and category as a Veneto cheese (Caseus Veneti).

## Monte Veronese (Monte Veronese DOP; Monte Veronese a latte intero; Monte Veronese d'allevo)
- Animal(s): cow (whole or partly skimmed milk)
- Kind: semi-cooked pressed cheese (table cheese when young, grating cheese when aged)
- What it is: A DOP cheese from the northern part of the Verona province (Lessinia and the Veronese Prealps). Two types: whole-milk (25–40 days) and partly skimmed "d'allevo" (aged).
- How it is made: Milk was historically collected from several milkings (the name is linked to the dialect word for milking). The source describes the product types and labels, not the full recipe.
- Texture / ripening: Whole-milk: slightly straw-coloured paste with small scattered holes, mild. D'allevo: 90 days to 6 months (mezzano) or over 6 months (vecchio), sharp, used for grating; can be aged up to two years.
- Where / who: Northern Verona province, Veneto. Slow Food presidium for malga (alpine-pasture) milk, marked with an "M" branded on the rind. DOP since 1996 (Reg. CE 1263/1996).
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Veronese — DOP status, zone, two types, history; https://www.onaf.it/uploads/public/11778_monte-veronese.pdf — ripening times, labels, the "M" malga brand, zone of the municipalities; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11675318/ — PDO category and ripening range.

## Piave (Piave DOP; fresco, mezzano, vecchio, vecchio selezione oro, vecchio riserva)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: hard, cooked-curd cheese
- What it is: A DOP cheese named after the Piave river. The official product comes from the province of Belluno, Veneto. It is sold at five ages, from fresh to a grating cheese.
- How it is made: Made from pasteurised cow's milk. The source gives the ages but not the full process.
- Texture / ripening: Fresco 20–60 days (soft crust, white paste); mezzano 61–180 days; vecchio over 6 months (harder, brownish rind, slightly flaky paste); selezione oro over 12 months; riserva over 18 months. Paste has no eyes; it is firm and straw-coloured as it ages.
- Where / who: Province of Belluno only; consortium-branded rind. PMC11675318 notes the upper Piave valley.
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piave_(formaggio) — DOP zone (Belluno), ages, texture, pasteurised milk; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piave_cheese — hard cooked-curd type, ages and label colours; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11675318/ — Piave as a Veneto cheese, skim milk, upper Piave valley.
- Unsure: The English article says the official Piave is made in Belluno; the Italian page says the same. No conflict.

## Montasio (Montasio DOP)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: semi-hard, pressed cheese (soft when young, harder when aged)
- What it is: A DOP mountain cheese made in Friuli-Venezia Giulia and in the Veneto provinces of Treviso, Belluno, Venice (as far as Chioggia) and the north of Padua.
- How it is made: Cow's milk with bacteria limits, salt and rennet; minimum ageing 60 days (Italian article). Full process not read.
- Texture / ripening: Pale yellow to gold, creamy and open texture; 2–18 months ageing in the PMC table.
- Where / who: Veneto part of the DOP zone is in Treviso (10 producers), Belluno (1), Venice (2) and Padua (1) according to the Italian article. Consortium at Codroipo (Friuli).
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montasio — DOP zone by province, producer counts, minimum ageing; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montasio — Friuli-Venezia Giulia and Veneto; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11675318/ — Montasio PDO category (3 types, 2–18 months).

## Casatella trevigiana (Casatella trevigiana DOP)
- Animal(s): cow (pasteurised milk)
- Kind: fresh, soft, no rind
- What it is: A fresh, soft DOP cheese from the Treviso province, traditionally made by farm housewives in winter with the richest milk.
- How it is made: Pasteurised milk is acidified; coagulation with calf rennet. Curd is put into cylindrical moulds, moved to a warm room to acidify, salted (dry salting in a pan or in brine). Result: cylindrical, shiny, slightly creamed paste, white, with no rind.
- Texture / ripening: Very fresh (under 1 week); lightly creamed paste, no rind.
- Where / who: Produced only in the province of Treviso. DOP (EC Reg. 487/2008). Industrial dairies and producers in the DOP area (PMC11675318 lists it as a fresh, full-fat cheese).
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casatella_trevigiana — DOP, process, zone, uses; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11675318/ — fresh full-fat category, under 1 month.

## Provolone Valpadana (Provolone Valpadana DOP)
- Animal(s): cow (Frisona)
- Kind: pasta filata (stretched-curd) cheese
- What it is: A DOP stretched-curd cheese made in the Po Valley. In Veneto it is made mainly in the west of the Verona province, per PMC11675318.
- How it is made: Raw milk is heated; the "siero-innesto" (whey starter) and rennet of calf, lamb or kid are added; the curd is cut and cooked, then kneaded and stretched in a machine. Shaped, cooled in cold water, brined, and aged in controlled rooms for 10 days to over a year.
- Texture / ripening: Straw-coloured, rounded shapes (salami, melon, pear); aged from 10 days to over one year.
- Where / who: Mainly Verona province in Veneto, according to PMC11675318; dairies in the Po Valley.
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provolone_Valpadana — process and ageing; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11675318/ — Veneto location (western Verona province) and ripening range.

## Morlacco del Grappa (Morlacco; Morlacco di Grappa; Burlacco)
- Animal(s): cow (Burlina breed, raised in the Grappa area)
- Kind: soft, uncooked-curd, low-fat cheese (alpine)
- What it is: A traditional Veneto cheese from the Grappa massif, made from the milk of the Burlina cow (a native breed at risk). It is a Slow Food presidium and a regional traditional cheese.
- How it is made: Historically a soft, lean, raw-curd cheese. Today it is made in alpine pasture with the skimmed evening milk, to which the whole morning milk is added. Ready after about 15 days; best eaten within three months.
- Texture / ripening: Soft; 15 days to 3 months.
- Where / who: Area of Monte Grappa across parts of the provinces of Belluno, Treviso and Vicenza. Alpine pastures (malghe).
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morlacco_(formaggio) — presidium, process, zone, Burlina cow; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morlacco — three provinces, raw curd, ageing; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11675318/ — Veneto regional traditional cheese, produced on permanent mountain farms.

## Formaggio agordino di malga
- Animal(s): cow (Pezzata Rossa and Brown Alpine)
- Kind: semi-fat, semi-cooked, semi-hard to hard alpine cheese (raw milk)
- What it is: A summer cheese from the alpine pastures of the Agordino (Belluno). Local names include "Casél", "Nostrano" and "Formai". It is a regional traditional product (PAT).
- How it is made: Raw whole or partly skimmed cow's milk, made only in summer when the cows graze on the malghe. Cylindrical wheel, 25–35 cm diameter, 3–4 kg; ages 2–3 months for maturity, longer for medium/long ageing.
- Texture / ripening: Straw or brownish rind, firm paste with medium eyes; strong, full flavour, butter and barn notes, sometimes smoky. Ageing: medium to long.
- Where / who: Province of Belluno, Agordino area (Agordo, Alleghe, Canale d'Agordo, Falcade and others). Alpine dairies and malghe.
- Sources: https://www.onaf.it/uploads/public/12427_formaggio-agordino-di-malga.pdf — description, PAT status, zone, local names; https://www.onaf.it/uploads/public/12430_formaggio-agordino-di-malga-reg-veneto.pdf (listed in search results, not opened).
- Unsure: The ONAF sheet is the only source read for this entry.

## Formaggio malga bellunese
- Animal(s): cow (Pezzata Rossa and Brown Alpine)
- Kind: semi-fat, semi-hard alpine cheese (raw milk)
- What it is: A summer alpine cheese of the Belluno Prealps and Dolomites. A recent study said only one malga in six in the Belluno province still makes it by tradition; it is at serious risk of disappearing. Slow Food presidium for the aged version.
- How it is made: Raw cow's milk partly skimmed by creaming. Some producers heat the copper directly over fire, which gives a smoky note. Standard ageing 30–35 days; 2–9 months for the Slow Food product.
- Texture / ripening: Cylindrical, 25–40 cm diameter, 2–7 kg. Rind varies from straw to dark grey (mould). Paste from white to deep yellow with marked, irregular eyes. Aroma intense, especially when mature.
- Where / who: Belluno Prealps and Dolomites; alpine huts (malghe).
- Sources: https://www.onaf.it/uploads/public/12460_formaggio-malga-bellunese.pdf — description, endangered status, ageing, zone, production; https://www.onaf.it/uploads/public/formaggi/12463_formaggio-malga-bellunese-reg-veneto.pdf (in search results, not opened).

## Pecorino fresco di malga
- Animal(s): sheep
- Kind: fresh-to-short-ageing pressed semi-cooked cheese (raw sheep's milk)
- What it is: A sheep's-milk cheese from the alpine pastures of the Asiago plateau (Vicenza province). It is a PAT product of Veneto.
- How it is made: Entirely raw whole sheep's milk from the alpine pastures; pressed, semi-cooked paste. Cylinder 22 cm diameter, about 8 cm tall, 2–2.5 kg.
- Texture / ripening: Thin soft rind, white-straw paste, sweet and not sharp. Short ageing.
- Where / who: Malghe of Vicenza province, especially the communes of Roana and Lusiana. Sold in summer at the producing dairies.

## Caciocapra
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: semi-cooked, firm, heat-treated goat cheese (semi-ripened)
- What it is: A goat's-milk cheese of the Asiago plateau and Lusiana (Vicenza), a PAT product of Veneto. Made from the local tradition of goat herding (Alpine and Saanen breeds).
- How it is made: Heat-treated (thermised) whole goat's milk; the method is passed on orally by cheesemakers.
- Texture / ripening: Cylindrical, compact, elastic and slightly firm; semi-ripened 2–4 months on wooden boards in cool cellars. Rind smooth and straw-yellow; sweet and slightly salty.
- Where / who: Vicenza province, Altopiano dei Sette Comuni, mainly Lusiana.
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caciocapra — milk, zone, PAT, ageing, rind.

## Formaggio acidino
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: fresh, uncooked, creamy or soft cheese (no rind)
- What it is: A fresh goat's-milk cheese typical of the Sette Comuni plateau (mainly Vicenza), a PAT product of Veneto.
- How it is made: Whole milk is pasteurised, filtered and left to rest for 15–20 hours so that it acidifies naturally (up to about 18 % acidity), then moulded.
- Texture / ripening: No rind; white, small, cone-shaped or sausage-shaped; mild and slightly sour; very short shelf life.
- Where / who: Altopiano dei Sette Comuni, Vicenza province.
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formaggio_Acidino — milk, acidification, form and shelf life.

## Nevegal (Nevegal cheese; Formaggio Nevegal)
- Animal(s): cow (Brown Italian and Brown Alpine)
- Kind: semi-cooked semi-hard cheese (brined)
- What it is: A cow's-milk cheese of the Belluno province, named after the Nevegal alpine pasture where it was first made in shared dairies. Now widely sold across Veneto.
- How it is made: Milk heated to 30 °C; powder rennet added and left to set for 40 minutes; curd broken into small granules and cooked to 40 °C for 40 minutes; put into moulds, rested a day, brined a day, then matured about one month in a cool, humid room.
- Texture / ripening: Almost no rind; paste semi-hard; forms 25–30 cm diameter, rind 5–7 cm, about 4 kg; matured about one month.
- Where / who: Belluno province; sold across Veneto.
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevegal_(formaggio) — history, milk, process, forms.

## Tosella (Tosela in Trentino)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh, unsalted, cooked-curd cheese (made from a curd portion taken off in alpine pastures)
- What it is: A fresh cow's-milk cheese made in Trentino-Alto Adige and in Veneto. Veneto version is a PAT product named "Tosella".

- Where / who: Alpine pastures and dairies of Veneto (Trentino-Alto Adige is the other region).
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tosella — PAT status, process, uses.

## Moesin di Fregona (Moesin)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh, cooked-curd, brined cheese
- What it is: A cheese from Fregona in the Treviso province (Cansiglio area), made by a single producer, the Agricansiglio co-operative.
- How it is made: Milk is pasteurised and put into vats at 35 °C; a little cream is added for creaminess; starter and rennet added. Once coagulated, the curd is broken and cooked to 42 °C, put into moulds without cloth, stewed, brined by immersion, dried for 1–2 days, then matured 20–25 days at 9–12 °C and 80–85 % humidity. Keeps about 2 months.
- Texture / ripening: Small cylinders about 7 cm high; ripened 20–25 days.
- Where / who: Fregona, Treviso province; Agricansiglio co-operative at Fratte.
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moesin_di_Fregona — process, producer, dimensions.

## Ricotta fioreta delle vallate vicentine (fioreta)
- Animal(s): cow (from whey)
- Kind: whey cheese (semi-liquid ricotta)
- What it is: A semi-liquid whey ricotta of Recoaro Terme and the Valle dell'Agno (Vicenza), a PAT product. It is eaten fresh and is the basis of gnocchi con la fioreta, celebrated in Recoaro.
- How it is made: The whey left after the curd is removed is reheated to 80 °C; magnesium sulphate is added, which makes a white mass that rises to the top (the name "fioreta" means flowering). It is not fully drained, so consistency varies with the cheesemaker and use.
- Texture / ripening: Semi-liquid to firm depending on residual whey; neutral, fresh, with a slight acid note; not aged.
- Where / who: Recoaro Terme and Valle dell'Agno, province of Vicenza.
- Sources: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricotta_fioreta_delle_vallate_vicentine — process, taste, dish.
