## Metsovone (Μετσόβου / Metsovone; Metsovela, Foumarino, Boukovela are related Metsovo names)
- Animal(s): cow's milk, or cow mixed with sheep or goat milk
- Kind: semi-hard smoked pasta filata
- What it is: a smoked, stretched-curd cheese from the Aromanian village of Metsovo in the Pindus mountains of Epirus. It is the Metsovo cheese best known outside the area.
- How it is made: the source gives no steps for heating, rennet, cutting or pressing. It is smoked and aged for more than three months. Traditionally unpasteurised; commercially made from pasteurised milk.
- Texture / ripening: semi-hard, smoked; aged over three months.
- Where / who: Metsovo, Epirus; made at the Tositsa Foundation cheese factory of Metsovo (per the source). PDO since 1996.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metsovone — PDO status, milk, smoked pasta filata, aging, Metsovo origin; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirotic_cuisine — names Metsovone, Foumarino, Boukovela and Metsovela as Metsovo cheeses.
- Unsure: the Wikipedia article gives no production method, so the method is unconfirmed.

## Kefalotyri of Epirus (Κεφαλοτύρι, traditional sheep-milk type from Arta and Filippiada)
- Animal(s): sheep (native Karamaniko and Karagouniko breeds crossbred in the Arta area); goat milk may be mixed in
- Kind: hard, salty, grating cheese
- What it is: a hard salty cheese made in Epirus. A research programme named "Traditional Kefalotyri of Epirus" was funded for the local producer.
- How it is made: traditionally raw sheep milk with more than 3 % salt. Nowadays the milk is pasteurised or thermised (63–65 °C for 30–60 s) before cheesemaking. Steps other than heat treatment are not given in the source.
- Texture / ripening: hard and dry; ripening time not given.
- Where / who: the Arta area of Epirus; a traditional dairy at Filippiada, Epirus, producing from sheep yards at Filippiada and Dryofyto. Protected status not given in the source.
- Sources: https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10727937 (Europe PMC full text PMC10727937, Sioziou et al. 2024, Current Research in Microbial Sciences) — Kefalotyri made from raw or thermised sheep milk in Epirus, >3 % salt, thermisation 63–65 °C for 30–60 s, sheep breeds, Arta and Filippiada sources.
- Unsure: the Wikipedia Kefalotyri article names only Greece and Cyprus; the Epirus provenance rests only on the research paper.

## Feta (Φέτα), Epirus artisanal example
- Animal(s): sheep, with goat (up to 30 % goat milk in the PDO)
- Kind: brined fresh/ripened cheese
- What it is: Greece's best-known brined sheep-milk cheese. Epirus is in the mainland PDO area.
- How it is made: artisanal Feta from Theodoriana (Arta, Epirus) was made from raw sheep milk without starter cultures, with rennet only.
- Texture / ripening: sampled at early and late ripening; not described further.
- Where / who: Theodoriana, Arta, Epirus (artisanal producer). Epirus dairy companies (e.g. DODONI) also make feta.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feta — PDO area is mainland Greece and Lesbos, milk rule (sheep, up to 30 % goat); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirus_(region) — Epirus dairy brands make feta; https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC8780491 — artisanal Feta from Theodoriana (Arta), starter-free, rennet only.
- Unsure: that Epirus is inside the PDO is an inference from "mainland Greece" in the PDO text, not a sentence naming Epirus.

## Kefalograviera (Κεφαλογραβιέρα), Epirus artisanal example
- Animal(s): raw sheep milk (artisanal samples)
- Kind: hard, pressed, cooked-curd cheese
- What it is: a Greek hard cheese made in the same artisanal style as Feta; an artisanal example was sampled in Epirus.
- How it is made: artisanal sample made without starter cultures, rennet only. Full method not given.
- Texture / ripening: sampled at early and late ripening; microflora is dominated by Levilactobacillus brevis and Enterococcus faecium early, and Weissella later.
- Where / who: Theodoriana, Arta, Epirus. Elsewhere Samarina, West Macedonia (not Ípeiros).
- Sources: https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC8780491 — artisanal Kefalograviera from Theodoriana (Arta, Epirus), starter-free, rennet only.
- Unsure: this is one artisanal sample; PDO and wider availability in Epirus not confirmed.
