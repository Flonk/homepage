## Feta (Φέτα, ΠΟΠ)
- Animal(s): sheep; up to 30% goat is allowed; cow or buffalo are not part of the PDO.
- Kind: brined, white, crumbly.
- What it is: the PDO cheese of Greece, made from sheep (or mixed sheep and goat) milk, matured in brine.
- How it is made: pasteurised milk cooled to about 35 °C; rennet added; curd cut and placed in moulds or cloth bags to drain; dry-salted and left several days; then brined in 7% salt solution for several weeks at room temperature; then matured at least 2 months refrigerated and humid.
- Texture / ripening: crumbly; at least 2 months of ripening.
- Where / who: the PDO area is given as "mainland Greece and Lesbos" in English Wikipedia; Greek Wikipedia's PDO list for this cheese names Sterea Ellada (Στερεάς Ελλάδας) as part of the area. Protected status: PDO.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feta — PDO milk rule, method steps, and the "mainland Greece and Lesbos" wording; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Κατάλογος_ελληνικών_τυριών — Sterea Ellada named in the Feta PDO area.
- Unsure: the English and Greek sources disagree on whether the area names Sterea Ellada; the Greek list is the one that names it, and the EU registration text was not read.

## Katiki Domokou (Κατίκι Δομοκού, ΠΟΠ)
- Animal(s): sheep and goat (blend).
- Kind: soft, white, low-fat fresh cheese.
- What it is: a soft fresh cheese from Domokos in the Phthiotis prefecture.
- How it is made: pasteurised milk that sets without rennet; curd drained in fabric sacks; no times or temperatures given.
- Texture / ripening: soft; sold packaged, not aged.
- Where / who: Domokos, Phthiotis (Fthiotida); PDO (no registration date given); max 75% moisture, 1% salt, at least 40% fat in dry matter.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katiki_Domokou — origin, milk, method, PDO; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Κατίκι_Δομοκού — composition limits and retail, uses on toast and in dakos.
- Unsure: none on origin; the method is not detailed.

## Formaela Arachovas (Φορμαέλα Αράχωβας, ΠΟΠ)
- Animal(s): sheep and/or goat (mainly).
- Kind: hard cheese, pungent.
- What it is: a hard cheese produced in Arachova, grilled or fried (often as saganaki).
- How it is made: no steps, times, or temperatures given; composition max 38% moisture and min 40% fat.
- Texture / ripening: firm, cohesive rind; light yellow interior with no holes; strong flavour.
- Where / who: Arachova, Mount Parnassus (the PDO name is "Φορμαέλα Αράχωβας Παρνασσού"); Arachova is in Boeotia (Viotia), the prefecture is not stated on the cited page. PDO registered in the EU since 1996.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formaela — origin, milk, texture, use, PDO since 1996; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Κατάλογος_ελληνικών_τυριών — Arachova PDO entry.
- Unsure: the prefecture (Viotia) is my own addition from general geography, not from the cited page.

## Kefalograviera (Κεφαλογραβιέρα, ΠΟΠ)
- Animal(s): sheep; less than 10% goat.
- Kind: hard cheese, salty.
- What it is: a hard table cheese, salty, with nutty and buttery notes, used in cooking and for saganaki.
- How it is made: the page only says it is made like kefalotyri; no steps given. Ripening takes about 3 months.
- Texture / ripening: firm and ivory, with small scattered holes.
- Where / who: the PDO area includes Aetolia-Acarnania and Evrytania (regional units) on English Wikipedia; the Greek list also names Western Macedonia, Epirus and these two. PDO status.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kefalograviera — regions named, PDO status; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Κεφαλογραβιέρα — milk, texture, ripening; no region named there.
- Unsure: the Stereá part of the area (Aetolia-Acarnania, Evrytania) comes from the English page only; the Greek page names no region.
