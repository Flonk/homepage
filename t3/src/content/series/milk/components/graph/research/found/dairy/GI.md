## Béchamel (béchamel; French: béchamel; Spanish: bechamel)
- Animal(s): Cow milk (in general béchamel is a milk-based sauce; the Gibraltar source does not say so)
- Kind: Sauce (milk-based)
- What it is: A white sauce used as a topping in fideos al horno, a baked macaroni dish, where it melts and binds the dish during baking.
- How it is made: Not described on the page opened.
- Where / who: Gibraltar; home cooking. Not stated.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibraltarian_cuisine — names béchamel as the topping of fideos al horno; does not describe its ingredients.
- Unsure: Béchamel is a general European sauce used here as a topping; it is not a Gibraltar-specific dairy product, and the milk base is not stated for Gibraltar.
