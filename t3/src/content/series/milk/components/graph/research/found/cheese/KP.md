- How it is made: Not stated.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated.
- Where / who: Pyongyang, Kwangbok Street pizza restaurant; imported from Italy with flour, butter and an Italian pizza oven. Not made in the DPRK. Customers not described in the sentence.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_in_North_Korea — opened; states the chefs "used flour, butter, and cheese that had been flown in from Italy, as well as an Italian pizza oven", citing China Daily (2009), The Guardian (2009), Atlas Obscura (2018) and Vice (2018), none of which I could open.
