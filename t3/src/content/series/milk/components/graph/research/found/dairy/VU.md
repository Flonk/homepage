## Ice cream and frozen desserts (imported)
- Animal(s): cow (imported; milk not identified by source)
- Kind: frozen dessert
- What it is: Ice cream and edible ices imported into Vanuatu, tracked in import statistics and tariff analysis.
- How it is made: not described in the sources.
- Where / who: imported, sold on the market; the sources give no local production.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC8447725/fullTextXML (Ravuvu et al. 2021, Global Health 17:107, via Europe PMC full text PMC8447725) — ice cream and edible ices rising in imports 2008–2019 and tariff context; 5 locally owned food and beverage firms since 2014 list ice cream and frozen desserts among their products.
- Unsure: local production is not confirmed. The paper says "32 locally owned food and beverage companies registered since 2014", and of those, 5 sell confectionery, ice cream and frozen dessert products. It is not clear whether these are made in Vanuatu or re-packed.
