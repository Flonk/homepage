# Botswana (BW) — cheese
Searched:
- English Wikipedia "Cuisine of Botswana" (opened): dairy mentioned only as sour/fermented milk (madila, sour milk in bogobe and melon dishes); no cheese, curd cheese or whey cheese named.
- English Wikipedia "List of cheeses" (opened, first ~100k of 303k characters): only African entry found was Kwaito (South Africa); no Botswana entry in the part read. Rest of the page not read.
- Europe PMC full-text search for "Botswana" AND cheese (API): hits were off-topic (clinical, veterinary, nutrition papers); no cheese source.
- Europe PMC search for (Botswana OR Setswana OR Tswana) AND (cheese OR curd OR fermented milk OR madila): hits were general African fermented-food reviews and milk-safety papers.
- Europe PMC full text of PMC10678836 ("Microbial Diversity in African Foods and Beverages", opened): Botswana row in its table lists ting (sorghum) and madila (cow or goat milk, lactic bacteria); text says madila is a fermented milk product sold commercially in Botswana. Fermented milk, not cheese; recorded as a lead only.
- Web search tool: budget exhausted before the checklist was done (no fresh cheese, cottage, whey, brined, stretched, bloomy, washed-rind, blue, pressed, smoked, dried-curd, PDO/GI, or per-animal searches were run). The checklist is therefore NOT complete.
- Not yet tried for lack of budget: Setswana-language searches (tswana terms for cheese and curd), SADC/Botswana dairy board or ministry pages, FAO Botswana dairy pages, Botswana cheese makers and shops, goat or camel cheese searches.
- Second pass: journals.ub.bw "Dairy production in Botswana: current status and prospects" (Aganga, Thobega & Setimela, 2010) opened: cheese listed among dairy imports; no local cheese production named.
- Second pass: afsic.net "Top dairy companies in Botswana" opened: Parmalat Botswana listed as making cheese, with no variety named.
- Second pass: PMC6417723 and PMC9817819 opened: no Botswana cheese.
- Second pass: web search budget exhausted again; no further cheese searches were possible.

## Parmalat Botswana cheese (variety not named)
- Animal(s): not specified (presumed cow)
- Kind: industrial cheese
- What it is: Cheese listed among the products of Parmalat Botswana, alongside UHT milk and cream.
- How it is made: Not described in the source.
- Where / who: Industrial, Botswana.
- Sources: https://www.afsic.net/top-dairy-companies-in-botswana/ — Parmalat Botswana: "Their product lineup also includes a variety of other dairy goods, such as cheese and cream"; https://journals.ub.bw/index.php/bojaas/article/view/292 — cheese among dairy imports
- Unsure: The cheese type and process are unknown; the company-listing source is secondary and the Aganga paper lists cheese only as an import.
