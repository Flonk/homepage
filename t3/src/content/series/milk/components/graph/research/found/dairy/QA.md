## Ghee (سمن; samn)
- Animal(s): cow or buffalo milk (cow most likely in Qatar; not confirmed for Qatar specifically)
- Kind: clarified butter / butter oil
- What it is: Butterfat cleared of water and milk solids. Used as a cooking fat and a garnish in Qatari dishes.
- How it is made: Butter is simmered and skimmed; the clear fat is poured off and the milk solids are discarded. A traditional route starts with boiled milk, cultured with yoghurt and churned to butter, then simmered. One source gives pre-stratification at 80–85 °C, drain buttermilk, then heat the fat to 110 °C.
- Where / who: Used in harees (fat mixed into wheat; garnish) and jareesh (a main ingredient), both listed in the Qatari cuisine article. Home and everyday use; industrial production not checked.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_cuisine — ghee as the fat in harees and as a main ingredient of jareesh; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harees — ghee as garnish, Qatar listed among countries where harees is eaten; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghee — general method of making ghee (no Qatar detail)
- Unsure: The method comes from general ghee sources, not from Qatari practice. Qatari-specific making is unconfirmed.

## Butter (زبدة; zubda)
- Animal(s): cow (most likely); camel only where soured first (see camel milk)
- Kind: butter
- What it is: Fat churned from cream or soured milk. Used in Qatari sweets and savoury dishes.
- How it is made: Not described for Qatar. The camel milk article says camel milk must be soured before churning, and a clarifying agent is added.
- Where / who: Ingredient in luqaimat (sweet dumplings) and khabees (a sweet, per the Qatari cuisine article); also cooked into harees. Home cooking; industrial not checked.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_cuisine — butter in luqaimat and khabees; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harees — butter cooked into harees; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camel_milk — camel butter needs soured milk, churning and a clarifying agent
- Unsure: Qatari butter source is a dish ingredient list, not a description of butter-making in Qatar. Second pass: the Khabees and Luqaimat Wikipedia pages list no butter or milk, so the butter-in-luqaimat/khabees claim rests on the Qatari cuisine article alone and conflicts with those pages.

## Evaporated milk (in karak)
- Animal(s): cow (commercial)
- Kind: preserved milk (evaporated)
- What it is: Concentrated, sterilised milk tinned for shelf life. In Qatar it is the milk in karak, the spiced sweet tea.
- How it is made: Not described in the sources opened.
- Where / who: Used in karak, the Gulf milk tea; home and cafés; industrial brands not named.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_cuisine — evaporated milk added to spiced tea in karak; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masala_chai — "Chai Karak" name for the Gulf and popularity among South Asian expatriates in the Gulf
- Unsure: Qatar-specific use is from the Qatari cuisine article only. The brand and sourcing of the milk are unchecked.

## Karak (kark; karak chai) — milk tea drink
- Animal(s): cow (via evaporated milk)
- Kind: milk tea / milk drink
- What it is: Strong black tea boiled with evaporated milk, sugar and spices (cardamom common). Popular across the Gulf.
- How it is made: Not described for Qatar in the sources opened; the Qatari cuisine article says evaporated milk is added with spiced tea.
- Where / who: Everyday drink; cafés and home. Introduced to the Gulf by South Asian expatriates, per the masala chai article.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qatari_cuisine — karak with evaporated milk and spiced tea; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masala_chai — Gulf naming and expatriate origin, citing Khaleejisque 2011 and Al-Saigh 2024
- Unsure: Spices and method are from general Gulf usage. Drink, not a dairy product on its own; included because milk is its main ingredient.

## Fresh cow's milk (Baladna)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh milk; processed dairy
- What it is: Fresh cow's milk and processed dairy sold in Qatar by the national dairy company Baladna.
- How it is made: Cow milking began in July 2017 at a farm in Umm al Hawaya; the company began processed dairy in May 2017. The company was first a sheep and goat farm.
- Where / who: Baladna, headquartered in Al Khor. By the article's account it supplied over 85% of the country's fresh dairy products and half of Qatar's fresh milk by June 2019, and later exported dairy products.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baladna_(company) — milking start, processed dairy from May 2017, market share, exports, Al Khor HQ
- Unsure: Market-share and export figures are from one encyclopaedia article; no company or government source checked. The article does not say which processed dairy products are made.
