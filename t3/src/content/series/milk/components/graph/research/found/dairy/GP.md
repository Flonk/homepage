## Chaudeau (chaudeau; Guadeloupean eggnog)
- Animal(s): cow (milk or cream)
- Kind: drink
- What it is: A rich, eggnog-style drink made with milk or cream, served on special occasions. The Guadeloupean version is paired with a whipped sponge cake (génoise).
- How it is made: Not given in the Guadeloupe source. General eggnog method (second source, not Guadeloupe-specific): milk or cream and sugar, with raw eggs whipped to a froth; a spirit is optional; some recipes cook the yolks with the milk into a custard, and the drink is served chilled.
- Where / who: Guadeloupe; home preparation for special occasions.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadeloupean_cuisine — names chaudeau as a Guadeloupean-style eggnog made with milk or cream, served with génoise (Gastronomy section); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eggnog — general ingredients and preparation of eggnog (milk or cream, sugar, whipped eggs, optional spirit, served chilled); not a Guadeloupe source
- Unsure: The Guadeloupean recipe itself is not confirmed. The eggnog-style description may be a simplification, and the method above is the general eggnog method.

## Blancmange (blanc-manger)
- Animal(s): cow (milk, if the usual pudding recipe applies)
- Kind: sweet / dessert
- What it is: A dessert listed among the island's sweets.
- How it is made: Not given in the Guadeloupe source. General method (second source, not Guadeloupe-specific): milk or cream with sugar, thickened with rice flour, gelatin or cornflour, often flavoured with almonds; set in a mould and served cold.
- Where / who: Guadeloupe; home and bakery desserts.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadeloupean_cuisine — lists blancmange among Guadeloupean sweets (Gastronomy section); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blancmange — ingredients (milk or cream, sugar, rice flour, gelatin or cornflour, almond flavouring) and serving in a mould, cold; not a Guadeloupe source
