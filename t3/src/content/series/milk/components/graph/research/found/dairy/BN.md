## Teh C (teh si; Malay "teh C")
- Animal(s): cow (evaporated milk; animal not stated in sources)
- Kind: milk tea (drink)
- What it is: A milk tea made with unsweetened evaporated milk; common in Malaysia and listed in Brunei's drinks.
- How it is made: Strongly brewed black tea poured between vessels, with unsweetened evaporated milk added; sugar is usually added by the vendor unless "kosong" (no sugar) is asked for. Variants Teh C Peng (layered tea, milk, palm sugar syrup) and Teh C Ais (iced) are named in the source.
- Where / who: Malay-speaking population; street stalls and kopitiams. Brunei named among places drinks are found. Industrial supply of evaporated milk.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teh_C — describes Teh C as evaporated-milk milk tea and its variants; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Brunei — lists milk tea and Teh C among Bruneian drinks
- Unsure: The Teh C page covers Malaysia; Brunei presence rests on the Culture of Brunei drinks list.

## Teh tarik
- Animal(s): cow (sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk; animal not stated)
- Kind: milk tea (drink)
- What it is: A hot, frothy milk tea, most often found at restaurants, outdoor stalls and kopitiams; Brunei is named among countries where it is found.
- How it is made: Strongly brewed black tea mixed with sweetened condensed milk (evaporated milk may be added), then poured repeatedly between two vessels from a height to froth and cool it. Served hot.
- Where / who: Malaysia-based drink, widely found in Brunei; street and restaurant use.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teh_tarik — describes ingredients, pouring method and Brunei as a place it is found
- Unsure: The page gives no Brunei-specific recipe or variant.

## Bandung (sirap bandung)
- Animal(s): cow (evaporated or condensed milk; animal not stated)
- Kind: sweet milk drink
- What it is: A pink, sweet milk drink flavoured with rose syrup; popular in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore.
- How it is made: Evaporated or condensed milk flavoured with rose-based cordial syrup; the source gives no step-by-step method. In Malaysia it is often served with grass jelly.
- Where / who: Malay communities, served at Ramadan iftar and weddings; home and street/cafe use.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandung_(drink) — ingredients, Brunei named among places where it is popular; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirap_bandung — Brunei listed as a place it is popular and associated with its cuisine
- Unsure: Brunei is named only as one of several places; no Brunei-specific serving details.

## Imported condensed and sweetened milk, milk and cream, yoghurt and buttermilk (imported, mainly from Thailand)
- Animal(s): cow (not stated in source)
- Kind: imported dairy products (concentrated or sweetened milk and cream; milk and cream not concentrated; buttermilk, yoghurt and kefir)
- What it is: Dairy products bought in Brunei but not made there. The 2024 import values from Thailand were: concentrated or sweetened milk and cream about US$747K; buttermilk, yoghurt and kefir about US$160K; milk and cream not concentrated about US$52K.
- How it is made: Not described in the source; made by the exporter in Thailand, not in Brunei.
- Where / who: Imported into Brunei; the source does not say which brands or retail outlets sell them.
- Sources: https://tradingeconomics.com/brunei/imports/thailand/dairy-products-eggs-honey-edible-products — 2024 Brunei import values from Thailand for each dairy item
- Unsure: Trade statistics only; these are imports, not Bruneian products, and the source does not show the products on sale. Entered because the file asks for supermarket products.
