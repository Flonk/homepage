## Sbrinz (Sbrinz AOP; also "Alp-Sbrinz")
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: pressed, very hard cooked-curd cheese (Hartkäse)
- What it is: A very hard cheese made in central Switzerland; one of the oldest cheeses in Europe. It is made by hand in copper kettles in Luzern, Obwalden and Nidwalden.
- How it is made: Raw cow's milk, with cultures and salt; rennet coagulation. Ripening is at least 16 months before sale (de.wikipedia says at least 18 months); the best flavour comes after about 24 to 30 months. The source gives no cutting, cooking or pressing temperatures or times.
- Texture / ripening: Very hard; traditionally grated, broken with a special cutter, or planed rather than cut. About 40–45% fat in dry matter. Stronger after five years.
- Where / who: Luzern, Obwalden and Nidwalden (de.wikipedia, wikivoyage). Described as produced in 42 dairies in "Central Switzerland" (en.wikipedia). Protected: AOC from 2001, now AOP (since 2013); Alp-Sbrinz is a protected name too.
- Sources: https://de.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Luzern_(Kanton) — names Sbrinz as made by hand in copper kettles in Luzern, Obwalden and Nidwalden; https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sbrinz — raw milk, rennet, ripening, three cantons, AOP status; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sbrinz — cow's milk, ripening, 42 dairies in central Switzerland, AOC 2001 and AOP 2013.
- Unsure: The sources differ on the minimum ripening (16 months en, 18 months de); both are recorded above. The AOP register itself was not reachable, so the protection details are from Wikipedia.
