## Fresh cow's milk (cow milk, self-sufficient supply)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh/raw or pasteurised milk
- What it is: Fresh cow's milk produced on the island, which the sources describe as one of the few farm products Barbados does not have to import.
- How it is made: not described in the sources read.
- Where / who: local production by small household herds; industrial/supermarket supply implied but not described.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture_in_Barbados — says Barbados is self-sufficient in milk and poultry, apart from the island's large imports of other basic foods; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture_in_Barbados — also says livestock is mostly owned by individual households and that the island must import large quantities of dairy products.
- Unsure: the source gives the self-sufficiency claim and the import claim together, without figures for the current year. The article's livestock numbers are from 1999 (23,000 cattle, 5,000 goats).

## Butter (home-churned, historical)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: butter
- What it is: A participant in a Barbados qualitative study recalled that people once churned their own butter at home.
- How it is made: home churning, not described in detail.
- Where / who: home production in the past; the source does not say whether it is still done.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13164642/fullTextXML — one participant quote: "A long time ago, we could have churned our own butter."
- Unsure: a single memory from one participant. Current home or commercial butter production is not confirmed.
