## Isle of Man Creamery milk (fresh whole, semi-skimmed and skimmed cow milk)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: heat-treated fluid milk (pasteurised packaged milk, as sold by the creamery)
- What it is: Fluid cow milk sold in whole, semi-skimmed and skimmed forms. The creamery says it is a co-operative of family-owned dairy farms with farm-assured herds supplying "farm fresh, grass-fed" milk.
- How it is made: Not described in the sources read beyond the creamery handling the milk; the sources give no processing temperatures or times.
- Where / who: Isle of Man, industrial. About 26 million litres of milk is produced on the island each year. Milk travels under 15 miles from farm to creamery. The creamery has processed dairy since 1919.
- Sources: https://www.biosphere.im/partners/isle-of-man-creamery — farm-assured herds, under 15 miles from farm to creamery; https://www.foodmanufacture.co.uk/Article/2017/11/30/Creamery-on-the-Isle-of-Man-faces-special-challenges — whole, semi-skimmed and skimmed milk listed as dairy liquid products; https://www.dairyindustries.com/news/46942/small-island-big-stomach/ — creamery makes milk, cream, butter and buttermilk; 26 million litres a year; https://www.isleofmancreamery.com/ — co-operative of family-owned dairy farms, "farm-assured herds", product tile for Milk
- Unsure: The 2017 article says the cooperative has 28 or 30 farmers, and a trade report says 33, so the farm count varies across sources. The creamery's own butter page says "32 dairy farms" (https://www.isleofmancreamery.com/products/butter), a third figure.

## Butter (full cream butter, made from cream)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: butter
- What it is: Butter sold by the Isle of Man Creamery. Dairy butter is made with Isle of Man Salt Company products, according to the trade report.
- How it is made: The 2017 article describes butter cream being turned into full cream butter. No churning temperatures or times are given.
- Where / who: Isle of Man, industrial (creamery).
- Sources: https://www.foodmanufacture.co.uk/Article/2017/11/30/Creamery-on-the-Isle-of-Man-faces-special-challenges — "butter cream – which gets turned into full cream butter"; https://www.dairyindustries.com/news/46942/small-island-big-stomach/ — butter in the creamery's range, dairy butter made with Isle of Man Salt Company products; https://www.isleofmancreamery.com/products/butter — "Salted Dairy Butter (250g)": "made only with milk from our 32 dairy farms"
- Unsure: The salt link appears in a single trade-press sentence; it is not confirmed elsewhere. The butter page does not say whether the butter is churned from cream.
