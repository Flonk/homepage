## Jersey cow milk (fresh milk, pure-breed Jersey)
- Animal(s): cow (Jersey breed)
- Kind: fresh milk, sold as full-fat, semi-skimmed and skimmed
- What it is: Milk from the Jersey breed, known for high butterfat. The island dairies hold a monopoly on local milk supply.
- How it is made: No processing steps found in the sources opened.
- Where / who: Island-wide, industrial/commercial supply. Jersey Dairy says all its milk comes from pure-breed Jersey cows; the 1789 import ban on cattle is cited to protect the breed.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_Island_milk — milk types and island dairy monopoly, fat/protein comparison with Holstein; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_(cattle) — breed, 1789 import ban, butterfat 4.84%; https://www.jerseydairy.com — pure-breed Jersey claim, farmer-owned
- Unsure: Whether pasteurisation is used is not stated in the sources opened.
