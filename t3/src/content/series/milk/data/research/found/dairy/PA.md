## Manjar blanco (manjar; sweet filling)
- Animal(s): cow (milk, as dulce de leche type; milk type not stated for Panama)
- Kind: sweet / milk caramel
- What it is: A milk-based sweet; on the Panama pages it is a sweet filling for empanadas, equated there with dulce de leche.
- How it is made: General dulce de leche method from the dulce de leche article: simmer milk and sugar while stirring until thickened and browned, reduced to about one sixth of the volume. Not confirmed as the Panamanian method.
- Where / who: Panama (as empanada filling); the term is widely used across Spanish-speaking America.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuisine_of_Panama — empanada filling, equated with dulce de leche; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manjar_blanco — term used in Spanish-speaking regions; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulce_de_leche — general method; Panama not named there.
- Unsure: Whether Panamanian manjar blanco follows the dulce-de-leche method is not confirmed. The sources differ in naming.

## Chicheme (chicheme; maize drink with milk)
- Animal(s): cow (milk as an ingredient; species not stated)
- Kind: drink / maize drink with milk
- What it is: A drink of maize with milk and sugar, flavoured with spices.
- How it is made: Maize is left to ferment gradually, soaked and finely ground; the dough is cooked and cold water is added to strain it. Cinnamon, nutmeg, panela and ginger are added (per the article's text).
- Where / who: Panama, especially Colón and Panamá Oeste (preparation noted in La Chorrera); the same article also places it in Costa Rica and Colombia.
- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicheme — ingredients, fermentation and straining steps, Panama attribution; https://www.laestrella.com.pa/opinion/columnistas/lexico-panameno-relacionado-con-refrescos-panes-postres-y-dulces-ED21005177 — maize, milk and sugar drink.
- Unsure: The type of milk is not stated; the page's image filename refers to "leche agria" (sour milk), which is not confirmed in the text.

## Enyucado (enyucado; cassava cake with coconut, butter and cheese)
- Animal(s): cow (butter; milk optional; cheese queso blanco)
- Kind: dessert / cassava cake with dairy
- What it is: A cassava (yuca) cake with coconut, sugar and anise, made with butter and cheese.
- How it is made: Base of yuca, coconut, sugar, butter, anise, a pinch of salt and cheese. Some cooks use the coconut's milk or plain cow's milk instead of grated coconut; some add egg yolks or wheat flour. Steps are not described.
- Where / who: Panama and Costa Rica (with the Colombian Caribbean), per the article; in Panama the cheese is queso blanco nacional.
- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enyucado — ingredients, optional cow's milk, queso blanco nacional in Panama, Panama attribution.
- Unsure: The cheese itself is a cheese-file item (queso blanco); it is listed here only as an ingredient of the dessert.
