## Bovški sir (Bovec cheese; bovški sir, Formaggio di Plezzo vero)
- Animal(s): Sheep (native Bovec sheep and crosses); may contain up to 20% goat or cow milk
- Kind: hard, full-fat, pressed cheese
- What it is: Sheep-milk cheese from the Bovec area in the upper Soča valley; compact, elastic, aromatic and slightly piquant.
- How it is made: raw sheep milk; traditionally made on high-mountain pastures (planšarije) and on farms; ripens 60–90 days, or up to two years for grating. Heating, rennet, cutting and pressing steps not given in sources.
- Texture / ripening: compact, elastic, dense; 60–90 days (sl wiki), 1–3 months per variant
- Where / who: Bovec municipality, Goriška region; the first record is a 1756 price list in Udine. Protected designation of origin (PDO), registered 2012, on the market.
- Sources: https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MKGP/PODROCJA/HRANA/SHEME-KAKOVOSTI/PUBLIKACIJA/Protected_Slovenian_Agricultural_Products_and_Foodstuffs_2021.pdf — milk rule, history, PDO, region; https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bovški_sir — ripening, production places, history
- Unsure: The sl wiki says goat or cow milk is up to 20%; the booklet agrees at 20%.

## Tolminc (tolminski sir; Formaggio di Tolmino)
- Animal(s): Cow (raw or thermised cow milk; industrial versions use heat-treated milk)
- Kind: hard, full-fat, pressed cheese
- What it is: Round hard cow-milk cheese from the Upper Soča valley (Zgornje Posočje), named after the town of Tolmin; smooth straw-coloured rind, occasional lentil- or pea-sized eyes, sweet and piquant.
- How it is made: raw or thermised cow milk within the defined area; technology based on the Italian Montasio; ripens at least 60 days, sometimes a year or more (1–15 months by variant). Heating, rennet, cutting and pressing not described.
- Texture / ripening: pliable yellow interior; at least 60 days
- Where / who: Tolmin, Kobarid and Bovec area; Zgornje Posočje; traditionally made by local farmers. PDO (registered 2012), EU PDO logo and national quality symbol.
- Sources: https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MKGP/PODROCJA/HRANA/SHEME-KAKOVOSTI/PUBLIKACIJA/Protected_Slovenian_Agricultural_Products_and_Foodstuffs_2021.pdf — raw/thermised milk, history, PDO; https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolminc — Montasio basis, ripening, PDO status; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenian_cuisine — cow milk (description)
- Unsure: The en wiki list says Tolminc may only be made in the northernmost part of the valley; the booklet says the broader Zgornje Posočje.

## Nanoški sir (Nanos cheese; nanoški sir)
- Animal(s): Cow (historically sheep; now mainly cow milk)
- Kind: hard, full-fat, pressed cheese
- What it is: Yellow, piquant hard cheese named after the Nanos plateau, produced since the 16th century.
- How it is made: milk is high in beta-carotene, which gives the intense yellow colour; the piquant flavour develops after two months of ripening. Heating, rennet and pressing steps not given.
- Texture / ripening: small pea-sized holes; about two months ripening
- Where / who: Nanos plateau, Goriška region. PDO, registered 2011 (booklet lists it as not currently on the market).
- Sources: https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MKGP/PODROCJA/HRANA/SHEME-KAKOVOSTI/PUBLIKACIJA/Protected_Slovenian_Agricultural_Products_and_Foodstuffs_2021.pdf — history, colour, ripening, PDO; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenian_cuisine — hard cow-milk cheese, pea-sized holes
- Unsure: Milk type differs between sources: sheep historically, cow now.

## Mohant (mohant cheese)
- Animal(s): Cow (native Bohinj Cika; other cattle breeds allowed)
- Kind: semi-soft, washed or smear-ripened, cloth-moulded, pressed
- What it is: Bohinj cheese with a whitish-yellow paste, distinctive pungent smell and taste; sometimes bitter after long ripening.
- How it is made: milk warmed to 32–35 °C; calf-stomach rennet, coagulation 20–40 min; curd cut to wheat-grain size, lightly reheated; hand-shaped in cloth-lined moulds, pressed; turned and salted for 1–2 days; dried 2–3 days on shelves; pressed under weights in wooden form; ripened at 20–25 °C for 4–6 weeks (spring) to up to 3 months (winter).
- Texture / ripening: paste uniform, smooth, partly elastic, from hard-to-spread to spreadable; ripened over two months
- Where / who: Bohinj, Gorenjska region; traditional farms away from main milk routes. PDO (EU, registered 2013; production rules since 2004), with national "Slovenska kakovost" mark.
- Sources: https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MKGP/PODROCJA/HRANA/SHEME-KAKOVOSTI/PUBLIKACIJA/Protected_Slovenian_Agricultural_Products_and_Foodstuffs_2021.pdf — texture, ripening, PDO, region; https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohant — milk, heating, rennet, cutting, pressing, ripening steps
- Unsure: Described as semi-soft (sl wiki) and as soft (en wiki list summary); the booklet describes it as hard-to-spreadable.

## Trnič
- Animal(s): Cow (domestic cattle; the page does not say how much is cow milk)
- Kind: hard, dried, smoked, hand-shaped curd cheese
- What it is: Traditional shepherds' cheese shaped like a squat onion, with decorative stamped patterns; very hard, dried and lightly smoked.
- How it is made: skimmed sour milk heated more than usual to form a dry curd; curd drained, heavily salted and mixed with cream; shaped by hand into onion-like balls, flat on the bottom, pointed on top; stamped with carved wooden stamps (pisave); dried on a shelf over the hearth and smoked lightly with beech wood; loses half its mass in about 14 days. Rennet and pressing not mentioned.
- Texture / ripening: very hard and dried; ripening through drying and smoking
- Where / who: Velika planina above Kamnik, also Mala planina and Gojška planina; made by shepherds. No protected status mentioned.
- Sources: https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trnič — milk, method, drying, smoking, region; https://dediscina.zrc-sazu.si/?p=3651 — listed as an alpine heritage cheese (page unavailable at fetch; name-only evidence)
- Unsure: Milk type is only stated as "domestic cattle"; the ZRC SAZU source could not be read.

## Poltrdi sir brez konzervansov (poltrdi siri Zelene doline)
- Animal(s): Cow
- Kind: semi-hard cheese
- What it is: Semi-hard cheese made without preservatives.
- How it is made: standard semi-hard method, except the milk is bactofuged (removes about 99–99.5% of bacteria and spores), so no nitrate or other additive is needed to stop blowing.
- Texture / ripening: standard semi-hard
- Where / who: Zelena dolina, Savinjska region. Designation of higher quality, registered 2010, on the market.
- Sources: https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MKGP/PODROCJA/HRANA/SHEME-KAKOVOSTI/PUBLIKACIJA/Protected_Slovenian_Agricultural_Products_and_Foodstuffs_2021.pdf — method, bactofugation, region, registration

## Bohinjski sir (Bohinj cheese)
- Animal(s): Cow (traditionally raw, whole-fat Bohinj cow milk; industrial versions use partly skimmed, heat-treated milk)
- Kind: hard cheese
- What it is: Hard cheese from Bohinj, based on the Swiss Emmental (ementalec) technique.
- How it is made: Traditionally made in copper kettles (so curd grains do not stick to the walls) on some high-mountain pastures; industrially at a dairy in Srednja vas v Bohinju. Yield 8–9 kg cheese per 100 kg milk. Ripened at least 2 months, depending on variant. Rennet, pressing and temperatures not stated.
- Where / who: Bohinj, Gorenjska region; no protected status mentioned in the article.
- Sources: https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir — listed among hard cheeses (water content 35–45% of the non-fat part); https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohinjski_sir — raw whole cow milk, copper kettles, Emmental technique, yield, ripening, Srednja vas dairy
- Unsure: Not checked against the protected-products booklet; the article does not say whether it is the same product as Mohant (a Bohinj cheese with its own PDO).

## Kraški ovčji sir (Karst sheep cheese)
- Animal(s): Sheep (raw, unpasteurised sheep milk)
- Kind: hard cheese
- What it is: Hard sheep-milk cheese from the Karst (Kras) region, with a PDO.
- How it is made: Made on individual Karst farms from raw sheep milk; ripened 60–90 days, or sometimes up to a year (the Karst way of speaking of ripening is "drying" the cheese). Heating, rennet and pressing not described.
- Where / who: Karst region; traditional farm cheese; EU PDO logo and national quality symbol on authentic cheese.
- Sources: https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir — listed among hard cheeses; https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraški_ovčji_sir — raw sheep milk, farm production, ripening, PDO
- Unsure: The infobox gives 2–3 months ripening, which differs from the text's 60–90 days.

## Skuta (true skuta; prava skuta)
- Animal(s): Whey from cow or goat (or sheep) cheesemaking
- Kind: whey cheese (ricotta-like, boiled-down whey)
- What it is: Fresh whey cheese; a by-product of cheesemaking, eaten fresh or warm.
- How it is made: whey mixed with rennet and heated; the mass coagulates and the thick curd rises to the surface and is skimmed off continuously; drained in perforated containers or sieves until it stops dripping; firm skuta turned out onto greased paper and wrapped; softer skuta packed in cups.
- Texture / ripening: fresh, soft; eaten fresh
- Where / who: Slovenia-wide; traditional farm and dairy product
- Sources: https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skuta_(mlečni_izdelek) — whey origin, method, uses; https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir — listed among fresh cheeses (85–87% water)
- Unsure: The source does not mention acid coagulation.

## Skutni sir (curd cheese)
- Animal(s): Cow or goat (some milk added to the whey)
- Kind: fresh curd cheese
- What it is: Mild, grainy curd cheese, sometimes with cream, sold in jars; the sibling of prava skuta.
- How it is made: whey and milk mixture, with rennet, heated and drained as for skuta; milk-to-whey ratio sets the firmness and shelf life.
- Texture / ripening: mild and grainy; fresh
- Where / who: Slovenia-wide; industrial and farm
- Sources: https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skuta_(mlečni_izdelek) — description, use

## Liptovski sir (Liptauer-style spread)
- Animal(s): Cow or goat (from skuta)
- Kind: spread, ripened curd cheese
- What it is: Spreadable skuta mix with sour cream, paprika, caraway and black pepper, ripened in a cool place.
- How it is made: skuta mixed with sour cream, paprika, caraway and black pepper, then allowed to ripen
- Texture / ripening: soft, spreadable; short ripening
- Where / who: Slovenia as a home dish; the name comes from the Liptov region, shared with Slovakia
- Sources: https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skuta_(mlečni_izdelek) — spread recipe and use
- Unsure: Recipe-level description only; the origin of the name is not confirmed from Slovenian sources.
