## Scharfe Maxx (Scharfe Maxx)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: hard, Swiss-type / Alpine-style cheese
- What it is: a hard Swiss cheese from the canton of Thurgau, with a sharp flavour that resembles Appenzeller but is creamier.
- How it is made: made from thermized cow's milk (gently heated, not pasteurised); aged about 6 to 9 months. Coagulant, cutting, pressing and salting steps not given in the source.
- Texture / ripening: aged 6 to 9 months, sharp; creamier than Appenzeller.
- Where / who: produced at the Studer cheesery in Hatswil (part of Hefenhofen, near Lake Constance), run by the Studer family for three generations. Industrial vs farm not stated.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scharfe_Maxx — the cheese's origin, milk, thermisation, ageing, producer and location.
- Unsure: the source is a short encyclopedia article; the Hatswil location is as stated there and was not checked against a second source.

## Emmentaler (Emmental; Emmentaler AOP)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: pressed, cooked hard cheese (Swiss-type, with eyes)
- What it is: a yellow, medium-hard Swiss cheese with holes, originating in the Emme Valley, Switzerland.
- How it is made: not described on the page opened (only origin and region).
- Texture / ripening: not given on the page opened.
- Where / who: its protected-origin (AOP) area includes the canton of Thurgau, alongside Aargau, Bern, Glarus, Luzern, Schwyz, Solothurn, St Gallen, Zug and Zürich, and parts of Fribourg. Protected status: AOP. The Thurgau mention is part of the PDO area; no Thurgau dairy is named.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmental_cheese — origin and the list of cantons in the AOP area, including Thurgau.
- Unsure: this is a multi-region protected cheese; it is included here because Thurgau is inside its PDO area, not because a Thurgau-specific producer was found.
