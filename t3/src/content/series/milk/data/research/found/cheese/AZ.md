## Motal pendiri (motal pendiri; Azerbaijani; Qazax motal, Qarabağ motal)
- Animal(s): sheep or goat, or a mix (Seasoned Traveller); the Azerbaijani Wikipedia motal article does not state the milk.
- Kind: brined/dried crumbly cheese matured in a skin container.
- What it is: A crumbly, salty cheese, stronger than feta, eaten broken into pieces with fresh herbs. The cheese is named after the motal, a container made from a small-horned animal's skin. Motal use is also described for butter and şor.
- How it is made: Made in the mountains and matured inside sheepskin, then hung to dry (Seasoned Traveller). The motal skin is soaked, shaved, salted and dried, softened, turned wool-side in, and tied with rope; the cheese is packed with some fresh milk, dələmə or cream to help it keep (az.wiki Motal). Qazax motal: salted, sun-dried skin, cheese, salted butter or şor packed in, sealed and pressed to exclude air.
- Texture / ripening: crumbly, salty, stronger than feta (Seasoned Traveller); ripening time not stated.
- Where / who: Mountain production (Seasoned Traveller); Qazax motal linked to western Azerbaijan, the other type called Qarabağ motal; valued by Azerbaijanis and other South Caucasus peoples (az.wiki Motal).
- Sources: https://seasonedtraveller.com/eat-drink/unmissable-dishes-azerbaijan — milk, mountain making, skin ripening, texture; https://az.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motal — motal container method, Qazax and Qarabağ variants.
- Unsure: Seasoned Traveller says sheep or goat; the Azerbaijani article is silent on milk. Ripening time not found.

## Qurut (qurut, dried curd; Azerbaijani)
- Animal(s): cultured milk; the milk type is not stated (yogurt or ayran base).
- Kind: dried, salted curd.
- What it is: A dried, salted curd made from strained cultured milk, used as a seasoning and as a cheese-like ingredient.
- How it is made: Yogurt or ayran is strained in a cloth bag until thick, salted, kneaded into a dough-like mass, shaped into balls and dried (az.wiki Qurut and az.wiki Azərbaycan mətbəxi). Drying time not stated.
- Texture / ripening: dried and hard; keeps for several years (az.wiki mətbəxi).
- Where / who: South Caucasus, western Azerbaijan, Kurdish, Iranian, Afghan and Central Asian cooking; used in xəngəl, əriştə, qurut aşı, kələcoş; Naxçıvan and Ağbaba use noted (az.wiki Qurut).
- Sources: https://az.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qurut — method, uses, regions; https://az.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azərbaycan_mətbəxi — shaping, drying, storage, xəngəl use.
- Unsure: whether it is classed as cheese (the English page does not call it a cheese).

## Şor (şor, salted curd; Azerbaijani)
- Animal(s): buttermilk-based; milk type not stated.
- Kind: salted curd.
- What it is: Salted kəsmik, the curd left after buttermilk (ayran) is strained.
- How it is made: Curd from strained buttermilk, salted (az.wiki Azərbaycan mətbəxi). No ripening or time stated.
- Texture / ripening: not stated.
- Where / who: Motal is used to store şor (az.wiki Motal).
- Sources: https://az.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azərbaycan_mətbəxi — definition; https://az.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motal — şor stored in motal.
- Unsure: the milk type is not stated directly.

## Süzmə (strained curd; Azerbaijani)
- Animal(s): fermented milk (qatıq); buffalo milk makes a fattier süzmə; cow and buffalo milk are the main dairy milks (az.wiki Azərbaycan mətbəxi).
- Kind: fresh strained curd.
- What it is: A soft curd made by straining qatıq.
- How it is made: Strained qatıq (az.wiki Azərbaycan mətbəxi). No time or temperature given.
- Texture / ripening: soft; fresh.
- Where / who: Not stated.
- Sources: https://az.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azərbaycan_mətbəxi — straining from qatıq, fat content from buffalo milk.
- Unsure: produced in the country but no region given.

## Sulguni (Sulquni; Georgian; listed as popular in Azerbaijan)
- Animal(s): not stated for Azerbaijan; Georgian entry gives no milk type.
- Kind: brined, elastic cheese.
- What it is: A Georgian cheese from Samegrelo, named in the Azerbaijani popular cheese list.
- How it is made: Pickled, giving a sour, moderately salty taste and elastic texture; often deep-fried and served in wedges (en.wiki List of cheeses, Georgia row).
- Texture / ripening: elastic, pickled.
- Where / who: Samegrelo, Georgia; in Azerbaijan the name appears only in the az.wiki list.
- Sources: https://az.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pendir — listed as popular in Azerbaijan; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cheeses — Georgian origin and method.
- Unsure: whether it is made in Azerbaijan or only sold there; the cited list does not say.
