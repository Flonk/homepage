## Quesillo (quesillo; queso; Spanish)
- Animal(s): Not stated on the source page
- Kind: Not stated (fresh cheese is an inference from the name only; unverified)
- What it is: A cheese served in Bolivian valley cooking, paired with motes (dried, boiled grains). The source describes cheese as a major protein source in those valleys.
- How it is made: Not stated in any source opened.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated.
- Where / who: Bolivian valleys (per the Spanish Wikipedia gastronomy article). Farm or industrial status not stated.
- Sources: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronomía_de_Bolivia — names quesillo with motes and describes cheese as a protein source in the valleys.
- Unsure: Only a dish-level mention, not a description of the cheese. Quesillo is also a name for cheeses in other Latin American countries, so the Bolivian product is not confirmed beyond this one mention. Probably needs a source that covers the cheese itself. Second pass: the Spanish Wikipedia Quesillo article does not mention Bolivia (its only Bolivian link, "Quesillo cochabambino", is a red link) and describes a generic Latin American cheese, so it does not confirm the Bolivian product's animal or method.
