## Imsil cheese (임실치즈; Imsil cheese)
- Animal(s): Cow (nearby livestock farms supply the milk)
- Kind: Industrial/factory cheese (type not stated in source)
- What it is: The cheese produced by the Imsil Cheese Factory in Imsil County, North Jeolla. The source calls it the first domestic Korean cheese, made in Imsil; the factory also makes yogurt.
- How it is made: Not stated in the source.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated.
- Where / who: Imsil County. Began with a farmers' milk cooperative started in the mid-1950s by a Belgian Catholic priest (Korean name Ji Jeong-hwan) in the postwar years. Protected status: none mentioned.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imsil — Imsil Cheese Village, factory history, cheese named after the county, pizza use since 2004; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cheeses — Imsil listed under Korea
- Unsure: The English Wikipedia article is flagged for needing citations and gives 1950s/1966 dates that do not fully match the Korean page. Not confirmed as a traditional cheese.

## Cheddar (Korean-made)
- Animal(s): Cow (not stated for Korean production)
- Kind: Hard pressed cheese (industrial; Korean cheddar)
- What it is: Cheddar made in Korea from 1970, after a cheesemaker studied in Europe for about three months.
- How it is made: Not described for the Korean version.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated.
- Where / who: South Korea, industrial. Seoul Milk Cooperative produced cheddar from 1972 per the source. The source also mentions a contract with a major hotel that led to bulk supply.
- Sources: https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/치즈 — Ji Jeong-hwan's 1970 cheddar, Seoul Milk Cooperative 1972, bulk contract
- Unsure: The page describes cheddar's English origin, not Korean methods. The milk and method are not given.

- Animal(s): Not stated (cow milk is likely but not confirmed by the source)
- Kind: Processed/melting cheese (Korean category name, not a single named cheese)
- What it is: A cheese produced by several Korean companies after pizza became popular in Korea.
- How it is made: Not described.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated.
- Where / who: South Korea, industrial. Demand grew with pizza.
- Sources: https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/치즈 — pizza cheese production by several companies
- Unsure: This is a product category, not a traditional cheese; included because the country's cheese production includes it.
