## Yogur bebible (drinkable yogurt, fruit flavours)
- Animal(s): cow (presumed; not stated)
- Kind: fermented milk drink
- What it is: a yogurt sold as a drink in many fruit flavours, widely drunk in Ecuador.
- How it is made: not found in the sources read.
- Where / who: industrial and supermarket product, nationwide.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_cuisine — says drinkable yogurt in many fruit flavours is popular and is often drunk with pan de yuca.
- Unsure: animal milk and process not stated; the source is a general cuisine article.

## Cuajada / quesillo (curd dessert with honey, Loja)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: fresh curd / dessert
- What it is: a curd served as a dessert with honey (miel) in the southern Loja Province; the Wikipedia cuisine page calls it "quesillo" or "cuajada".
- How it is made: not found in the sources read.
- Where / who: southern Sierra (Loja Province), home cooking.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_cuisine — Loja dessert "miel con quesillo, or 'cuajada'"; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ecuadorian_dishes_and_foods — lists cuajada; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronom%C3%ADa_de_Ecuador — lists cuajada among Sierra dishes.
- Unsure: "quesillo" can also mean a cheese, which this project excludes; the dessert here is treated as a curd, not a cheese.

## Arroz con leche (rice pudding)
- Animal(s): cow milk
- Kind: milk dessert
- What it is: rice pudding of cooked rice, milk, cinnamon or nutmeg and raisins; one of the most common home and restaurant desserts.
- How it is made: cooked rice simmered with milk, flavoured with cinnamon or nutmeg, with raisins.
- Where / who: nationwide, home and restaurant.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_cuisine — description of arroz con leche; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronom%C3%ADa_de_Ecuador — listed among desserts.

## Natilla (custard)
- Animal(s): cow milk (presumed)
- Kind: milk custard / dessert
- What it is: a thick custard dessert listed among Ecuadorian dishes.
- How it is made (general, not Ecuador-specific): milk with egg yolks, sugar and vanilla or lemon, thickened gently and often with cornstarch.
- Where / who: nationwide (listed); Ecuador-specific details not checked.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Ecuadorian_dishes_and_foods — lists natilla; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natilla — mentions Ecuador (context not checked); https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastronom%C3%ADa_de_Ecuador — lists natilla; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natilla — general recipe (milk, egg yolks, sugar, vanilla or lemon; cornstarch to thicken).
- Unsure: the Ecuadorian recipe is not confirmed; the entry rests on the lists and an unread mention.

## Dulce de leche (in alfajores and other sweets)
- Animal(s): cow milk
- Kind: milk caramel / milk jam
- What it is: caramelised sweetened milk. Used in Ecuador as a filling, for example in alfajores.
- How it is made: not read here.
- Where / who: industrial and home use, nationwide.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulce_de_leche — notes its use in Bolivia, Ecuador and Peru; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecuadorian_cuisine — alfajores filled with dulce de leche.
- Unsure: the product is shared with neighbouring countries; Ecuador-specific production not described.

## Raw (unpasteurised) cow milk
- Animal(s): cow (zebu/crossbred dairy herds)
- Kind: fresh/raw milk
- What it is: milk sold and used raw, from small and mid-size farms and informal channels.
- How it is made: not a process; milk is taken from farm barrels and sold raw.
- Where / who: Pastaza Province (Amazon) and Pichincha and Manabi provinces; farm and informal sale.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13203744/fullTextXML — 127 raw milk samples from dairy farms in Pastaza, 2024; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11816838/fullTextXML — in rural Ecuador raw milk consumption raises E. coli risk, 633 samples from Pichincha and Manabi; dairy production is mainly informal; annual consumption about 110 L per person, including raw milk (from the Ministry of Agriculture, MAG, as cited).
- Unsure: the consumption figure is cited secondhand from the paper.
