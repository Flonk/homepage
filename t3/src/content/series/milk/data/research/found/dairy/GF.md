## Goat's-milk yoghurt (yaourt de lait de chèvre)
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: fermented milk (yoghurt)
- What it is: Yoghurt made from goat's milk on a farm at Montsinéry; the farm's entire milk output goes into yoghurts and cheeses. Cheese is excluded here.
- How it is made: Milk is pasteurised on the farm, then cultured in a yoghurt maker (equipment in an on-farm dairy built in 2011). Temperatures and times are not given.
- Where / who: Montsinéry, a small farm (about 35 goats, roughly 600 L per goat per lactation); home-scale, farm-processed.
- Sources: https://www.reussir.fr/chevre/du-lait-et-du-fromage-de-chevre-sous-lequateur — describes the Montsinéry goat farm, its on-farm dairy equipment, herd size and that its yoghurts and cheeses are sold in Kourou, Cayenne and nearby towns.
- Unsure: The article names the family and farm in summary form; the search snippet called this the only goat dairy in French Guiana, but I did not open a page that confirms that claim.

## Fresh cow's milk and yoghurt (lait frais, yaourt de la Ferme de Lait-Quateur)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh milk; fermented milk (yoghurt)
- What it is: Fresh milk and yoghurt from a dairy farm, "La Ferme de Lait-Quateur", sold in Guianese supermarkets and small shops. A fresh white cheese (fromage blanc) is also named and excluded here.
- How it is made: Not described beyond farm processing of its own milk.
- Where / who: French Guiana, farm dairy; the source describes it as a quality-focused alternative to industrial dairy.
- Sources: https://daaf.guyane.agriculture.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/ficheiaa-guyane-v9-bd_cle44136c.pdf — French Guiana agriculture fact sheet (PDF, text read with pdftotext); says the farm's fresh milk, yoghurts and fresh cheese are widely present in local retail.
- Unsure: Only one source, a government fact sheet; the farm's current status is not confirmed.

## Industrial yoghurt from reconstituted milk powder (Solam, Yoplait franchise)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fermented milk (yoghurt); drink
- What it is: Industrial yoghurts made in French Guiana from milk powder, produced by Solam, a Yoplait franchisee.
- How it is made: The fact sheet says all industrial dairy products are made exclusively from milk powder because local fresh-milk production is low. Process steps are not described.
- Where / who: Industrial, French Guiana.
- Sources: https://daaf.guyane.agriculture.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/ficheiaa-guyane-v9-bd_cle44136c.pdf — states the industrial dairy products are made from milk powder by Solam, a Yoplait franchisee.
- Unsure: Only one source, and the fact sheet is undated in the extracted text.
