- Kind: condensed/evaporated milk (industrial)

- How it is made: not described in the sources read.

## Pain perdu soaked in condensed milk (dish)
- Animal(s): cow (milk in condensed form)
- Kind: sweet dish made with milk; not a dairy product in itself
- What it is: a French-toast-style dish that the chef calls a Gabonese classic and a favourite of children.
- How it is made: bread soaked in eggs, sugar and condensed milk, then coated and cooked (per chef's account, no temperatures or times).
- Where / who: home cooking.
- Sources: https://dairy-products-from-france.com/?p=20446 — the dish and its soaking mixture.
- Unsure: included only as a milk-using dish; milk is not the main ingredient.

## Yoplait yoghurt, "Dahi" (Yoplait brand)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: yoghurt (industrial, packaged)
- What it is: Yoplait-brand yoghurt sold in Gabon. The national food safety agency AGASA withdrew a Yoplait "Dahi" batch (lot 160) from the market.
- How it is made: not described in the sources read. The recall article says inspectors found excess water in lot 160 and that the factory had no self-checks on its preparation tanks after washing and rinsing, so the production line was suspended and the plant temporarily closed.
- Where / who: industrial, retail. The market report calls Yoplait the most scanned yoghurt and sour milk brand in Gabon.
- Sources: https://gabonmediatime.com/agasa-des-produits-dahi-retires-du-marche-pour-defaut-de-qualite/ — the Dahi lot 160 recall, article byline 29 June 2023; https://sagaciresearch.com/?p=42318 — Yoplait the most scanned yoghurt and sour milk brand in Gabon, 115 g most common pack size, cup/pot most frequent format, 87 SKUs scanned.
- Unsure: the recall article does not define "dahi". The 115 g and cup/pot figures describe the whole yoghurt category, not Yoplait alone. The recall is from 2023. The plant's location is not stated in the excerpt read.

## Frozen yoghurt ice cream (Yogurt Factory, Libreville)
- Animal(s): cow (milk source not stated)
- Kind: frozen dessert, yoghurt-based
- What it is: an ice cream made from yoghurt, sold by the Yogurt Factory chain in Libreville and customisable with toppings. Example mix: plain yoghurt with mango, coconut and salted butter caramel.
- How it is made: not described.
- Where / who: a dessert shop inside the Géant Casino Mbolo galleries in Libreville; it opened on Wednesday 10 November (article dated 16 November 2021).
- Sources: https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-yogurt-factory-ou-quand-le-dessert-est-sublime/ — the product, the example flavour and the shop's location.
- Unsure: a single shop's product, not a standard market product. The source does not say whether the yoghurt base is local.
