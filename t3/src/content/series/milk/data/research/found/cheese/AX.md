## Mattas farm cheese (Mattas gårdsmejeri ost; Mattas Gårdsmejeri, Sund)
- Animal(s): cow (cow's milk from two nearby farms, per the dairy's own page)
- Kind: cheese (type not stated)
- What it is: A cheese made by the small Mattas farm dairy in Sund, northern Åland, alongside its ice cream and yoghurt. The dairy names its products after its first cows.
- How it is made: The dairy turns the milk into cheese on the farm; no method, rennet, ripening or temperature given in the sources read.
- Where / who: Small farm dairy, Sund, northern Åland.
- Sources: https://www.aland.com/en/artikel/delicious-and-inspiring-artisanal-food — "Milk is turned into cheese at Mattas farm dairy"; https://www.mattas.ax/ — the dairy processes cow's milk from two nearby farms into cheese and ice cream.
- Unsure: The type of cheese is not named on any opened page, and "Mattas Tiffany" is named as a product without saying whether it is a cheese or an ice cream. The milk source (two nearby farms) conflicts with the dairy file's note that the farm keeps five cows.
