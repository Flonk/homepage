## Imported milk and milk products (fresh, UHT, powdered, condensed)
- Animal(s): cow (imported; origin not stated)
- Kind: heat-treated and dried milk, supplied by import
- What it is: the milk eaten in Liberia is not made locally; the national agriculture overview says the country has no dairy industry and all milk is imported. This is the only dairy supply statement found.
- How it is made: not described (imported, processed abroad)
- Where / who: nationwide; industrial and supermarket supply, not a traditional product
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture_in_Liberia — "there is no dairy industry, and all milk is imported" (Livestock section); no quantities given; https://frontpageafricaonline.com/u-s-experts-in-liberia-to-launch-first-post-war-modern-dairy-farm-project/ — (29 May 2026) a planned Liberia Dairy Farm project aims to "reduce Liberia's dependence on imported milk and dairy products"; no volumes given
- Unsure: this is an import, not a product made in Liberia. It is kept here because the country eats milk that comes from elsewhere. The pages do not say which forms (fresh, UHT, powdered or condensed) are imported.
