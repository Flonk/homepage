## Queso blanco Panamá (queso blanco Panamá; queso prensado; queso molido)
- Animal(s): cow (the standard says "leche fluida nacional"; species not named)
- Kind: fresh white cheese, unripened
- What it is: The national queso blanco defined in the Panamanian technical standard: the product obtained by separating the whey after coagulation of pasteurised national fluid milk, ready to eat soon after manufacture. The standard names two variants as examples: "molido" (ground) and "prensado" (pressed).
- How it is made: Pasteurised national fluid milk is coagulated, and the whey is separated. Permitted ingredients are a coagulant or rennet, harmless lactic or flavour cultures, food-grade sodium chloride or other salts, and harmless enzymes and flavourings. Cutting, draining and pressing steps are not in the part of the standard read.
- Where / who: Panama; the standard applies to making, handling, distributing and selling it in the Republic of Panama.
- Sources: https://mici.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/RT-DGNTI-XX-2025_Queso-Blanco-Panama-VERIFICADO.docx — definition, raw-milk and ingredient rules, variant examples; https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enyucado — queso blanco nacional used in Panamanian enyucado.
- Unsure: Milk species is not stated. The standard also mentions raw milk of grades A, B and industrial, but the cheese is made from pasteurised milk. Full make steps are not confirmed.
