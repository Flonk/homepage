## Spisestedet Sarfalik restaurant cheeses (Greenlandic cheese, Nuuk; cheese names not given)
- Animal(s): Cow (blue cheese); sheep (second cheese)
- Kind: blue cheese (cow's milk); sheep's milk cheese (kind not stated)
- What it is: The restaurant's first Greenlandic cheeses, "made of organic cow's or sheep's milk". The blue cheese is made with lingonberry and crowberry; the sheep's milk cheese is flavoured with thyme and parsley from Greenland.
- How it is made: Only flavouring steps are given: the sheep's milk cheese is flavoured with thyme and parsley and then dipped in dried angelica; the blue cheese contains lingonberry and crowberry. The curding, ageing and other making steps are not described.
- Where / who: Spisestedet Sarfalik at Hotel Hans Egede, Nuuk; a restaurant cheese, not a dairy's regular product.
- Sources: https://www.four-magazine.com/?p=16660 — restaurant made its first Greenlandic cheese this year from organic cow's or sheep's milk; blue cheese of cow's milk with lingonberry and crowberry; sheep's milk cheese flavoured with thyme and parsley, dipped in dried angelica; restaurant is Spisestedet Sarfalik at Hotel Hans Egede in Nuuk.
- Unsure: The source does not say the milk is Greenlandic, and it does not say the cheese is sold beyond the restaurant. Found on the second pass only.
