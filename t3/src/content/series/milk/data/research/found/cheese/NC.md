## Fresh cheese, 100 % New Caledonian milk (made in Païta)
- Animal(s): not stated in the article seen
- Kind: fresh cheese (fromage frais)
- What it is: Fresh cheeses made by Isabelle Leblanc, a cheesemaker in Païta, from milk the article describes as "lait 100 % calédonien".
- How it is made: Not described in the visible part of the article. It says she works from a new laboratory of about 40 m² next to her house.
- Where / who: Isabelle Leblanc, Païta, New Caledonia. The article says there are two local producers using 100 % local milk, the second not named in the visible text.
- Sources: https://www.lnc.nc/article/nouvelle-caledonie/consommation/le-fromage-du-pays-a-l-assaut-de-nos-assiettes — producer, fresh cheese, 100 % local milk, laboratory; shown date "Samedi 23/05/2020" next to a related item, not clearly the article's date
- Unsure: Paywalled, so the cheese range and the milk's animal are not known. The date is not confirmed.
