## Masala tea (chai; Hindi/Fiji Hindi "chai")
- Animal(s): cow (milk or cream; water buffalo is used in India generally, not stated for Fiji)
- Kind: milk tea / milk drink
- What it is: spiced black tea brewed with milk, drunk daily in many households.
- How it is made: simmer spices in water, add tea and brew, strain, then add milk or cream. A Maharashtrian method heats equal parts water and milk with sugar, grated ginger and tea masala, then adds loose black tea and brings it to a boil.
- Where / who: Indo-Fijian households; the Fijian cuisine article calls it the main drink in many Indo-Fijian households.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fijian_cuisine — masala tea linked as "the main drink in many Indo-Fijian households"; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masala_tea — preparation with milk or cream, Maharashtrian method
- Unsure: the Fiji sourcing is one sentence on the Fijian cuisine article. The preparation is general Indian/Indo-Fijian practice, not a Fiji-specific recipe.

## Butter (table butter)
- Animal(s): cow (as far as the source says; not specified)
- Kind: butter (spread, cooking)
- What it is: churned cream or milk fat, eaten as a spread and used in cooking.
- How it is made: general method, not Fiji-specific: butter is made by churning milk or cream to separate the fat globules from the buttermilk; churning produces small butter grains.

## Condensed milk (sweetened)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: evaporated/condensed milk
- What it is: milk with water removed, usually sweetened, sold in tins.
- How it is made: general method, not Fiji-specific: water is evaporated from the milk and sugar is added until a 9:11 sugar-to-evaporated-milk ratio is reached; the sweetened product is canned.
- Where / who: industrial; tinned pantry staple, and used to flavour a cardamom cake.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fijian_cuisine — listed among tinned pantry goods, and flavouring in the cardamom cake (with cardamom, ginger, coconut milk and sugarcane syrup); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_dairy_products — general definition; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensed_milk — sweetened condensed milk production (general)
- Unsure: the source does not say whether the tin is imported or made locally.

## Barfi (burfi; khoa sweet)
- Animal(s): not stated for Fiji (khoa is milk solids; the source does not name the animal)
- Kind: milk-based sweet, fudge-like
- What it is: a milk-based sweet from the Indian subcontinent with a fudge-like consistency.
- How it is made: khoa (milk solids) is mixed with granulated sugar and cooked until it thickens to a fudge-like consistency; may be decorated with coconut, ground nuts or powdered milk, and flavoured with fruit, nuts, legumes or spices.
- Where / who: listed in Wikipedia's "Fijian desserts" category; no Fiji-specific text on the page.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Fijian_desserts — Barfi listed under Fijian desserts; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barfi — milk-based sweet, khoa and sugar method
