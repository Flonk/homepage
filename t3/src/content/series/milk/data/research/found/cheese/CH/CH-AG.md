## Emmentaler (Emmentaler AOP; Emmental, Emmenthaler; "Emmentaler Switzerland")
- Animal(s): cow (raw or heated milk; Swiss Emmentaler AOP requires silage-free raw milk)
- Kind: pressed cooked, hard, with eye holes
- What it is: a yellow, medium-hard Swiss cheese with characteristic holes. Protected designation of origin in Switzerland since 2006 (Swiss Federal Court ruling), with a premium "Premier Cru" grade.
- How it is made: raw or pasteurised milk (pasteurised at 72–75 °C outside the Swiss, Allgäu and Bregenzerwald production rules); rennet and bacterial cultures. The steps (cutting, cooking, pressing, salting) are not described in the source read.
- Texture / ripening: ripened at least 4 months under AOP; Premier Cru at least 14 months in a humid cellar; raw-milk versions can be kept 18 or more months.
- Where / who: the Swiss protected area includes Aargau among the cantons (Aargau, Bern, Glarus, Luzern, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Zug, Zürich, Freiburg). Dairies across the cantons, industrial and farm.
- Sources: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_von_Käsesorten_aus_der_Schweiz — lists Aargau among the cantons of Emmentaler's origin; https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmentaler — AOP status, milk requirements, ripening times.
- Unsure: the Emmentaler protected area names Aargau, but the source does not confirm that Aargau dairies make it in quantity; it is included here because the area is protected and listed as the origin of the cheese.
