## Goat's milk on Miquelon-Langlade (lait de chèvre; chèvre)
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: fresh milk, made into local dairy and cheese
- What it is: Goat milk produced by small farms on Miquelon-Langlade, the commune on the Miquelon island group. The farming is described as serving the local population and passing sailors, and as including dairy and cheese production for the local market and for Saint-Pierre.
- How it is made: Not found in the sources opened. The goat farm Saveurs Fermières offers tastings of its farm products and teaches cheesemaking, but no method is given.
- Where / who: Miquelon-Langlade, Saint Pierre and Miquelon; home and small farm production.
- Sources: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miquelon-Langlade — Agriculture section: goat and sheep rearing, and dairy and cheese production for the local market; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miquelon-Langlade — goat farm Saveurs Fermières teaches goats and cheesemaking, with tastings of local farm products
- Unsure: The sources name goat rearing and "production laitière et fromagère" without stating the milk type for each; goat milk is inferred from the goat rearing and the goat farm. A Réussir report on a farm producing goat-milk cheese is unconfirmed (see leads).
