## Feta (φέτα; Greek name only in local script)
- Animal(s): sheep, with up to 30% goat
- Kind: brined
- What it is: The protected brined white cheese of mainland Greece; its PDO area includes Macedonia and Thrace, so the region is inside it.
- How it is made: Pasteurised milk is cooled to about 35 °C; a starter may be added for acidity and flavour; rennet is added and the curd is cut and drained in moulds or cloth bags; about 3% salt is added after several hours; salted curds rest for several days; the cheese ages in about 7% brine for several weeks at room temperature, then at least 2 months chilled. Raw milk is also allowed.
- Texture / ripening: Brine-ripened; ripening at least about 2 months after brining (chilled).
- Where / who: PDO. The PDO area is named as mainland Greece and Lesbos prefecture on en.wikipedia; el.wikipedia names Macedonia and Thrace among its areas. Eastern Macedonia is not named separately.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feta — PDO area, milk split, production steps; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Φέτα_(τυρί) — list of PDO regions incl. Macedonia and Thrace; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Μακεδονική_κουζίνα — feta named among Macedonian cheeses
- Unsure: The region's own boundary is not stated in these sources; the entry relies on the PDO covering "Macedonia" and "Thrace" as whole regions.

## Kasseri (Κασέρι; Kaseri)
- Animal(s): sheep, with up to 20% goat
- Kind: pasta filata (stretched-curd), hard
- What it is: A protected stretched-curd cheese; its PDO names Xanthi, which lies in this region, as one of its production areas.
- How it is made: Milk heated to about 36 °C, rennet added; curd cut to maize-cob-sized pieces and cooked at 38–40 °C while stirred; drained, hand-ground, bound in cloth under a small weight and left to ferment to about pH 5.2; sliced and dipped in 70–80 °C water, kneaded into a stretchy mass and spun into a thread at least 1 m long; salted, moulded for 2–3 days, then aged at least 3 months at 18 °C. The page gives no brine. (Other source: milk must be curdled within 48 h of milking, raw or pasteurised; usually shaped into cylinders about 30 cm across dipped in wax.)
- Texture / ripening: Aged at least 3 months; sold as cylinders or loaves.
- Where / who: PDO. Named production areas: Thessaly, Macedonia, Lesbos and Xanthi (en.wikipedia and el.wikipedia). Xanthi is in Anatolikí Makedonía kai Thráki.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasseri — PDO areas incl. Xanthi, milk, steps, ageing; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Κασέρι — PDO areas, pasta filata method, ageing, wax; https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Μακεδονική_κουζίνα — kasseri named with Xanthi
- Unsure: Thrace and Drama are not named in the sources; Macedonia and Xanthi are the stated areas.
