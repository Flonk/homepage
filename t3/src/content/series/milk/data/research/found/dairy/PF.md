## Fresh cow's milk (lait de vache frais; local dairy in Tahiti)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh/raw milk
- What it is: Fresh cow's milk produced locally and drunk, reported in the mid-1960s to mid-1970s mainly in Tahiti and, less often, Raiatea in the Society Islands. Not reported in other archipelagos.
- How it is made: not described in the source; only consumption is reported.
- Where / who: Tahiti and Raiatea (Society Islands). Children in Tahiti: about 6–32% by age group (highest at 4–6 years); about 30% of pregnant and breastfeeding women in Tahiti drank it, around 150–170 mL/day.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7173359/fullTextXML — 1960s–70s focus-group and case-control dietary survey (Tahiti, Raiatea) reporting fresh cow's milk consumption, the main iodine-131 exposure source in that study
- Unsure: The source notes the 1985 Grouzelle survey did not separate locally produced milk from milk reconstituted from imported powder, so the local share is unclear.

## Reconstituted milk from imported powder
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: milk powder (reconstituted)
- What it is: Milk made by mixing imported powder with water, used as a substitute for fresh milk in the period studied.
- How it is made: reconstituted from imported powder with water; no ratios or steps given.
- Where / who: Used in French Polynesia; the source does not say which islands or groups. Not a separate product named locally.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7173359/fullTextXML — says a 1985 survey's milk figures may include milk reconstituted from imported powder, so such milk was in use
- Unsure: Only mentioned as a possibility in one source; not confirmed as a separate local product.

## Local yoghurt (yaourt; made by a factory in Tahiti, name not given)
- Animal(s): cow (fresh milk supply)
- Kind: yoghurt, factory-made
- What it is: Yoghurt made by a local yogurt factory in Tahiti, which takes most of the small quantity of fresh milk produced on the island.
- How it is made: not described in the source.
- Where / who: Tahiti (Society Islands).
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture_in_French_Polynesia — says Tahiti produces a small quantity of fresh milk, mostly for the local yogurt factory, while most people drink imported UHT and powdered milk
- Unsure: The Wikipedia sentence is undated and gives no factory name, output or how the yoghurt is made.
