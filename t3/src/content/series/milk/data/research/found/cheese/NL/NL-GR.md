## Kievitskaas (De Kievit, Grijpskerk; kievitskaas)
- Animal(s): cow (dairy factory; inferred, not stated)
- Kind: factory cheese (kaasfabriek); type not stated
- What it is: A well-known product of De Kievit, a dairy at Grijpskerk, Groningen province, operating 1888–1969. The article calls it "kievitskaas" and says the factory was mainly a cheese plant by 1968.
- How it is made: Not described in the source.
- Texture / ripening: Not described.
- Where / who: De Kievit dairy, Grijpskerk, Groningen; a farmers' co-operative dairy that later became the province's price leader.
- Sources: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Kievit_(zuivelfabriek) — names "kievitskaas" as a well-known product and says the factory was mainly a cheese plant in 1968; also butter "Grijpskerk", milk powder, condensed milk.
- Unsure: The article does not say what kind of cheese kievitskaas is or how it was made; whether it was made only at this dairy is not stated.
