## Garis / shubat (camel fermented milk; gariss; other names not confirmed)
- Animal(s): camel
- Kind: fermented milk (sour milk, traditional)
- What it is: a traditional fermented camel milk, documented in pastoral communities in Sudan. The review lists it among the "shubat-type" fermented camel milk products of Sudan.
- How it is made: spontaneous fermentation of raw camel milk, as described in general for the Horn of Africa in the review; no Sudan-specific steps, temperatures or times were found in the source I opened. Second pass: the table of PMC12907412 gives "Gariss (Sudan)" as spontaneous fermentation at pH 3.4–3.7 and 30–37 °C, ambient, with no starter, giving a liquid, slightly thick product (citing Hamed et al. 2024 and Shori 2017).
- Where / who: pastoral and agro-pastoral households, traditional household consumption; minimal documented industrial processing.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13172142/fullTextXML (full text of PMC13172142, Mahdi Hassan et al. 2026, "Camel milk in the horn of Africa", Food Sci Anim Resour 46(1)) — the review's Sudan row in its product table lists raw and shubat-type fermented camel milk, citing Osman et al. and Hassan and Elzubar (2006); the review also names "Garis" as a Sudanese camel fermented milk in its reference list; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12907412/fullTextXML (Ali et al. 2026, "Processing difficulties and biochemical barriers in camel milk fermentation", Front Microbiol, PMC12907412) — product table row "Gariss (Sudan)", and the "Shubat (Kazakhstan)" row, which lists the two as separate products.
- Unsure: the review names "garis" and "shubat" as separate items in its Sudan row, and PMC12907412 places shubat in Kazakhstan, not Sudan; the Sudan entry is probably Garis/gariss only. The reference-list titles on Garis are unconfirmed (not opened).

## Raw camel milk (Sudan)
- Animal(s): camel
- Kind: fresh/raw milk
- What it is: raw camel milk, the basic product of camel herders.
- How it is made: milked raw; no processing. The review notes Sudan's raw-milk production is informal.
- Where / who: pastoral and agro-pastoral herders; informal production (the review cites a Khartoum State study of informal raw camel milk production).
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13172142/fullTextXML — Sudan row of the product table ("raw milk"), and the Sudan camel production passages.
- Unsure: none.
