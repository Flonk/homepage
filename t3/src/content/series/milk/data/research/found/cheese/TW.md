## Taiwanese cheese foam (nǎi gài, 奶蓋; powdered-cheese foam)
- Animal(s): cow (dairy milk and cream; cream cheese in later versions)
- Kind: cheese-based topping, not a cheese in the strict sense (unsure)
- What it is: a salty-tangy foam served on cold tea, which began at Taiwanese night markets around 2010.
- How it is made: powdered cheese and salt blended with whipping cream and milk, and whipped into a foam over cold tea. Source gives no temperatures or times.
- Texture / ripening: foamy, tangy topping; not ripened.
- Where / who: Taiwan night-market vendors around 2010; the later cream-cheese version was created in Guangdong, China (about 2012), not in Taiwan.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheese_tea — describes the early Taiwanese powdered-cheese version and the later Hey Tea cream-cheese version.
- Unsure: it is a topping, not a cheese made from curd; included only because it is a cheese-based product whose origin is Taiwanese. Whether it belongs in a cheese list is for the editor to decide.
