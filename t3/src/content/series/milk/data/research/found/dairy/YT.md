## Fresh cow's milk (lait de vache, raw; Mahoran local name not found)
- Animal(s): cattle (cow); dairy herds are cross-bred cows
- Kind: fresh milk, sold raw (no local processing)
- What it is: the only milk produced on Mayotte; about 22% of the bovine herd is kept for milk
- How it is made: not described beyond milking; no milk processing plant exists on the island
- Where / who: about 2,200 dairy cattle; average yield rose from about 1,000–1,500 to 2,500 litres per cow per year; sold at about €4 per litre; larger farms sell fresh milk and meat regularly; estimated consumption 35–50 litres per resident per year
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7448016/fullTextXML — EFSA report "Rift Valley Fever: risk of persistence, spread and impact in Mayotte" (2020), section 3.1.3: milk is only cow milk, 22% of bovine herds for milk, ~2,200 dairy cattle, yield and price, no milk processing plants, consumption estimate; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11639882/fullTextXML — Cabaret et al. 2024, "Family Husbandry in the Tropical Island of Mayotte" (Animals, doi 10.3390/ani14233405), Table 1 and section 3.1: farms keep cattle for milk and meat, and larger farms sell fresh milk
- Unsure: the price and yield figures are from a 2015–2019 study and one educational farm, extrapolated island-wide by EFSA
