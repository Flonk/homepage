## Goat cheese, fresh and aged (fromage de chèvre frais et affiné)
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: fresh and aged cheese
- What it is: Fresh and aged goat cheeses made on a farm at Montsinéry and sold at markets and shops in Kourou, Cayenne and nearby areas.
- How it is made: All of the farm's milk is processed into yoghurts and fresh and aged cheeses; the milk is pasteurised on the farm in a dairy built in 2011 (pasteuriser, yoghurt maker, thermiser, cold rooms). The cheese-making steps and the cheese names are not given.
- Where / who: Odile and Bob Robin, Montsinéry; a herd of 30 to 35 milking goats.
- Sources: https://www.reussir.fr/chevre/du-lait-et-du-fromage-de-chevre-sous-lequateur — the farm restarted cheese-making, all milk goes into yoghurt and fresh and aged cheese, the dairy's equipment, and the markets and shops where cheese is sold.
- Unsure: The article does not name the cheeses or the aging times; it is one source.
