## Jibneh Arabieh (جبنة عربية / jibneh; also "jibne arabieh", Arabic cheese)
- Animal(s): historically goat or sheep (per source); current practice cow (per source)
- Kind: fresh white cheese, soft, open texture (Wikipedia list describes it as similar to feta but less salty)
- What it is: A soft white cheese described as traditional to the Arabian Peninsula and found across the Middle East, particularly popular in the Persian Gulf region.
- How it is made: Not described in the sources opened (no coagulant, cutting, draining, salting or ripening steps found).
- Texture / ripening: Open texture; not dried or aged per the list entry.
- Where / who: Attributed to Bedouins by the Wikipedia article; Oman is not named in any source opened. Saudi Arabia's jibneh is sampled as a quasi-indigenous fermented dairy in the Saudi study (PMC10528461), which supports the Peninsula tradition, but that is Saudi evidence, not Omani.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cheeses — lists Jibneh Arabieh as a traditional Arabian Peninsula cheese, open texture, similar to feta but less salty; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jibneh_Arabieh — traces it to Bedouin goat/sheep milk, now cow, popular in the Persian Gulf region, no Oman mention; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10528461/fullTextXML — jibneh (cheese) as a traditional Arabian dairy product sampled in Saudi markets
- Unsure: Oman is not confirmed as a making or eating region in any opened source. Included because the Arabian Peninsula tradition and the Gulf popularity point toward it. Treat as provisional until an Omani source is found.

## Camel-milk cheese (Rakhyut Cheese Factory; Arabic جبن الإبل)
- Animal(s): camel
- Kind: cheese (camel milk)
- What it is: A camel-milk cheese introduced locally by Rakhyut Cheese Factory, according to a market report on Oman camel-milk products.
- How it is made: Not given; the source has no product detail.
- Where / who: Oman; made by Rakhyut Cheese Factory (industrial or small-scale not stated).
- Sources: https://www.thereportcubes.com/public/report-store/camel-milk-market-oman — names Rakhyut Cheese Factory as introducing camel-milk cheese locally
- Unsure: One sentence on a paid market-report sales page. Cheese type, ripening and method are unconfirmed; check the company's own products if a second source is found.
