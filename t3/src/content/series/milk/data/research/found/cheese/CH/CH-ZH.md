## Emmentaler (Emmentaler AOP; Emmental, Emmentaler)
- Animal(s): cow (raw cow's milk from small rural dairies)
- Kind: hard, round, natural-rind Swiss-type cheese (pressed; the page does not give the cooking or pressing steps)
- What it is: A protected Swiss cheese. Its AOP area covers several cantons, and Zürich is one of them.
- How it is made: Raw cow's milk. Only water, salt, natural starter cultures and rennet may be used; preservatives and GMO ingredients are not allowed. Shaped round, with a natural rind. Aged at least four months in traditional cellars; the infobox gives 2 to 18 months depending on variety. Cooking, pressing and specific temperatures are not stated on the page.
- Texture / ripening: Ripening 2 to 18 months per the infobox; minimum four months under the AOC rules.
- Where / who: AOP area covers Aargau, Bern (excluding Moutier), Glarus, Lucerne, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Zug and Zurich, plus the See and Sense districts of Fribourg. Whether Zürich's own dairies make it is not stated; the page only lists Zürich in the protected area. Protected (AOP).
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmental_(cheese) — protected area cantons (Zürich included), raw cow's milk, permitted ingredients, shape, rind, ripening minimum.
- Unsure: Zürich appears only in the protected-area list. The page does not confirm that the cheese is made in the canton of Zürich in practice, so treat this entry as "in the protected area", not as proof of local production.
