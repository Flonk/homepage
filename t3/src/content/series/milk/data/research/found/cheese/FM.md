## Processed cheese (English "processed cheese")
- Animal(s): not stated in the source
- Kind: processed cheese, packaged or industrial (as recorded)
- What it is: A packaged cheese recorded in the top five dairy foods of Yap children (4 children). It is not in the top five for Chuuk, Kosrae or Pohnpei. The same item is in the top five in several other jurisdictions (American Samoa, Palau, CNMI, Guam, Hawaii and Alaska).
- How it is made: Not described in the source.
- Where / who: Children aged 2–8 in Yap (n=190), 2011–2013 (CHL Program).
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9839993/fullTextXML — Novotny et al. 2023 (CHL Program), Table 3 (top five dairy foods by jurisdiction).
- Unsure: Only four Yap children reported it; the source does not say whether it is imported or made locally.
