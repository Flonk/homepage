## Evaporated milk (evaporated milk, unsweetened canned)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: canned / shelf-stable milk (evaporated, low-fat evaporated)
- What it is: milk concentrated by removing roughly 60% of the water; shelf-stable canned product.
- How it is made: fresh milk is concentrated by evaporation under heat to about 60% water removal, then canned (process per Wikipedia summary).
- Where / who: eaten in TT as a consumer product, listed in a TT food-frequency questionnaire; mostly industrial/imported.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaporated_milk — general definition and process; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11450712/fullTextXML — "low fat evaporated milk, evaporated milk" in the TT food list.
- Unsure: TT consumption confirmed by the food list only; no TT-specific manufacturer found.

## Condensed milk (sweetened condensed milk)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: sweetened milk / sweet
- What it is: sweetened, thickened canned milk, used as a sweetener and an ingredient.
- How it is made: cow's milk from which about 60% of the water is removed, with sugar added (general process, Wikipedia Condensed milk).
- Where / who: used in TT snow cones, in peanut punch, in fresh fruit drinks with milk; industrial (canned) product listed in the TT food list.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago_cuisine — snow cones "often sweetened with condensed milk"; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut_punch — TT peanut punch with milk and sugar; https://punchdrink.com/articles/peanut-punch-caribbean-drink-guinness-rum/ — TT peanut punch with sweetened condensed milk ("Jamaica" and general) and TT cartons; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11450712/fullTextXML — "condensed milk" in the TT food list.
- Unsure: the PunchDrink sentence on condensed milk is attributed to the general recipe, not specifically TT. Process source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensed_milk (general).

## Fresh cow milk (liquid, full cream and low fat)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh / raw-or-pasteurised milk (liquid)

## Yoghurt (all types)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fermented milk
- What it is: fermented milk products, all types listed in the TT food list.
- How it is made: milk is inoculated with bacteria (Lactobacillus delbrueckii subsp. bulgaricus and Streptococcus thermophilus) and kept warm for 4 to 12 hours to ferment (general process, Wikipedia Yogurt).
- Where / who: eaten in TT; mostly commercial yoghurt.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11450712/fullTextXML — "yogurt (all types)" in the TT food list; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogurt — fermentation process (general).
- Unsure: no TT-specific yoghurt brand or traditional homemade yoghurt found; see Dahi entry for the traditional curd.

## Butter (all kinds)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: butter (fat)
- What it is: butter, eaten on bread and with starchy foods; also listed as a fat.
- How it is made: made by churning milk or cream to separate the fat globules from the buttermilk (general process, Wikipedia Butter).
- Where / who: eaten with coconut bake and boiled cassava (breakfast); "butter (all kinds)" in the TT food list; imported and local.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago_cuisine — "coconut bake ... served with ... butter", "boiled cassava with butter"; https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11450712/fullTextXML — "Butter (all kinds)" in the TT food list; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butter — "made by churning milk or cream" (general process).
- Unsure: cheese paste (a cheese-based relish that uses butter) is not counted here.

## Ghee (clarified butter)
- Animal(s): cow, buffalo
- Kind: clarified butter / butter oil
- What it is: milk fat cooked to remove water and solids, used for cooking and in Hindu ritual.
- How it is made: butter is boiled, impurities skimmed off, the clear fat is kept and the residue discarded (Wikipedia Ghee page).
- Where / who: Indo-Trinidadian households and temples; used in Indian sweets commonplace in TT.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghee — preparation and ritual use; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suji_ka_halwa — mohan bhog (suji halwa, made with ghee) known in the Caribbean as parsad offered at pujas; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo%E2%80%93Trinidadians_and_Tobagonians — Hindu festivals and Indian sweets in TT; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadhi — TT kurhi served at poojas and yajnas.
- Unsure: no source opened says ghee is made locally in TT; use in TT is inferred from the Indian sweets and pujas above.

- Animal(s): cow, buffalo

- Where / who: Dahi page lists Trinidad and Tobago among its national cuisines; used in Indo-Trinidadian cooking.

## Khoa (khoya, mawa, milk solids)
- Animal(s): cow, water buffalo
- Kind: dried milk / milk solids (base for sweets)
- What it is: milk reduced to a soft dough-like solid.
- How it is made: whole milk is simmered in a large, shallow iron pan for several hours until about one-fifth of its volume remains.
- Where / who: base for Indian sweets eaten in TT (peda, barfi, gujiya).
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khoa — preparation; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo%E2%80%93Trinidadians_and_Tobagonians — sweets such as pera and gujiya commonplace in TT.
- Unsure: Wikipedia describes khoa as a soft cheese; included here as a milk solid base for sweets, but the cheese researcher may cover it.

## Goat milk (dairy goat sector)
- Animal(s): goat
- Kind: fresh milk (small dairy sector)
- What it is: milk from dairy goats, a small, low-input, small-scale sector.
- How it is made: goats milked on small farms; a TT Dairy Goat Manual covers breeds, milking and herd health.
- Where / who: small-scale producers; imported Alpine, Saanen and Toggenburg breeds; meat goat sector is much larger.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC6668862/fullTextXML — "a small goat milk industry is developing using imported Alpine, Saanen, and Toggenburg breeds"; "dairy goat sector is much smaller than the meat goat sector in Trinidad and Tobago, and is small scale and low input"; Dairy Goat Manual by the TT Goat and Sheep Society.
- Unsure: no output or volume figure found; the source is a review and may be dated.

## Ice cream (homemade and commercial)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: frozen milk dessert
- What it is: frozen dessert, sold on hot days, including homemade flavours.
- How it is made: milk or cream sweetened and flavoured, cooled below freezing and stirred to incorporate air (general process, Wikipedia Ice cream); homemade flavours (barbadine, coconut, soursop) listed in the TT food study; TT recipe not given.
- Where / who: street and shop sale; homemade and commercial.

- Unsure: milk content of the homemade flavours not stated; ice cream assumed to contain milk.

## Kulfi
- Animal(s): cow, buffalo
- Kind: frozen milk dessert (Indian)
- What it is: dense frozen dessert of thickened, sweetened milk, "traditional Indian ice cream".
- How it is made: sweetened, flavoured milk is slow-cooked and stirred until it condenses and thickens, then frozen (Wikipedia Kulfi page).
- Where / who: eaten by locals on hot days in TT.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago_cuisine — "On hot days, locals enjoy ice cream, ... kulfi"; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulfi — preparation.
- Unsure: TT kulfi is mentioned only in the list; no local recipe source opened.

## Peanut punch (milk drink)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: milk drink
- What it is: a Caribbean drink of peanuts or peanut butter with milk, sugar and spices; in TT often sold in cartons and at punch carts.
- How it is made: peanut butter, milk, sugar and sometimes spices, rum sometimes added (Wikipedia Peanut punch: "In Trinidad and Tobago, it is made with peanut butter, milk, sugar and sometimes spices. Rum is sometimes used").
- Where / who: drinks in TT; store-bought 250 ml cartons and punch carts; Guinness-spiked version in Port of Spain.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peanut_punch — TT method; https://punchdrink.com/articles/peanut-punch-caribbean-drink-guinness-rum/ — TT cartons, carts and Guinness-spiked cup; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago_cuisine — peanut punch in the TT drinks list.
- Unsure: whether TT versions always use condensed milk is not stated.

## Ponche de crème (rum and milk punch)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: alcoholic milk drink
- What it is: a rum-based creamy punch, served at rum shops and at Christmas; the TT version of ponche crema, which originated in Venezuela.
- How it is made: milk, eggs, sugar, rum and other flavourings such as vanilla, nutmeg, cinnamon or lemon rind (ponche crema page gives the general recipe).
- Where / who: sold at rum shops; Christmas drink.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago_cuisine — "rum shops ... serving ... ponche-de-crème"; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponche_crema — TT developed "Ponche de Crème"; main ingredients milk, eggs, sugar, rum.
- Unsure: TT-specific recipe not opened; the milk-egg-rum method is from the general ponche crema article.

## Sea moss punch (milk)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: milk drink (blended, milkshake-like)
- What it is: a cooling, spiced sea-moss drink described as Trinidad's answer to the summer milkshake.
- How it is made: sea moss gel blended with milk, sweetener, spices and ice (BBC: "1 cup milk ... blend until smooth").
- Where / who: home-made and sold at public markets and drink vendors in TT.
- Sources: https://www.bbc.com/travel/article/20240802-sea-moss-punch-the-caribbeans-cooling-healthy-elixir-for-summer — TT sea moss punch "milky, spiced drink" made with milk and sweetener; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinidad_and_Tobago_cuisine — sea moss punch in TT beverages.
- Unsure: the BBC recipe is a general version; TT households may use condensed milk or no milk.

## Kheer (rice pudding)
- Animal(s): cow, buffalo
- Kind: milk sweet / pudding
- What it is: sweet pudding of rice (or vermicelli) boiled in milk with sugar, nuts and dried fruit.
- How it is made: boiled with milk, sugar and rice or vermicelli; flavoured with dried fruit and nuts (Wikipedia Kheer page).

## Barfi (burfi)
- Animal(s): cow, buffalo
- Kind: milk sweet (fudge)
- What it is: milk-based fudge-like sweet.
- How it is made: khoa (milk solids) cooked with sugar until it thickens, spread to cool, then cut into squares or diamonds.
- Where / who: popular in TT, brought by indentured workers in the mid-19th century (per Wikipedia citing a Naparima Girls' High School cookbook); eaten at weddings and festivals.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barfi — TT history and khoa method; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo%E2%80%93Trinidadians_and_Tobagonians — burfi among TT sweets.
- Unsure: none.

- Animal(s): cow, buffalo

## Gulab jamun
- Animal(s): cow, buffalo
- Kind: milk sweet (fried milk-solid dumpling in syrup)
- What it is: fried balls of milk solids soaked in sugar syrup.
- How it is made: khoya kneaded with a little flour and baking powder, shaped, deep-fried in ghee or oil, then soaked in syrup.
- Where / who: Indian sweet eaten in TT; TT cuisine category; Indo-Trinidadian festivals and weddings.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulab_jamun — khoya-based recipe; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo%E2%80%93Trinidadians_and_Tobagonians — gulab jamoon in TT sweets.
- Unsure: none.

## Rasgulla (rasgula)
- Animal(s): cow, buffalo
- Kind: milk sweet (syrup-filled ball)
- What it is: spongy balls of chhena (milk curd) in light sugar syrup.
- How it is made: chhena dough shaped into balls and cooked in sugar syrup until the syrup permeates them.
- Where / who: eaten in TT as one of the Indian sweets; Indo-Caribbean cuisine.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasgulla — chhena dumplings in syrup; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo%E2%80%93Trinidadians_and_Tobagonians — rasgula among TT sweets.
