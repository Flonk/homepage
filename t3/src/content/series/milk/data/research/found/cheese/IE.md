## Cashel Blue (J&L Grubb, Co. Tipperary)
- Animal(s): cow (sister cheese Crozier Blue is sheep)
- Kind: blue, semi-soft
- What it is: hand-made semi-soft blue cheese with a creamy texture and mild, slightly acidic blue veining.
- How it is made: pasteurised milk, blue flecks from Penicillium roqueforti; about half the milk from the Grubbs' own Holstein-Friesian herd, rest from nearby farms. Coagulant, cutting, draining, salting and ripening times not given.
- Texture / ripening: creamy, semi-soft; ripening time not given.
- Where / who: J&L Grubb farm near Fethard, Co. Tipperary; started 1984 in a 90-litre copper brewer's vat. Protected status not mentioned.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cashel_Blue — J&L Grubb, Cashel Blue and Crozier Blue description, milk, mould.
- Unsure: none.

## Dubliner
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: hard, granular, cheddar-style
- What it is: hard, sweet, granular cheese meant to combine mature cheddar sharpness with a buttery sweetness like Parmigiano; may contain calcium lactate crystals.
- How it is made: pasteurised cow's milk, aged 12 months to two years. Coagulant, cutting, pressing and salting not described.
- Texture / ripening: hard and granular; aged 1-2 years.
- Where / who: made by Carbery in Co. Cork; marketed internationally by Ornua (Kerrygold label) since 1996. Developed in 1990 by John Lucey as "Araglen"; commercial production from 1994. Named after Dublin but made in Cork.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubliner_cheese — description, milk, ageing, origin.
- Unsure: none.

## Gubbeen
- Animal(s): cow (own herd of British Friesian, Simmental, Jersey and Kerry cows)
- Kind: surface-ripened, smear-ripened, semi-soft
- What it is: semi-soft cow's-milk cheese with a pink-and-white rind; creamy, with mushroom and nutty notes that deepen with age.
- How it is made: pasteurised milk; starter culture and rennet added in vats, the curd is cut and stirred. Salting not described. Cheese matures in curing rooms and the rind is washed daily.
- Texture / ripening: creamy, smear-ripened; rind washed daily during ripening.
- Where / who: made since 1979 by Tom and Giana Ferguson at Gubbeen Farm near Schull, Co. Cork. Smear bacterium Microbacterium gubbeenense named after study of this cheese. Smoked Gubbeen also listed on the Irish cheese page.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gubbeen_Farmhouse_Cheese — milk, process, rind washing, origin.
- Unsure: none.

## Imokilly Regato
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: hard
- What it is: hard, dry-firm cheese with a piquant, Italian-style flavour and nutty, creamy taste; light golden-creamy colour.
- How it is made: pasteurised cow's milk, high in beta-carotene; makers revived old Irish coagulating methods and developed their own rennet. Cutting, pressing, salting and ripening time not described.
- Protected status: Protected Designation of Origin (PDO) under EU law, 1999.
- Where / who: Imokilly Cheese Company (Dairygold cooperative), Mogeely, Co. Cork; production began 1980.
