## Ko'i lechi (koi lechi; K'oi lechi)
- Animal(s): cow (condensed milk)
- Kind: sweet / dessert
- What it is: A sweet of condensed milk and sugar, served at weddings and other special occasions.
- How it is made: Condensed milk and sugar, cooked into a sweet; no temperatures or times found in the sources opened.
- Where / who: Curaçao, home and celebration use; the name is Papiamento.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curaçao_cuisine — describes it as a condensed milk and sugar sweet served at weddings and special occasions (second pass: "ko'i lechi (condensed milk and sugar sweet)", re-read at offset 100000); https://islaguru.com/articles/local-food-guide-curacao — lists "milk sweets" (K'oi lechi) without preparation detail.
- Unsure: Both sources name condensed milk and sugar as the ingredients, but no method or times have been found in any page opened. The islaguru page could not be re-read on the second pass (404). Milk type not confirmed beyond condensed milk.

## Bolo di manteka (butter cake)
- Animal(s): cow (butter)
- Kind: baked cake (butter-based)
- What it is: A pound-cake-style cake where butter is a main ingredient, with flour, sugar and many eggs in roughly equal weights.
- How it is made: Butter, flour, sugar and eggs in equal pound amounts, baked; no temperatures or times found.
- Where / who: Curaçao, Dutch-influenced cake; home and bakery.
- Sources: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curaçaose_keuken — describes the butter cake with one pound each of its ingredients.
- Unsure: Butter is a main ingredient, but this is a cake and not a dairy product in the strict sense; included because butter is the milk-fat element. Only one source opened.

## Ayaka (Papiamento: ayaka)
- Animal(s): cow (butter, milk, grated cheese in the dough)
- Kind: savoury dish / steamed wrapped cake
- What it is: A savoury corn-dough parcel with grated cheese, butter and milk in the dough, filled with chicken or pork and capers.
- How it is made: Dough of cornmeal, grated cheese, butter, milk, salt, sugar and anise seed, filled, wrapped in banana leaves and boiled, cooled, then reheated before serving.
- Where / who: Curaçao, Christmas and festive food.
- Sources: https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curaçaose_keuken — gives dough ingredients (milk and butter among them) and cooking in banana leaves.
- Unsure: Milk and butter are minor ingredients in the dough; listed because the dough uses them. Only one source opened.

## Pan bati (Papiamento; "smashed bread")
- Animal(s): cow (milk)
- Kind: flatbread / pancake
- What it is: A flat bread or pancake made with sorghum flour, wheat flour and milk; served plain or with sugar or other fillings.
- How it is made: No steps given in the source opened.
- Where / who: The English Wikipedia article places it in Aruba; a Curaçao food guide (princess.com) names pan bati among Curaçao foods without a recipe.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_bati — ingredients (sorghum flour, wheat flour, milk) and Aruban serving; https://www.princess.com/en-uk/blog/curacao-food — names pan bati as a Curaçao food.
- Unsure: Sources place it in Aruba and mention it for Curaçao only in passing; the Curaçao link is not confirmed by a detailed source.
