## Cheddar (Omaruru cheese)
- Animal(s): Cow (milk from cows on a dairy farm near Omaruru, milked daily).
- Kind: Cow-milk cheese; hard or aged status not stated in the source.
- What it is: One of the seven cheeses made by Tinkie Cornelissen in Omaruru.
- How it is made: Not described in the source. The article says only that she started in a home kitchen, later set up a cheese factory, and that "once you have mastered those steps, you can make anything". No second source found.
- Where / who: Tinkie Cornelissen, Omaruru. Sold at the Omaruru and Swakopmund farmers' markets and to the Strand Hotel.
- Sources: https://neweralive.na/?p=130523 — names Cheddar among her seven cheeses and gives the milk source.
- Unsure: The article says its list is "to mention some", so the seven are not fully named.

## Goat-milk white cheese (Otjizondu cheese and yoghurt plant)
- Animal(s): Goat (local people's goat milk).
- Kind: White cheese (fresh or brined status not stated in the source).
- What it is: White cheese made at a cheese and yoghurt plant of 300 litres a day in the rural Otjizondu region, built with Turkish TİKA support to process local goat milk.
- How it is made: The plant has a pasteurisation and standardisation unit; curd, salting and ageing are not described.
- Where / who: TİKA (Turkey) donor project; Otjizondu region, Namibia.
- Sources: https://tika.gov.tr/en/detail-modern_milk_production_facility_in_namibia_by_tika/ — white cheese and yoghurt, goat milk, pasteurisation, 300 litres a day, Otjizondu.
- Unsure: Article dated 18 November 2019; current operation of the plant not checked. Cheese name not given beyond "white cheese".
