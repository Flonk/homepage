## Sky juice (Bahamian sky juice; sky juice)
- Animal(s): cow milk, if the source's condensed/evaporated milk is cow milk (the source does not say which animal)
- Kind: alcoholic milk drink
- What it is: A Bahamian drink whose dairy base is condensed or evaporated milk, mixed with a coconut base and usually gin (rum may be substituted). Optional nutmeg and cinnamon.
- How it is made: Coconut water (or coconut milk) is blended with condensed or evaporated milk and gin. The source gives no quantities, no steps on whether it is served over ice, and no method for the combination beyond the blend.
- Where / who: Bahamas; likely a home and bar drink (the source does not say).
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahamian_cuisine — lists sky juice as a Bahamian drink made from coconut water, condensed milk and gin; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_juice — ingredients (coconut water or coconut milk; condensed or evaporated milk; gin or rum; optional nutmeg and cinnamon), and Bahamian origin
- Unsure: Milk type and exact recipe are not given. "Sky juice" is also a slang term for plain water in Malaysia; that is a different thing and is not part of this entry.
