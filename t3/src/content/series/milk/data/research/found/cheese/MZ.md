## Sandes de queijo (sandes de queijo; Portuguese, "cheese sandwich")
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: toasted sandwich with cheese, street food
- What it is: a toasted cheese sandwich on Portuguese-style pão bread, sold at stalls and described as cheaper than prego rolls.
- How it is made: the source says it is toasted until the bread is crisp and the cheese melted; the cheese type and the method of making it are not given.
- Where / who: sold at street stalls in Mozambique.
- Sources: https://afktravel.com/96684/mouthwatering-mozambique-10-food-to-try/ — "They are commonly sold at stalls and a bit cheaper than prego rolls"; "these just come with cheese, and the sandwich is toasted so the bread gets crispy and the cheese gets melty."
- Unsure: a dish made with cheese, not a cheese type; belongs in a food list rather than the cheese list.

## Mozzarella (mozzarella; imported)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: fresh cheese, pasta-filata style, imported (the trade category is HS 0406.10, fresh and unripened cheese including whey cheese and curd)
- What it is: a commercial cheese sold in Mozambique, recorded as imported trade.
- How it is made: not described in the opened source.
- Where / who: imported into Mozambique; 2023 trade volume 197,736 (unit not stated) and value USD 597,000.
- Sources: https://www.tridge.com/market-overview/mozzarella-cheese/MZ — "Mozzarella Cheese Mozambique Prices & Trade Data", HS 040610 trade table for 2023; no source countries given.
- Unsure: the source is a trade-data page; the presence of mozzarella is shown by import data, not by a retail product page.
