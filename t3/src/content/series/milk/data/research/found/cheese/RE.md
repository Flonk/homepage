## Piton Maïdo (fromage local; the producer's name, Fromages de La Réunion, uses this name)
- Animal(s): Cow (lait de vache), sourced from Réunion
- Kind: Pressed / hard-paste
- What it is: Hard-paste cheese made in La Réunion, named after the Maïdo summit; sold by the producer as one of its best-selling hard cheeses.
- How it is made: Not stated in the sources opened. The producer says it contains lactic and ripening cultures and is stored at +2 to +6 °C.
- Texture / ripening: Not stated beyond "hard paste".
- Where / who: La Réunion; made by Fromageries de Bourbon (Saint-Pierre), a subsidiary of CILAM. No protected status mentioned.
- Sources: https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piton_Maïdo_(fromage) — cow milk, pressed paste, made in La Réunion, producer; https://fromagesdelareunion.re/produits/fromages-a-pate-dure/ — hard-paste, cultures, storage, Réunion milk; https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fromageries_de_Bourbon — listed among the pressed cheeses of the factory
- Unsure: The Wikipedia stub calls it "pâte pressée" in the infobox but its title and the producer's page say "pâte dure" (hard paste). Both are listed here as the same kind.

## Ti'frais (fresh cheese range; Ti frais des Hauts)
- Animal(s): Cow (whole and skimmed milk from Réunion)
- Kind: Fresh (unripened)
- What it is: A fresh cheese range sold in several flavours: nature, Greek-style (à la grecque), chilli (piment), mixed peppers (aux poivres), ginger (gingembre), and garlic and fine herbs (ail / fines herbes). Sold in two-packs as well.
- How it is made: The ingredient lists give whole and skimmed milk, salt, milk protein and lactic cultures. No coagulant, cutting, draining or other steps are stated. Second pass: fr.wikipedia "Fromageries de Bourbon" says the fresh, soft and pressed cheeses are made from pasteurised cow's milk of the Hautes Plaines; no further steps are given there.
- Texture / ripening: Light, fresh, smooth, ivory-coloured (nature variant); no ripening.
- Where / who: La Réunion; Fromageries de Bourbon.
- Sources: https://fromagesdelareunion.re/produits/fromages-jeunes-sans-croute-formee/ — each flavour, milk, ingredients, storage at 2–6 °C; https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fromageries_de_Bourbon — "Fresh" list includes Ti frais des Hauts
