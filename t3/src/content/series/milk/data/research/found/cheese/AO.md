## Queijo picante (piri-piri spicy cheese; "queijo picante")
- Animal(s): goat (Serrana goat milk, 12 samples in the study)
- Kind: not stated
- What it is: a spicy cheese flavoured with piri-piri (chili), tested in a 2014 Portuguese-language master's study on Angolan dairy products.
- How it is made: the record says it followed the general procedures and norms for cheese; piri-piri added at 6 g and 15 g (plain version also made). No coagulant, heating, cutting, draining, salting or ripening steps given in the record.
- Texture / ripening: not stated; the plain version was preferred for smell and texture in the sensory tests.
- Where / who: linked by the study to omavele, a sour milk of the Nhaneca-Humbe (Nyaneka-Humbe) people of southwest Angola. Production context is a research replication, not a documented village tradition.
- Sources: https://agris.fao.org/search/en/providers/125158/records/67497b007625988a372237c9 — record of the 2014 master's thesis "Produção de derivados de leite: 'omavele' aromatizado e queijo picante" (Custódio, Mendonça, Galvão), describing the piri-piri cheese and its variants; full text (hdl 10198/11426) not reachable.
- Unsure: whether this is a traditional Angolan cheese or a product developed for the study; the cheese's steps are unknown; only one source.

## Queijo industrial (industrial cheese; Novagrolider, Aldeia Nova)
- Animal(s): cow (implied: the report describes the national milk supply as bovine; the species is not named for these two firms)
- Kind: industrial cheese; types not named in the report
- What it is: factory cheese made by local dairies. Novagrolider is described as specialised in cheese and derivatives; Aldeia Nova makes cheese among its butter and yoghurt.
- How it is made: not stated for these firms. The report says the few technified farms process their own milk in their own units into cheese, butter and yoghurt.
- Where / who: Novagrolider and Aldeia Nova. The report does not give Novagrolider's or Aldeia Nova's location; the 2016 Novagrolider article snippet names Cuanza Sul, which is unconfirmed.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "a Novagrolider é especializada na produção de queijos e derivados, operando com produção própria de leite"; "a Aldeia Nova ... produzindo manteiga, iogurtes e queijos a partir de leite próprio e de terceiros"; "Existem poucas fazendas no país que se dedicam à produção tecnificada de leite ... destinadas à fabricação de queijos, manteiga e iogurte"
- Unsure: cheese types and output are not stated. The 2016 Novagrolider yoghurt claim is not confirmed by the report.

## Queijo fatiado importado (sliced cheese, imported)
- Animal(s): not stated
- Kind: sliced cheese (queijo fatiado)
- What it is: sliced cheese, listed in the report as one of the most consumed dairy products in Angola. Cheese is also among the leading imports by quantity, after milk powder and fluid milk.
- How it is made: not stated. The report treats it as an import line; it does not say where each cheese is made.
- Where / who: imported; Portugal is the main dairy supplier overall (30.7% of dairy import value 2019–2025). Demand is rising in Luanda.
- Sources: https://www.gov.br/agricultura/pt-br/assuntos/relacoes-internacionais/adidos-agricolas/angola/ANGOLAMERCADODEPRODUTOSLCTEOSEMANGOLAOPORTUNIDADESPARAOSPRODUTOSBRASILEIROS.pdf — "Os produtos mais consumidos em Angola são o leite em pó, leite fluido, bebidas lácteas e os queijos fatiados"; "Em relação às quantidades, o leite em pó também aparece como principal produto, seguido do leite fluido e dos queijos"; "crescente procura por: queijos" in Luanda
- Unsure: the report does not split sliced cheese from other cheese imports.
