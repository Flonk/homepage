## Kefalonian white-brine cheese (Greek name not given in source; feta-type)
- Animal(s): sheep; goat up to 30% of milk (the five sampled farm cheeses were 70–80% sheep)
- Kind: brined; acid-curd white cheese of the feta family
- What it is: traditional artisanal white-brine cheese of Kefalonia, described in the paper as having a history back to Homeric times and as a local trademark.
- How it is made: sheep and goat milk, coagulated with animal rennet (abomasum) only, no commercial starter or enzymes; acid curd up to about 60% moisture; ripening up to about 1 month, then the curds are kept refrigerated in brine. Lacks a standardised process. Temperatures not given.
- Texture / ripening: as above; sampled farm cheeses had been in brine about 2.5–5.5 months at the time of analysis.
- Where / who: Kefalonia; five village dairy producers (Dilinata, Potamianata, Leivathos, Damoulianata, Poulata). Not stated to hold PDO/PGI status.
- Sources: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12532650/fullTextXML — full text of Lappa et al. 2025 (Probiotics and Antimicrobial Proteins, doi 10.1007/s12602-024-10239-x): milk split, rennet, brine, ripening, Kefalonian villages.
- Unsure: the paper is a microbiology study; "Kefalonian feta" is its own term, not an official name. Not the PDO Feta.

## Zakynthian myzithra (Μυζήθρα Ζακύνθου)
- Animal(s): not stated for Zakynthos (generic myzithra is made from ewe's or goat's milk, per the en.wikipedia Mizithra article)
- Kind: whey cheese
- What it is: a white cheese made on Zakynthos, named in the el.wikipedia article on the Zakynthos islets Μικρή and Μεγάλη Μυζήθρα.
- How it is made: not described for Zakynthos. The generic Mizithra method (en.wikipedia) is: milk brought to a slow boil, curdled with rennet or the whey of a previous batch, curds drained in a cheesecloth bag. Not confirmed as the Zakynthian method.
- Texture / ripening: not stated.
- Where / who: Zakynthos; producer not named.
- Sources: https://el.wikipedia.org/wiki/Μικρή_Μυζήθρα_Ζακύνθου — "λευκό τυρί που παρασκευάζεται στη Ζάκυνθο" (white cheese made on Zakynthos); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mizithra — generic method and milk.
- Unsure: only a passing mention in an islet article; no Zakynthos-specific method found.
