## Leche fresca (fresh cow's milk)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: fresh/raw or pasteurised milk
- What it is: Ordinary fresh milk sold in Puerto Rico; the industry article names it among the products the island's farms supply.
- How it is made: Not described in the source.
- Where / who: Industrial and retail; supplied by local dairy farms.
- Sources: https://newsismybusiness.com/dairy-farmers-celebrate-fresh-milk-month — names fresh milk among local dairy products and says 255 farms produce the milk consumers need.
- Unsure: Whether the fresh milk is raw or pasteurised is not stated.

## Coquito
- Animal(s): cow (condensed and evaporated milk); coconut (coconut milk and cream)
- Kind: alcoholic milk drink (rum-based)
- What it is: A creamy, spiced Christmas drink made with coconut and canned milks and rum, which the source says originated in Puerto Rico.
- How it is made: Stovetop method: gently cook coconut milk and cream, condensed and evaporated milk, egg yolk in some versions, and spices, then add rum after it cools; or blend everything without eggs. Bottle with cinnamon sticks and chill for a few hours; flavour peaks after two weeks or more.
- Where / who: Home and holiday drink, Puerto Rico and the wider Caribbean.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coquito — ingredients, method, Puerto Rico origin as a Christmas drink, variants (including pitorro rum).
- Unsure: Dairy content is condensed and evaporated milk only; many versions are dairy-free.

## Tres leches (Puerto Rican version)
- Animal(s): cow
- Kind: milk-soaked sponge cake (dessert)
- What it is: A sponge cake soaked in a mix of canned and fresh milk, topped with whipped cream. One Puerto Rican recipe site adds coconut milk and calls the mix "a fifth type of leche."
- How it is made: Sponge cake baked, then soaked in the milk mixture; the source gives no baking times or temperatures.
- Where / who: Home and bakeries; the Puerto Rican variant is named on a local recipe site.
- Sources: https://familiakitchen.com/?p=43014 — milk mix including coconut milk, whipped cream topping, recipe page without steps; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tres_leches_cake — milk mix, method, Latin American origin.
- Unsure: Wikipedia does not describe a Puerto Rican origin, and gives it as a Latin American cake. Whether PR has its own claim is not confirmed.
