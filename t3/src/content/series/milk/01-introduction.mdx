  ## Milk

  Milk! It's one of the most versatile animal products in the world, perhaps second only to meat.

  Since milk is used by animals to nourish their young, it is packed with essential nutrients that are beneficial for human consumption as well; you could probably survive off milk alone for a significant period of time.

  Now, keeping 270 million cows constantly pregnant so they give milk is at best ethically questionable, but let's not talk politics today.
</DeckCard>
